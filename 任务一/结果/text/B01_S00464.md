# AI Tutor for College Students Using Their Class Material

- 发布时间：2023-08-03

## 正文
Hi, My name is Hamsa Omar and I'm building an AI tutor similar to ChatGPT but for students(currently university students) based on their course material. This way they can get answers that are applicable to their course. The data is pulled from whatever Learning Management System their University provides, currently focusing on Canvas LMS which is the most popular among Universities. I've been able to put together a pretty shitty MVP here with limited data but that was enough for a professor to agree to try and incorporate it into his class this fall semester. I'm relatively technical so I've been able to build the v1 above and am working on a more polished v2 with contractors. I'm looking for technical expertise to come in as a co-founder to help build the product and iterate with feedback. Let me know if your interested.
