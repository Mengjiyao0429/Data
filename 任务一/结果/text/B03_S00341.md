# AI apps for ideation, research, and validation

## 正文
7 AI apps for ideation, research, and validation. 1. Notion: Notion is an all-in-one workspace where you can write, plan, collaborate and get organized - it allows you to take notes, add tasks, manage projects & more. Imagine a lego structure. Notion provides the building blocks and you can create your own layouts and toolkit to get work done. 2. Browse AI: Browse.AI lets you train a robot to scrape or monitor any website in 2 mins: No-code 1-click automations Adapts to website changes Works on almost any website Simulates user actions Integrates w. Google Sheet & Zapier API & webhooks. 3. Build: Build is a 4-week online community + cohort that enables & empowers anyone irrespective of their background to launch a side hustle in 4 weeks! Learn to ideate, build & launch to set yourself on the path of financial independence. 4. Gimme Summary AI: Free chrome extension to summarize articles on the web using ChatGPT AI. 100% free and works on all sites! 5. SaaS Library — Empower Your Journey: Unlock the full potential of your SaaS ideas with SaaSLibrary's detailed product descriptions, all of which can be easily built with AI tools. Our platform offers a wealth of information on cutting-edge product ideas, including personas, marketing strategies, features, and revenue models. 6. Komo: Komo Search is a generative search engine that you could chat, explore and search. - Chat: Deep dive, brainstorm or discuss topics with up-to-date info and links to sources. - Explore: See what the community is talking about. - Search: Get quick answers or links to resources. 7. Artifact:A personalized news feed driven by advanced artificial intelligence which is currently in private beta, developed by the founders of Instagram, it's kind of like TikTok for text.
