# Tired of chasing clients for late payments

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45537969
- 发布时间：2025-10-10
- 热度：点赞1、评论5

## 正文
Each month there is at least one client who does not pay my invoice. I absolutely despise sending follow up reminders it feels needy, awkward, and just a reframing of mental energy. So, I am thinking...what if an AI tool could send those reminders for me? Connect to Stripe or PayPal, it predicts who will likely pay late and automatically sends out a reminder email that gets firmer each time. IM NOT selling anything. Just qualifying if this is a pain point. Would you actually be willing to pay $49/mo for it to manage this whole mess for you? Or is this just a nice idea? And it's not worth cash? Only be brutally honest please :)
