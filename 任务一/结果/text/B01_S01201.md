## 正文
12-phase Flutter app with Supabase backend for detecting, reporting, and tracking scam messages/numbers in Sri Lanka. Uses Riverpod for state management, flutter_map for the scam map, rule-based scoring (with documented AI upgrade path), OCR screenshot scanning, push notifications, and dark mode.
