# Ask HN: What suite of management tools runs your small business' finances?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=13464647
- 发布时间：2017-01-23
- 热度：点赞6、评论8

## 正文
Those of us who run small businesses, what combination of affordable tools handle your finances -- accounting, invoicing, reconciliation, and other related functions? How did you move from checking bank-statements against a spreadsheet of invoices every month, to that sweet solution you're using? What software stack integrates best, and gives you performance, reliability, and confidence in the company? QuickBooks + iZettle + Zoho? ClearBooks + Stripe + Salesflare? Or have you gone all-in with a single provider for everything? If your business is based on per-appointment fees (e.g. lawyer, courier, hairdresser) how do you reconcile appointments with fees/invoices, and coordinate appointments with client details from your CRM system? Keen to hear from very small UK-based businesses under 10 employees in particular, but all experiences will be interesting to read.
