## 正文
Hi, I’m Armin, the CTO and founder of [cerebral.af]( ). Our mission is to enable software engineers to spend more time solving hard problems and less time performing mundane codebase maintenance tasks. That’s why we are building tools that automate mundane software engineering tasks in large codebases. Think, turning a story describing a major dependency upgrade with breaking changes into a submitted PR. Our product is an on-prem K8s service that interacts with client’s Git repo. It consists of a mix of ML and old-school algorithmic components for analyzing build logs, navigating large (100K+ files) codebases, and editing source code. We are looking for a Senior Python programmer to join as a founding engineer. By “Senior”, we mean someone with an exceptional track record of projects and shipped products. You’ll have technical freedom, but here are some projects you might choose to work on: 1. Package our tool for on-prem K8s deployment. 2. Design and build the front-end interface for Cerebral. 3. Create LLM-agents to automatically identify and edit problematic files in monorepos with 100K+ source files. 4. Fine-tune LLMs on Java code. 5. Build and lead a world-class team of software engineers. Nice-to-haves (we expect a successful candidate to have 1-2 of these): 1. Familiarity with NLP/ML. Has used PyTorch and/or HuggingFace. 2. Experience building devtools. 3. Front-end JS programming experience. If this resonates with you, please shoot me an email to armin [at] cerebral.af w/ "cerebral HN" as the subject and a line describing what aspect of cerebral excites you. Include your CV or a url to your LinkedIn/Github. Bonus points if you mention your favourite paper/blogpost.
