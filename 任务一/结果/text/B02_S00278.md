## 正文
Hey I'm Toad, I’ve been working on a tool called Boost Toad – it helps you go from a vague startup idea to a structured MVP feature roadmap in minutes. You just type in your idea with a description (e.g., "an AI startup assistant that generates you lean canvas business plan and then will guide you through planning features with suggestions and map out the quickest and most valuable way to build an MVP for the underlying problem"), and it generates: A core feature list - for you to choose from Suggested enhancements A lightweight MVP plan you can actually build, that will actually be valuable I built it because I've always been fascinated with business, I've read all the startup books, multiple times, and now I want to put that all into a reuasble framework for every project. This is just the beginning, the whole idea is to add every step of the way to keep you on track, from validating early, pivoting / stopping if the validation isn't there, how to market your idea etc. Pretty much I want it to be a robust assistant on helping you do the right things at the right time to ensure your product ends up profitable or you don't waste time on a dead goose.
