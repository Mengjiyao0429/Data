## 正文
Strip the personal data out of a document before you paste it into an AI. Converts PDF, Office, scans and email to Markdown and removes names, addresses, phone numbers, IBANs, cards and API keys. Validated, not just matched: an IBAN passes mod-97, a card passes Luhn, a protocol number stays a protocol number. Offline, deterministic, no model.
