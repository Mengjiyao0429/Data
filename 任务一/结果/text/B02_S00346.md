## 正文
Hey HackerNews! It’s just some market research and I thought it would be great to have some input from people like you. I'm exploring the concept of an AI-powered tool that would track the responses of the audience to your brand/content on X (Twitter), summon instant sentiment analysis of talks that involve your brand and provide specific recommendations regarding further action depending on audience feedback. Key question: Does the communication problem apply to the managing of reactions on the social media platforms and what would you expect to pay for a tool like this? I'm particularly interested in hearing from social media managers and business owners who: - Maintain and develop accounts that are currently used, has to constantly monitor brand references and social media consulting/agencies too expensive. Bookmarking, not selling anything – I wonder if this would solve any real problem. All feedback welcome! Edit: So thank you to everyone for giving some time and thought into this.
