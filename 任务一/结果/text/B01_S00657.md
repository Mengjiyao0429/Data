Here are the Google I/O predictions for 2016. These are just predictions and, we'll have fun guessing. - Google Weave will be publicly available as part of the Google Cloud Platform to connect and control internet of things. - Google Project Brillo will be announced exiting beta stage for embedding an Android O/S into your embedded device. - Project Brillo will support range of embedded devices, Intel's IoT microcontroller family (ie: Intel Edison). - Google Project Tango will further enable development content creation of VR / 3D spaces. - Live 360 video content creation. - Google services for businesses and other productivity tools. - Self driving cars and drones demonstrate the future of automation. - More on Android Pay and NFC / Bluetooth enabled payments. - Additional AI, Machine Learning services for Google Cloud Platform with particular concentration on NLP and chat bot systems. - Android N, or new Android Platform. - More on Google Spaces. The goodies and swag! What do we expect Google I/O attendees will receive this year? Google Nexus phones enabled for Google Fi network and/or Project Tango dev kit. Well thats a wrap. We expect that users will have a lot of open space particularly to see self driving cars and drones in action, possibly with the aid of VR and VR headsets. Let's see how well the predictions goes.
