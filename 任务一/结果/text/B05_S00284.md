Problem: unclean data goes without any intervention for a long time. So it will take space & also no value is generated. Our solution: will act as a crawler/filter to store only what has value Definition of value: it is like what is statistically significant So what I am building is a SaaS that will do all the preparation before an analyst starts his work. Just like a chef gets his veggies chopped before he starts. So an analyst has to do 3 steps primarily 1. Gather data 2. Clean data 3. Do the exploratory or initial statistical analysis My SaaS helps in steps 2 & 3. Also it will generate an information score to show the value of the data. Also btw this is completely automated with no interaction needed. I have the following ones in mind 1. Preliminary analysis of your data 2. Auto clean & summaries of your data. 3. Give Pre analyzed and cleaned data 4. All is meant to do in real time Incase you know any competitors/ you find no value please do let me know.
