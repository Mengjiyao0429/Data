## 正文
MorphDrop is a modern, 100% offline Android document toolkit & converter built for privacy. Convert images, PDFs, spreadsheets, text & markdown with on-device batch OCR, PDF compression, watermarking, native editor & Glance widgets. Fast, private & beautifully animated. Drop. Transform. Done.
