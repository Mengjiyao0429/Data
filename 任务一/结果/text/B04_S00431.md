# Ask HN: Automated resource scheduling for project management

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=20838682
- 发布时间：2019-08-30
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I've been looking around at the PM resource scheduling solutions that exist, but they all want me to manually pick people and assign them tasks, and quite often resolve conflicts manually. Are there any tools that will take my project with its tasks and automatically generate a schedule across all resources taking into account conflicts, leave time, due dates, task skill requirement, etc.? I want to spend my time focusing on the progress of the project, clearing roadblocks for people, not shuffling around calendars.
