## 正文
ScribeCrate: open-source, self-hosted Whisper speech-to-text server powered by faster-whisper. OpenAI-compatible transcription and English translation APIs, speaker diarization, JSON/SRT/VTT output, SSE streaming, offline operation with cached models, and Docker deployment on CPU (amd64/arm64) or NVIDIA CUDA (amd64).
