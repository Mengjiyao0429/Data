# Ask HN: Any note-taking software that has AsciiDoc support?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33234907
- 发布时间：2022-10-17
- 热度：点赞3

## 正文
The market is filled with note-taking apps, knowledge bases, second brains, etc. in which you can write notes in Markdown. How about AsciiDoc? It's better in quite a few aspects, though not gaining much popularity. Anchors can be put pretty much anywhere, and linking to them seems more natural than "backlinks". Complete table support. Also has native admonitions.
