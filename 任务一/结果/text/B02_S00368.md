## 正文
Local AI text-to-speech with voice cloning and voice design, powered by GGML. C++17 port of Qwen3-TTS (QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS). 10 languages, 24 kHz mono output, runs on CPU, CUDA, ROCm, Metal, Vulkan.
