## 正文
1. Robomart is developing self-driving stores that can be summoned to customers' homes or businesses. The company is banking on the concept of "store-hailing," which is similar to ride-hailing services like Uber and Lyft. Customers can use the Robomart app to order items from a variety of stores, and the Robomart will then drive to the customer's location and deliver the items. 2. Netflix and Walmart are offering six-figure salaries for AI jobs, reflecting the growing demand for AI talent and the importance of AI technology. Netflix is offering up to $900,000 for a product manager position, while Walmart is offering up to $288,000 for a director of retail media AI. 3. Anthropic, an AI startup co-founded by former OpenAI leaders, raises $100M from SK Telecom. The funding will be used to develop Anthropic's large language model (LLM) technology, which can be used for a variety of applications, such as generating text, translating languages, and writing different kinds of creative content. 4. Microsoft Azure ChatGPT is a private and secure version of ChatGPT that is hosted on Microsoft Azure. It offers the same features as the public version of ChatGPT, but it is hosted on Microsoft Azure, which provides a secure and reliable environment for running AI workloads. Azure ChatGPT is also more tightly integrated with Microsoft Azure services, making it easier for enterprises to integrate it into their existing applications and workflows. 5. A recent study by BlackBerry found that 75% of businesses are implementing or considering bans on ChatGPT, a generative AI tool that can be used to create realistic and engaging text. The study, which polled 2,000 IT decision-makers in Australia, Japan, France, Germany, Canada, the Netherlands, the US, and the UK, found that businesses are concerned about the risks associated with ChatGPT, including data security, privacy, and brand reputation. 6. Amazon is using AI to summarize product reviews. The AI will create short paragraphs summarizing key features and customer sentiment in product reviews. This will help customers get an overview of reviews without reading them all. AI technology is still under development, but Amazon says it can already generate accurate and informative summaries. 7. Snapchat engineer Kyle Goodrich has designed an AI camera that uses Stable Diffusion to turn photos into AI art. The camera, called DreamGenerator, has 30 different themes that users can choose from, such as retro, sci-fi, zombie, and pirate. The camera then uses Stable Diffusion to transform the photo into a work of art in a matter of seconds. Source -
