## 正文
I've been working on an app called ATyeS.co to help with the often overwhelming task of customizing resumes for each job application. With the job market being so competitive, and having a tailored resume for every position beings so crucial. What ATyeS.co Does: AI-Powered Resume Editor: The app uses AI to quickly adjust your resume to match specific job descriptions, aiming to help you stand out to when scanned by an ATS. User-Friendly: You can upload your current resume(this happens once) and paste the job description you're targeting. Our AI then analyzes and enhances your resume with relevant keywords and phrasing. Real-Time Preview and Edit: See changes in real time and make any additional edits you need. Multiple Formats: Download your enhanced resume in PDF or Word DOCX formats. Chrome Extension (Beta): We have a Chrome extension that can generate tailored resumes on the fly when applying on major job sites like LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and ZipRecruiter. This feature is still in beta and available to a limited number of users. Looking for Feedback: I'm considering fine-tuning our model to work better with resumes. This could make resume creation process even more faster, currently resumes are generated in 20 secs. I want to get it down to 5 secs. I would love for you to try out ATyeS.co and share your thoughts. Your feedback will be incredibly helpful as we continue to improve the app. Thanks for checking it out!
