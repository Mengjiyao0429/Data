## 正文
Darvin is a Python-based voice-activated chatbot that interacts with users and speaker. It can perform web searches, play music, tell the time and date, fetch weather information, and provide Wikipedia summaries on command. Utilizing various libraries such as speech_recognition, pyttsx3, requests, pygame, and wikipediaapi.
