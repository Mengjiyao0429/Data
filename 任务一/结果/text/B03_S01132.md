## 正文
Windows file converter and toolbox in your Explorer menu. Press Shift on files to convert images, video, audio, documents and PDFs. Also: copy text or QR links from the screen (OCR), paste an image as a file, phone transfer by QR, recipes and undo. A Tangerine alternative for Windows, free and open source.
