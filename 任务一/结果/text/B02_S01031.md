## 正文
Voice & Vision Assistant for the Blind is an AI-powered assistant that helps blind and low-vision users navigate the world more independently. It uses real-time vision, speech recognition, and natural language understanding to describe surroundings, identify people or objects, and answer spoken questions instantly.
