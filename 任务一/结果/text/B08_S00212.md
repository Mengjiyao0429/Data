# Ask HN: How would you feel about working for an AI boss?

## 正文
How would you feel about applying for a job where an AI interviews you, hires you, gives you your tasks and ongoing feedback, oversees your work by whatever metrics it wants (including directing other people to QA your work), writes your performance reviews (based on other people's feedback about your work, which it reads, and based on the metrics it has access to), and gives you bonuses and potentially fires you if you do not perform in line with its expectations? I started to wargame what this might look like, I imagined that already at the job application process people would start to troll it, but it saw through this in my example: (I wrote that it is an actual application but in fact I just made it up.) If it came with a real paycheck, how would you feel about applying for a job where an AI was your boss, would you do it, why or why not?
