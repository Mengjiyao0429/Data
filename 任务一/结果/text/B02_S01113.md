## 正文
WZ Reader (formerly WZ PDF) — presentation-focused document viewer for PDF, Korean HWP/HWPX, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, e-mail and images. Read aloud, narrated slide videos, OCR, annotate, save as PDF. Web and Windows desktop; nothing is uploaded.
