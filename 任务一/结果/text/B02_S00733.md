## 正文
JOB SUMMARY We’re looking for a passionate and ambitious QA’er to join the expanding QA team. The purpose of the role is to ensure that the Work Angel product works successfully across a broad range of smartphone, desktop and tablet devices. The everyday tasks will require close work within a team of testers to vigorously test applications to ensure stability and quality before they are released on the market. This role will act as a Tester in producing and maintaining test cases. The quality of testing of the product is paramount. Device testing is an integral part of the testing process and a key contributor to quality control. Experience Required Experience of working as a QA Tester in mobile Keen attention to detail Experience of running test plans and test cycles Have excellent communication skills (both verbally and written) to communicate defects of the product An excellent team player Excellent knowledge of bug tracking software and maintenance Strong documentation skills Strong automation testing experience in ios / iphone, android, ipad are the preferred platforms. Experience in setting up an automated test framework from scratch. Test automation experience on mobile applications Responsibilities To operate, maintain and develop QA procedures, tools and automated test capabilities Develop new tests, or modify existing tests, appropriately in response to defect reports Run tests manually on mobile devices in accordance with a test specification and report results in a clear and concise way Provide clear and concise bug reports bug database Pro-actively pursue resolution of defects in collaboration with development of engineers Work closely with the other testers to learn the testing framework Participate in internal QA meetings Integrate new mobile devices into automated test systems. Contribute continuously to the documentation of the QA team knowledge base
