## 正文
Hey HN, After months of solo building, trial and error, and countless chats with dog parents & vets, I just launched the 1st MVP of PawBud Scan - an AI-powered health scan tool for dogs. [ ) It’s simple: • Fill out a quick health profile about your dog • Get an instant AI-generated health risk score + care tips • Optionally, unlock a vet style report for $10 The idea was born when my own 8 year old Labrador Retriever was diagnosed too late with a preventable joint condition. It hit me how little proactive care exists for pets, especially for early stage common symptoms. So I built this MVP with: • GPT-4o for AI triage • Stripe for instant paywall testing • Replit + Flask + some good old late nights This is still very early, it’s not perfect, but I’d really appreciate your feedback: • Is the experience smooth enough? • Does the pricing feel reasonable? • Would you trust this enough to share with a fellow pet parent? • What other features would you like to see? Any feedback (brutal or kind) would go a long way. If this works, I plan to expand into full vet integrations, breed-specific care loops, and a smarter preventive health platform. Thanks for reading and thank you to this community for inspiring me to build. With regards, Anurag (Founder & solo builder)
