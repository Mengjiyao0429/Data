## 正文
Text2GraphRAG Disease Assistant builds a disease-focused retrieval-augmented generation workflow. It ingests structured Markdown (demo: ophthalmology), extracts entities and relations into Neo4j, builds Milvus vector indexes for semantic recall, and answers questions hybrid router that blends graph reasoning with semantic search using.
