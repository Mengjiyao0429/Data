# No Calls

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42730478
- 发布时间：2025-01-16

## 正文
I'm a freelancer and sometimes I have to recommend software or services for my clients. When I evaluate choices I automatically remove all of those that don't have pricing up front as I have no time nor intention to do this. I don't think any company lost millions on me, but many lost tens of thousands. API providers are the worst, but I kinda understand them.
