## 正文
ChatGPT at least has largely stopped relying much on factual knowledge in its training data. Nowadays it will likely assume that it’s out of date and do a web search, so it will be biased by whatever product listings and reviews it can find. That is, you should consider it a smart web search tool. The information is only as good as whatever it can find on the Internet.
