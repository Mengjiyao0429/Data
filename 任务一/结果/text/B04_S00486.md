## 正文
I need to place objects in a map. They are a lot but distributed regularly (zig-zag). I need an "auto-clicker" that can repeatedly: click move mouse of +5 pixels on y axis click move mouse of +3 pixels on x axis and -5 on y axis click repeat for n times It should be Open Source, or free with a "clean" installer. Most auto-clickers I've seen use absolute screen positions, I don't need that. If you can suggest a more complex program for macros, that's fine (as long as the above requirements are satisfied). I tried Auto Mouse Clicker, Move Mouse, MouseBot, AutoMouser and looked at the screenshots of some others. Thanks.
