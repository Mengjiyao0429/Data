## 正文
Hello! As a developer I have a problem with context switching at work because I need to use different apps at the same time to get the job done. For example, the majority time of a typical day at work is for using IDE to write code, but sometimes I need to switch to an other app/service like software management tool (Jira, Asana, TargetProcess...) or work messaging app (Slack, Facebook Workplace, MS Teams...) or version control service (Github, Gitlab,...) and sometimes checking my social media as well. Altering between these different tasks might cost time and our focus - which leads to reduce productivity. My initial research from friends and colleagues shows that I am not the only one having this issue at work. Are you having the same issue? To solve this problem, I am developing a AI virtual assistant app specializes for working environment called JoSunday. The app allows users to: Automate the daily tasks Assign the automated task with a speech command Summon the command at will by your voice command I am going to release the beta version of the app in the next couple of weeks and I am looking for users to join this beta program.
