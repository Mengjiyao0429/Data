# Ask HN: What are you building with the current wave of AI and LLMs?

- 发布时间：2023-03-18

## 正文
With the recent advancements in AI and large language models (LLMs) like GPT-4, I'm curious to learn what kind of projects you all have been working on utilizing these powerful tools. It's clear that we're in the midst of a transformative period for AI, and the possibilities for its applications seem endless. Personally, I've been experimenting with GPT-4 to create a platform that generates content for niche blogs, which has shown promising results so far. The ability of LLMs to understand context and generate relevant, high-quality content has made the process of content creation far more efficient. Here are a few areas I've seen people leveraging AI and LLMs: 1. Personalized learning platforms: Utilizing AI to create tailored educational content for students based on their strengths and weaknesses. 2. Sentiment analysis: Companies analyzing customer reviews and social media posts to better understand their audience and improve their products and services. 3. Content summarization: Summarizing long articles and documents into concise summaries for easier consumption. 4. Creative writing and storytelling: Assisting authors and screenwriters in generating ideas, characters, and plots for their stories. 5. Medical diagnosis: Enhancing the diagnostic process by analyzing vast amounts of medical data and generating relevant insights for doctors. These are just a few examples, and I'm sure there are many more interesting projects out there. So, please share your experiences and ideas! * What projects are you currently working on or have completed that incorporate AI and LLMs? * What challenges have you faced while working with these technologies? * How do you think the landscape of AI and LLMs will evolve in the next few years? Looking forward to hearing your thoughts and learning from your experiences!
