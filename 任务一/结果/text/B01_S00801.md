## 正文
PersonAi is a local-first desktop app that lets you create and chat with AI-powered characters. Built with Tauri, React, Rust, Go, and Python, privacy-friendly experience with persistent local history, character customization, and full offline support. Perfect for storytelling, roleplay, or prototyping local LLM assistants.
