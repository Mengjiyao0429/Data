## 正文
In three parts: (contd in comments)1/3 Specific to California. Problem: 1. Teachers and teachers unions disrupt education because they feel teachers are underpaid. 2. They are also recipients of lifetime pension under CalStrs. 3. Any incremental increase means an incremental increase in pensions when they are retired and not working. 4. Education is funded with property taxes in California. This is one of the reasons govt wants more housing and especially expensive housing. 4. We have Prop 13 that is a low tax and based on sale value and not perceived assessment. This was to protect retirees as real estate became more and more speculative in CA. 5. The domino effect of this has been a need to increase incoming property tax for the burgeoning public education expenses. CA is lagging in education. 6. We are also suffering from unfunded pension liabilities as the retired teachers draw 60-80% of their last pay stub. This was an ill conceived plan during Gov.Gray’s time when they thought Wall Street will keep blooms on forever on the money tree. 7. Result: unions threaten strikes and walk outs by teachers. Teachers are discouraged and feel they can’t afford to live and work in an area where they cant compete with Silicon Valley neighbours and their million dollar homes and life style.
