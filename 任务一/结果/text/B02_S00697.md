# Ask HN: I created a “SaaS platform”, but I have no idea what to do with it

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12542444
- 发布时间：2016-09-20
- 热度：点赞2、评论1

## 正文
For over a year I have been developing a web application (SaaS) for very specific line of business, together with a guy who works (and runs a company) in that business. I have been doing all the software development, and he was responsible for the domain knowledge. The idea was to let his company be the "pilot" for the application, and then when we have enough of a system we would sell it to other customers. He had a lot of connections as well, so there was a good chance to get several customers early on. However, things didn't work out. I halted the development. Soon after, he contacted another developer to solve the problems. The developer built a quick solution very specific to his company, so there were no longer a need for my system. I'm no longer interested in continuing in working towards that line of business, since the only real connection I had to it was through the guy I mentioned before. And to be honest... the business specific functionality of my application wasn't really that advanced. However, I still have my web application, a multi-tenancy SaaS web application, responsive and developed with the latest technology. I know the codebase very well, and it is 100% my own (no legal issues). So I was thinking taking on a completely new idea, and work towards a line of business. What do I do with the code base? What would you have done? How do I find another line of business that I can customize my application towards? Any ideas? tl;dr: I have developed a base for a SaaS / web app, but I have no idea what to do with it.
