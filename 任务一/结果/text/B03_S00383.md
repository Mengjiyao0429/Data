The Multi-Agent Reasoning framework creates an interactive chatbot where AI agents collaborate reasoning and Swarm Integration for optimal answers. Simulating a team that discusses, debates, and refines responses, it enables complex problem-solving and precise results. Now with Prompt Caching to reduce latency and costs.
