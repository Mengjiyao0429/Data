## 正文
Hi HN, I’m working on a project called LabhTark, a stock analysis platform built specifically for Indian retail traders. It aims to simplify both technical and fundamental analysis, and uses machine learning to forecast stock trends. My goal is to help people move away from gambling and toward data-driven investing—especially those new to the stock market who feel overwhelmed by tools like TradingView My site is labhtark.com (not linked because of HN spam filter) Some features I’ve built so far: * Auto-analysis of stocks with a recommendation score (Strong Buy to Strong Sell) * Easy-to-use stock screener like Finviz, focused on India * AI-based short-term prediction model (7–15 days forecast) * Beginner-friendly UI with explainers. I’d love to hear your thoughts: * What’s missing or unclear? * Would this solve any pain points for you or people you know? * What kind of features or data would make you trust it more? Thanks in advance!
