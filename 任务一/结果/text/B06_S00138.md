# Ask HN: Would an AI chatbot that searches guides/wikis in-game be useful?

- 发布时间：2025-08-22
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
would an in-game AI chatbot that (1) answers quick guide questions (best gear, boss tactics) and (2) opens/pins wiki pages on top of the game help you look up info while playing? I feel like it could save time compared to watching multiple YouTube videos, but I haven't seen a tool that combines both functions widely used by players yet. when I was playing Monster Hunter I asked ChatGPT for a gear recommendation — some of its suggestions differed from what I saw in videos. That made me wonder how much people would trust an AI versus curated wiki/video sources. A few things I'm curious about: • Would you trust an AI's build/gear suggestions, or prefer direct wiki citations? (Would video citations help?) • Would you want an AI companion that cheers you the first time you take down a boss, or just chats while you play — or would that get annoying?
