## 正文
AI Manga Reader is a next-gen manga app powered by the MangaDex API, offering vast multi-language content and flexible reading modes. It uses AI-powered OCR to extract and translate text from manga pages, combined with AI-driven text-to-speech narration—letting you effortlessly read and listen to any chapter or page, anytime, anywhere.
