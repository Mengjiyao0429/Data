# 28% of job postings on company career sites have been open over 90 days

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49820529
- 发布时间：2026-09-23

## 正文
AI tools have devalued resumes and cover letters, personal repos, leet code scores, etc... It's always been the case, but now it's more true than ever: your professional network is super important. If somebody on the inside can recommend you, your chances of getting an offer are good compared to throwing your resume on the pile.
