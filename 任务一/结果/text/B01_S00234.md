## 正文
I started a free weekly newsletter called AI PAPA that curates 3–4 AI tools or tips to help dads save time, get creative, or just survive parenting with tech. It’s a mix of useful tools (e.g. bedtime story generators, productivity hacks) and dad humor. I’m trying to grow it without paid ads and would love feedback from the HN community: What would make this kind of newsletter stand out? Would you read/subscribe if you’re not a parent but into AI? Growth tips or strategies you’ve seen work for niche newsletters? Appreciate any ideas, feedback, or roasts
