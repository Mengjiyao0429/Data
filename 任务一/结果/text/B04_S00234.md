# Ask HN: Which AI dev tools are people *really* using?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42628413
- 发布时间：2025-01-07
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
Every day I'm hearing that devs will be obsolete any moment now. We're supposedly on the verge of AI (AGI, even!) spitting out entire apps or, at a minimum, devs are currently getting 60%+ productivity gains from AI-assist tools. But, what are people actually using in the wild? Is there really an AI-powered low/no-code tool for building apps (Web, mobile or otherwise)? If not, does anyone know of a tool that's even close? And, who here can report 60% or higher output using AI-assist? Which specific tool(s) are you using to achieve this?
