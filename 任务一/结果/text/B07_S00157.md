## 正文
Is there an LLM based solution for classifying emails either server-side or client side? I do not mean a tool for email server administrators. Instead I mean a tool for email recipients where they can conveniently specify rules in natural language such as "If someone addresses me with my first name or with a personal greeting "Dear X" or "Hi X", mark this mail blue in the list panel, and star it/flag it. "Mark company newsletters as grey and move them into the newsletters folder". Currently, in most email client software, there are commonly rule-based or regex based filtering rules. These are too difficult to understand for most users.
