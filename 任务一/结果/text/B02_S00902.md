## 正文
PCB-2.0-YOLOV8-STREAMLIT is app build to detect six types of defects in printed circuit boards (PCBs) using the YOLOv8 object detection model. These defects include: Mouse Bite , Spur , Spurious Copper ,Short ,Missing Hole ,Open Circuit
