## 正文
High-performance PDF conversion library for macOS desktop apps. Convert PDF to Word, Excel, PPT, HTML, CSV, images, text, RTF, searchable PDF, JSON, Markdown, and OFD with native Mac integration. Support OCR and AI table recognition for intelligent document processing.
