# Ask HN: How do you manage your startup ideas?

- 来源：Hacker News

- 发布时间：2013-05-07
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
I personally use Evernote and Notational Velocity. I'd love a system that would let me input free form text, and tag the notes with different projects or categories and then automatically organize them - currently what I do is either scroll down my notes and enter the new note at the right place, or just include a keyword so that I can search my notes. Neither are ideal - I'm sure there's something that does this already though.
