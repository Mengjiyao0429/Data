## 正文
This would of course make use of the user's history of such adjustments in various contexts. For example, this could automatically reduce brightness when viewing a web page or app that does not respect the user's dark mode setting. As for sound, this could, for example, make speech be at the same volume regardless of whether it comes from a video, podcast, advertisement, speech synthesizer, etc.
