## 正文
QwenCleo-ASR is the currently State-Of-The-Art (SOTA) open-source model for Egyptian Arabic & code-switching speech recognition Built on Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, fine-tuned for Egyptian dialect and Arabic ↔ English code-switching.
