## 正文
Everybody is saying that you should start collecting e-mails of people who are reading your blog posts/visiting your page/using your app. But what to do next if you collected them? It’s a lot of hassle to send a newsletter each week to them or to write a blog post each week and send to them in a newsletter. Therefore I decided to automate this process and nurture my subscribers on auto with content they would love. #How does it work? Three easy steps to automate your newsletters 1. Choose website/blogs that your subscribers would love to read. 2. Set an AI filter based on keywords/categories related to your customer group 3. Set how often your subscribers should get a newsletter from you (each week, day, month) with a number of blog posts to put into the newsletter (1, 2, or maybe 10 posts) 4. Click send and put your newsletters on auto. #Example I run a blog for JavaScript developers. They are interested in javascript development and I know a few amazing blogs for them. 1. I add 5 amazing blogs to supply superb content for my subscribers 2. I set the keywords related to my developers to filter the best content ex. javascript, node.js, react, js etc. 3. I set an e-mail to be send each week on Monday with min. 3 to max. 5 posts in one e-mail. 4. Click Send and automate my newsletters for the next years. :) #Interested in having the same?
