## 正文
We're a startup at the MVP stage offering vector databases and our own in house + better version of langchain as a hosted API service to allow any developer to build AI into products. For our MVP, we make sure your vector DB is always in sync with your data source, and offer you endpoints to 1) extract data from, 2) summarize, 3) auto suggest text, 4) perform semantic search, and 5) generative QnA on your data. Of course, you could mix and match these functionalities to meet your use case. (eg, summarize content + using the summarization to semantically search for relevant items, extract data from text + auto suggesting text based on the data, qna based on data or documents) We focus on helping startups that couldn't otherwise figure out how to piece together and optimize AI tools on the market gain an unfair advantage over their competitors. We also help reduce costs by caching LLMS results. Would love some feedback, thanks for making to the end!
