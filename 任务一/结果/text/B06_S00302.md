# Does HN know any good tools to share stuff between Windows/iOS/OS X?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31856720
- 发布时间：2022-06-24
- 热度：点赞2、评论7

## 正文
I realize that Microsoft, Apple et al don't necessarily like to make their stuff super interoperable, but surely after this many decades there's something that works well. I used to use something called PushBullet which is now defunct. It was nice because it would actually push the data. Now I use AirDroid which is really clunky on Windows. Apple has AirDrop, but that doesn't help with my Windows (gaming/primary) computer. Why is there no easy LAN protocol or software for sharing stuff between all my machines like URLs, pictures, files that crosses the OS/Company boundary? Does anybody know of something that works well between iOS devices, Windows, and OS X that's better than AirDroid?
