## 正文
Suppose I have a phone with bad camera, I want a good quality image. I take a photo at each corner of a document. So I will have 4 photos. I want to concatenate these photos together to get one big photo that shows the entire document. This requires sophisticating computer vision techniques to combine 4 images that are taken at slightly different angle. The algorithm needs to find out the overlapped part between photos and merge them together. I'm wondering if there's an app on a phone that does this. Or at least a topic that I search more about. Please suggest me either an app or topic name.
