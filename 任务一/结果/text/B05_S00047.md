# Ask HN: Is now the time for an open-source, data-broker removal request bot?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47965354
- 发布时间：2026-04-30
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I tried out one of those data broker removal request services a while back, and it was meh, but not the cheapest thing. They are one of those services for me that's in the camp of: - Why would you not, and - Is this a scam The pattern(s) for facilitating removal requests seems really annoying in a world without AI, but this seems like a really good fit for AI. I wonder how many of these data-broker removal orgs will die as people learn you can automate this. Anyone know of something that already is an attempt to do this, that's open-source? Is there an appetite for this?
