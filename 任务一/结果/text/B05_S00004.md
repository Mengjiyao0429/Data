# Jaron Lanier on parallels between AI and social media

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50367820
- 发布时间：2026-11-04

## 正文
I really recommend the book “You are not a Gadget,”. It’s old but it doesn’t seem old because it was so prescient. People have moved on to worry about AI. So many of the dangers of AI come from the insecurities created by tech. Vulnerable software in IoT. Data scooped up everywhere and centralized. No privacy. Human interactions intermediated by machine. If we could just start to address some of those issues I would have more faith that we will ever really do anything to regulate AI.
