If you compare it to the Antrophic MCP apps directory review process both Apple and Google are a sweet dream. I apply for the Antrophic MCP directroy since March (sic!) and due to constant changes in their process I had to resubmit 5 times now!! We have November, 8 months. Sometimes you get ghosted, then approved, but you can't publish because they approved an older version that isn't compatible to _their_ updated publishing process anymore and they only way to make it work is to resubmit waiting again _months_ for an approval, although it already was approved. It is a nightmare! Folks, Antrophic is the living proof that you can make an app approval and publishing process even worse then Google Play or the Apple App store! Yes! :-D I am at a point where I do not care anymore about the Claude MCP Apps directory, although our users demand it. The ChatGPT app is running great by the way and got approved and published in April.
