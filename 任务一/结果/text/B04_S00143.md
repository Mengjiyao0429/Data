## 正文
We believe the next 5–10 years will completely transform how products are discovered. Instead of search engines, agentic AI tools will explore, evaluate, and recommend products directly to the right customers, fundamentally changing marketing, SEO, and user acquisition. We’re building in this space, with a focus on geo-contextual discovery (where location, intent, and AI reasoning meet). We’re looking to partner with 5 forward-thinking agencies or B2C startups for an early pilot. You’ll get early access, influence the roadmap, and help your marketing team get ahead of the coming wave, while we learn from your use cases to shape the product. If you’re interested in experimenting at the frontier of AI-driven marketing, reach out, and we’d love to chat.
