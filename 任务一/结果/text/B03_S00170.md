## 正文
We’re testing a new product focused on cold email outreach automation — and we’re looking for early feedback. Here’s what it does: • Connect your email and send cold email sequences • Control send limits per day, set follow-up rules • Track opens, clicks, and replies automatically But here’s the twist: This isn’t just another cold email tool. We’re building AI-powered personalization at scale. Instead of just using simple merge tags like {{firstName}}, our system analyzes each recipient’s industry, job title, and company to tailor the tone and content of each email — aiming for real “1:1 feeling” at scale. Right now, we’re testing whether this kind of AI-driven personalization can actually increase response and conversion rates. If you’ve ever sent cold emails, we’d love your thoughts: • What’s the biggest pain point you want a tool like this to solve? • How important is personalization to you — and what does it really mean to you? • What’s one feature you think cold outreach tools are missing? Feel free to drop your comments, share your ideas, or DM if you’d like early access. Let’s build something better — together.
