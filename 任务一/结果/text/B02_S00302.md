## 正文
Antoine Borrelly (PM with a background in software engineering) and Aude Flecchia (ex-Bain) are building Gleef, a next-generation localization tool that helps companies scale globally with AI-driven adaptation and seamless workflows. We started Gleef after seeing firsthand how frustrating and inefficient localization can be, especially in fast-moving teams. With early traction, funding, and a growing user base, we’re now looking for a *technical co-founder (Founding Engineer)* to join us at this critical stage. We need someone with extensive engineering experience (backend with good architectural knowledge and experience building devtools) who thrives in startup environments and is excited about building something from the ground up. This role comes with significant ownership and the opportunity to shape the product and tech stack. We’re looking for a true partner — someone who’s both an exceptional builder and a strategic thinker, ready to help us scale. If this sounds like you, let’s talk!
