## 正文
If you have worked with open-weight/local LLMs, you've known both the highs and the lows. Lows there are many: malformed response tags, empty responses, stuck loops, overthinking, inference engine errors and much more. You don't notice those when using commercial models, because the vendors iron out those quirks in their gateways, but you're left standing when using free models. Gauze is a transparent openAI compatible gateway which monitors and corrects bad LLM responses.
