## 正文
SemantiClip is an intelligent video processing application that transforms video content into rich, structured written formats. Built with Microsoft Agent Framework, SemantiClip automates the transcription, segmentation, and content generation process - demonstrating agents orchestration with the use of SLM & LLM.
