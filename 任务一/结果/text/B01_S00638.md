## 正文
Optimized ESP32-S3/P4 fully offline speech to text and text to speech system for Micropython and AtomVM, featuring a finetuned STT model for noisy environments, plus a data generation/field capture/model finetuning framework. Includes high performance 4/8bit int kernels for Xtensa/RiscV PIE vector units
