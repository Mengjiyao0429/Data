## 正文
There are several dating apps and AI relationship tools but I never saw one which would combine them. The closest I saw was a dating portal where a lengthy questionnaire had to be filled in order to get the best matches. But I wonder if there is a portal or app which lets a men and a woman have an AI relationship (to build an interaction pattern, known in forehand) and after some time the dating component informs them that there is a potential match (in case that there is an interaction pattern match between both AI/real person pairs).
