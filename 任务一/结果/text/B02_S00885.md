## 正文
Private voice dictation for Windows, macOS and Linux. Hold a shortcut, talk, and Lipwise types what you meant into any app: speech recognition runs on your computer, and an AI editor understands "scratch that", lists and rewrites. Works with local AI, Claude or OpenAI.
