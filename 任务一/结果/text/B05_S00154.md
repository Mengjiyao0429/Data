## 正文
Hi HN, I’m a young aspiring developer from Bangladesh, currently building an offline-first, multilingual journaling assistant app called Lokkhi Diary — designed for users in regions with limited internet access. The goal: To help millions of dreamers like me—who may not always have access to high-end devices or stable internet—use AI-powered journaling and learning tools that actually work offline. Key features: Timestamped journaling with version history Voice-to-text support (Bangla & English) Offline Bangla-English translation Smart reminders, search, and structure A special mode that works like a “personal ChatGPT inside your diary” I'm building this not just for myself, but for the entire community of learners, writers, and dreamers in low-connectivity areas. I want to create something that feels like magic, but is built with care, empathy, and purpose.
