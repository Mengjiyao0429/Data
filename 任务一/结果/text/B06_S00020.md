# Ask HN: Are you working less? By how much?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49900343
- 发布时间：2026-09-29
- 热度：点赞2、评论3

## 正文
The promise of AI is to significantly speedup and automate knowledge work. AI assistants are promising to handle personal productivity for us as well (planning, task management, research, reports, etc). Do you feel that your day is becoming less busy? that you have more free focus than ever? that you can sit bored because all chores are done for you by AI? In which direction are we going? I would appreciate your self-reflection and perspective on this.
