# Cloud Image Recognition End-of-Life

- 来源：GitHub
- 链接：https://github.com/PTCInc/vuforia-engine/issues/222
- 发布时间：2026-11-04

## 正文
As part of the ongoing evolution of the Vuforia product portfolio, PTC is retiring the Vuforia Engine Cloud Image Recognition service effective May 2, 2027. During the remaining support period, PTC will continue to provide access to the Cloud Image Recognition Service and associated image target databases. To prepare for this transition, we recommend evaluating alternative image recognition solutions that best meet your application needs. Here is what to expect: - Existing customers may continue to use their current Cloud Image Recognition services until the End-of-Life date, May 2, 2027. - The service will remain fully operational throughout the notice period. - After the End-of-Life date (May 2, 2027), Cloud Image Recognition databases and associated services will no longer be available. If you have questions about how this change affects your deployment, please contact PTC Support or your PTC representative. Thank you for your continued use of Vuforia, and we regret any inconvenience that this product retirement may cause. For more information, please see the [FAQ]( ).
