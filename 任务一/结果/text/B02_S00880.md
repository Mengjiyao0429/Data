## 正文
Movie recommendation system with Python. Implements content-based filtering (TF-IDF + cosine similarity), collaborative filtering with matrix factorization (TruncatedSVD), and a hybrid approach. Evaluates with Precision , Recall , and NDCG. Includes rating distribution plots, top movies, and sample recommendations.
