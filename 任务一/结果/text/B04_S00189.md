## 正文
As shown in the following code, I want to create a branch in RunnableLambda, and at the destination of the branch, I want to use RunnableWithMessageHistory to generate a response considering the chat history. Currently, RunnableWithMessageHistory is also used in the full_chain that includes RunnableLambda. When I check the contents of MessagesPlaceholder in LangSmith, I notice that the history of each exchange is recorded twice. There is no problem with the result as it is, but I want to fix it to avoid unnecessary cost.
