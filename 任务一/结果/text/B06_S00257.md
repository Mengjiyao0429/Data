## 正文
Hey, first time founder! I've worked a lot with backend in the past (databases, MLs, APIs, etc), but this my first attempt at building a product from start to finish, including designing the landing pages and marketing pages. I am building an article generator: I had a chance to learn SEO/content principles from people who are responsible for creating content strategy for some of the most successful startups. The main thing that I want to achieve with this tool is to allow teams of one to create highly valuable content with references to authoritative sources _and_ suggest how to link this content to the rest of the content on their websites. I think I can automate hours of work by combining LLMs with scraping. That's all for now. Just wanted to set context about the product direction. It is still very early days!
