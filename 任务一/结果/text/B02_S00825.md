croit AIplane — one plane for all your AI. Self-hosted AI infrastructure connecting applications and users with models, agents, tools and enterprise data: an OpenAI-compatible LLM gateway, server-side agent execution, MCP, RAG, memory, sandbox, identity, RBAC and quotas. Routes across vLLM, SGLang, Ollama and hosted APIs.
