# Ask HN: Is AI demotivating your will to code?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39569737
- 发布时间：2024-03-02
- 热度：点赞6、评论6

## 正文
I'm beginning to feel what's the point in coding anything algorithmic anymore. I can just fire up GitHub Copilot or whatever and it'll do it faster and likely better. It's like a dying craft now and it's so ironic because the whole beauty of software was to automate things. Maybe it's me realizing that coding was never the important part.
