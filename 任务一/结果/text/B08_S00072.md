## 正文
Since quitting my software engineering job in November because my boss was pushing reckless use of AI, I've spent a ton of time trying to figure out what place AI actually has in the future economy. Thanks in large part to some amazing Substack authors[0,1] as well as the fact that agentic engineering is in the process of rediscovering everything that we have learned about human delegation, I finally had a breakthrough: coherence doesn't scale. A ton of energy has been & will be expended trying to overcome this simple, yet possibly universal law.
