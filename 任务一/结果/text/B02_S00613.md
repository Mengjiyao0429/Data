# Ask HN: Calendar widget to indicate one-sided availability?

- 发布时间：2020-04-13
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
Hi all - I've been working on a tool that matches women to hormonal health specialists. Right now our "product" is more of a lead gen tool that funnels women ("users") to the women's health providers in our directory based on preferences like budget, comm style, treatment methods, etc. We're working on getting a more robust dashboard launched by early summer. That said -- we are having some leaky funnel issues and it seems that a few super quick fixes could have a pretty tangible conversion improvement for our providers. One of these fixes would be a more direct link to booking a free discovery session with one of our providers upon completion of the quiz. The flow we are thinking of is as follows: (1) User takes our quiz and receives their 3 provider matches (2) User indicates their availability for a free discovery session (3) Available slots are automatically pushed to provider(s) of choice, and the specialist can then take the lead on booking a discovery call We are both non technical :) and would ideally put something together quickly ourselves for this as opposed to convoluting the v2 that we'll have a dev be putting together and/or spending more $ to put something more complex together. That said - -we are looking for any widgets that could easily be dropped into a Webflow page where external party A (user) could indicate their availability and have said availability get pushed to another external party/ies (provider(s)). Having trouble finding something that meets this criteria (one-sided availability indication only). Any ideas?
