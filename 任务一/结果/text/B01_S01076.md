## 正文
一个简洁实用的终端音乐播放器，使用 Rust 实现，支持网络歌曲搜索播放、歌词显示、AI推荐、主题切换等功能，支持Windows、Linux、MacOS系统。A simple and practical terminal music player, implemented in Rust, featuring functions such as online song search and playback, lyrics display, AI recommendation, theme switching, etc. It is compatible with Windows, Linux, and MacOS systems.
