## 正文
I have thousands of texts with varying transcription quality. They were generated by an OCR engine that converted document images into text. Sometimes the OCR worked well, but other times the results were poor. I’m looking for a program or web service that can automatically identify which texts are of high quality. Any license, OS or price is fine.
