Good morning/afternoon/evening. I figured this would be an appropriate place to ask due to the demographic and wide ranging roles and responsibilities of the users. So if you're using LLMs or indeed some other Gen AI tool; What specific LLM are you using and for what reason? What are you using it for? Are you using it in a professional or personal capacity? What does your workflow look like? Personally I've only used them for simple code snippets, basic research, summarization and image generation. They each have their own pros and cons[1] but more often than not I find myself either double checking sources or tweaking the end results most of them and only saving maybe 5-10 minutes. [1] The most amusing of which is Gemini, it won't link to any political parties website but will happily help you plan a terrorist attack under the guise of vulnerability analyses and risk management...
