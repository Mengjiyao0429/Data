# Building multiple small AI tools instead of one big SaaS

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46850970
- 发布时间：2026-02-02
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I’m trying an alternative approach to building SaaS. Instead of committing early to one large product, I’m shipping multiple small, focused AI tools to test demand, learn quickly, and double down on what shows traction. I’m curious how others see this: Does this help with discovery and speed? Or does it create long-term focus and positioning issues? Looking for perspectives from people who’ve built or scaled products.
