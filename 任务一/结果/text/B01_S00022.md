# AI-generated posters don’t have to be horrible

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49768641
- 发布时间：2026-09-19

## 正文
Is there an explanation for why AI images, or posters, look "AI"? What I mean is that even some of the better ones, that I quite like, have a distinct AI look that I can’t quite put my finger on. Some elements are, the very saturated color or being overly busy, but not all images have these tells, and not all of them. For a while I thought it was that sort of very subtle dithering/texture that a lot of the images have, but again not all of them. Looking online, most seem to focus on things like the wrong number of fingers, things merging into one another, details that don't make sense, etc., but that’s not what I’m talking about. Does anyone have an explanation for why they are this easy to recognize as AI even when they are decent?
