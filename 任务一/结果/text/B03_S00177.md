## 正文
Hi HN, I recently built CircleCropImage.net — a growing suite of lightweight, privacy-friendly image tools that all run 100% in your browser. No logins, no uploads, no tracking. Just fast, focused utilities that solve common image-editing tasks. Current tools include: Circle Crop – Crop any image into a perfect circle with transparent background Image Enhancer – Automatically improve sharpness, contrast, and detail Background Remover – Remove image background instantly (powered by AI, local preview) Compress to 500KB – Smart image compression for file size limits Blur Image – Apply adjustable Gaussian blur to any photo Resize Image – Resize by dimensions or percentage, while keeping quality Why I made this: Most image tools either: require signup, upload your photos to a server, or are crammed with ads and clutter. I wanted something lightweight, privacy-first, and genuinely useful — especially for quick edits like preparing a profile picture, compressing an image for web, or blurring something before sharing. Everything runs client-side (except the AI background remover which requests a cloud function), and works on desktop & mobile. Would love your feedback or feature requests — thanks!
