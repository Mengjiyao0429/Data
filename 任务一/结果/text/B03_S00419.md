## 正文
DocMind AI is a powerful, open-source Streamlit application leveraging LlamaIndex, LangGraph, and local Large Language Models (LLMs) , LMStudio, llama.cpp, or vLLM for advanced document analysis. Analyze, summarize, and extract insights from a wide array of file formats, securely and privately, all offline.
