# Oido Studio – Build AI employees that run your business

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49010974
- 发布时间：2026-07-22

## 正文
I'm a solo founder building Oido Studio, an open platform for creating AI employees that can use tools, collaborate with other agents, and automate real business workflows. I started building it because I found that most AI assistants are great at answering questions but not at doing work. Businesses don't need another chatbot—they need systems that can execute tasks reliably. With Oido Studio, you can visually build AI agents that: Connect to your existing tools (Gmail, Slack, databases, APIs, CRMs, etc.) Use MCP servers and custom integrations Work together as teams of specialized agents Learn from reusable skills and prompts Trigger workflows and automations Run on the AI provider of your choice instead of locking you into one model A few examples: A sales agent that qualifies inbound leads, researches companies, drafts personalized emails, and updates your CRM. A customer support agent that answers questions, looks up internal documentation, and creates tickets when needed. A real estate assistant that responds to inquiries, schedules viewings, follows up with buyers, and keeps the CRM synchronized. A marketing team that researches competitors, generates content, and publishes campaigns. My goal isn't to replace humans. It's to eliminate repetitive work so people can focus on higher-value decisions. I'd especially appreciate feedback on: Does the product positioning make sense? What would stop you from using it? Which integrations or features are missing? Is there anything that feels overly complex? Website: I'm happy to answer any technical or product questions. Thanks for taking a look!
