## 正文
Anecdotal, I've used ChatGPT to do most of my research on buying things lately. For instance, I am learning how to sew outdoor gear -- backpacks, duffel bags, pouches, etc. I basically take ChatGPT's recommendations at face value when I'm asking about what kind of thread, zippers, webbing, etc. I should by based on what I'm trying to create. While I'm doing the research, I have at times wished I could just say, "Okay, buy that for me from one of these handful of retailers."
