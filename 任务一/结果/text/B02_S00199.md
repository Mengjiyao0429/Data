## 正文
Hey HN, I’ve been building ElevateSells; an AI sales agent that helps solo founders and small teams automate cold outreach from start to finish. Here’s how it works: • You can ask the AI to find leads or use the built-in advanced lead search tool. • You tell it what your business does and who you want to reach. • It writes personalized cold emails, sends them, and follows up automatically. • You can create unlimited email sequences to nurture leads over time; no limits, no manual scheduling. You still control the tone, audience, and strategy — the AI just handles the research, writing, timing, and sending. I built it after getting tired of managing three different tools for lead gen, copywriting, and follow-ups. I wanted one system that sounded human, stayed consistent, and actually got replies. ElevateSells is live now; no waitlist, no demos, just working software. Happy to answer questions, share feedback, or walk through how it works. Thanks, Ahmed
