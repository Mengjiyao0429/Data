# Chatbot recommends specific veterinarians

- 来源：GitHub
- 链接：https://github.com/amavankal/pawgo-chatbot-dev/issues/9
- 发布时间：2026-11-18

## 正文
### Problem The chatbot currently sometimes recommends a specific veterinarian from one of our grounding sources when asked a sensitive question that requires referral to a veterinarian. ### Solution Either clean the grounding sources better, or update the system prompt to clarify that the chatbot should not recommend a specific veterinarian (i.e. no specific locations, brands, hospitals, or people-- only recommend general veterinary advice). System prompt is a more realistic way to go about fixing this.
