## 正文
An offline AI-powered video analysis tool with object detection (YOLO), image captioning (BLIP), speech transcription (Whisper), audio event detection (PANNs), and AI-generated summaries (LLMs ). It ensures privacy and offline use with a user-friendly GUI.
