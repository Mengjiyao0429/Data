# Ask HN: Is all AI coded code in the public domain?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46063586
- 发布时间：2025-11-26
- 热度：点赞4、评论5

## 正文
AFAIK it's generally assumed (but not(?) tested in court, that LLMs can't be assigned copyright, and that copyright for work generated by LLMs can't be claimed by a human just for being in the loop. Is there going to be an avalanche of code that is legally in the public domain, going forward?
