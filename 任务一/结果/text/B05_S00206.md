## 正文
Hey everyone, I'm Edward, Founder at Wandercay ( ) Wandercay is a platform for sharing trip itineraries and travel photos in a more organized and concise way. Our goal is to enable travelers to plan their trips by browsing through other traveler’s and AI generated itineraries for inspiration and then using them as a basis for their own planning. The platform is aimed at those who like to see trip itineraries at a glance, rather than reading through long blog posts and articles. For me, planning a vacation has always taken a lot of time, reading through posts on blogs, social media and other websites to get inspiration. After having the attractions, figuring out where everything is on a map and then organizing their ordering on Google Maps and Sheets was frustrating. Many times, if a friend had already gone to a destination that I wanted to go to, I would ask them for their itinerary and essentially just copy it to minimize the amount of planning I had to do. So after many years of going back and forth on whether I should just build a platform for this, I finally decided to give it a try. While there are other trip planning tools out there, we felt that having a platform focused on organizing the travel community’s itineraries can be of some value. I’d be happy if you gave the website ( ) a try and grateful for any feedback from your experience! It is entirely free to use. If you have any questions or comments, I will be active on the thread and you can also reach us at contact .com. Thank you for the time!
