## 正文
Hey HN, As a solo founder, I struggled to keep my LinkedIn personal page and product blog active while running my business. So, I’m validating an idea for an AI-powered tool that: Understands your brand & products automatically to create content that feels like you Mixes in real-time industry trends to keep posts relevant Writes, schedules & posts automatically – no manual work needed Learns from engagement data to improve future posts Runs hands-free, so you never have to worry about content again Would this save you time? Or do you see other challenges with content consistency that AI could help solve? I’d love to hear your thoughts!
