## 正文
Has anyone found a good solution for managing your software's billing process? We currently use Netsuite and we have a variety of billing types including: Setup fees for new customers Usage based billing for one to many types depending on the customer (example is billing per location but also billing per device active) Min/Max thresholds for usage based billing Pass through merchant of record credit card processing costs To add additional complexity: Invoices are sent monthly or annually We bill some people for future months and others for activity in the last month Right now our dev team runs reports and our accounting team manually builds the invoices out of them based. I'm not to kean on using Chargebee or similar solutions. Seems a bit bloated for what we need. Has anyone tackled this problem before?
