## 正文
I'm currently working on a UK startup and i'm struggling to find a solution for easily paying out my clients. We need to pay our clients every 2 weeks, and our only solution at this moment is to manually send payments banking. Batch payments are limited to 50 accounts and every new account needs to validated pin-entry system which is very slow. We expect to have a quick increase and payouts having to pay over 100 people with different amounts and the process currently is slow and time consuming and i’m trying to find a way around it. I’ve had several calls with my bank and several departments with no success. Does anyone know a UK api/service that can help me achieve my goal so we can create e custom system for paying out using sort-code and account number? We bank with Barclays. Thank you.
