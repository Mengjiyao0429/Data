## 正文
YOW (Your Own Wiki) — a private, local document archive with OCR full-text search. Drop in your scanned PDFs and search them by title and content. Runs entirely on your own computer: no server, no Docker, no cloud, no sign-up. Auto-sorts scans, PDF preview, favorites, trash, reminders, dark mode, bilingual (EN/DE).
