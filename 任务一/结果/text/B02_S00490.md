## 正文
Problem we are solving Managing and searching through unstructured data in the workplace can be cumbersome and time-consuming. Employees spend an avg of 1 hour a day looking for internal information just to do their job. This leads to frustration and significant loss in productivity. The solution We are working on an AI-powered knowledge discovery tool that offers a conversational interface for you to talk to your data and helps you retrieve and make sense of all your data, documents and resources across apps. Think of it as ChatGPT on your own data. Link: My goal I hope to find 10 or more Beta customers in various fields who share the pain points of finding existing information in their daily jobs.
