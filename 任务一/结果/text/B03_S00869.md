## 正文
Offline voice-agent application layer: session core with byte-deterministic replay, spoken-LLM adapters (scripted/replay/loopback-mock), injection-safe function-call grammar, slot grounding, workflow engine with compensation and resume, permissioned tool sandbox, repair-dialog policies, task-success and latency evaluation.
