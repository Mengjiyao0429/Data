## 正文
The full PaddleOCR document pipeline, natively in .NET on ONNX Runtime — no Python, no native PaddlePaddle. Text detection & recognition (80+ languages, auto-detect), layout, tables, formulas & seals, plus LLM-backed key-info extraction, Q&A and chart-to-data. AOT-ready.
