## 正文
Deploy a self-hosted AI stack with Docker Compose: local LLMs, chat, document parsing, embeddings, transcription, text-to-speech and MCP tools. Powered by Ollama, LiteLLM, AnythingLLM and more. Local-first with auto-configured authentication, lightweight stacks, optional HTTPS and NVIDIA GPU support. MIT licensed. Multi-arch: amd64, arm64.
