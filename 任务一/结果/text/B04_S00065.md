## 正文
Working with agents and current-generation coding tools to me still feels relatively cumbersome. You have to do a lot of /manual /skill invocations and go through many review cycles before I consider code even close to workable, let alone commitable. I believe orchestrating this whole ordeal can probably be automated/improved through making good use of coding pipelines, thereby reducing manual intervention in the coding phase itself. So, to minimise reviewing and AI oversight efforts, I was looking for a good way to orchestrate my local pi agent fleet. I found 'synapse' Reddit thread [0], in which the author (not me!) also asks for feedback and collaborators. The tool seems very interesting/promising to me! Final note: I do _strongly_ believe that supervision of the coding process is still a _strict_ necessity. It's just that I think it can probably be more streamlined. [0]:
