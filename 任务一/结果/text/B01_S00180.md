## 正文
We are launching Berri, our always-on-top productivity companion that transforms how you work. Instead of constantly Alt+Tabbing between apps and losing your flow, Berri floats above everything else, giving you instant access to your essential tools Download: Demo video: Why Berri? Instead of constantly Alt+Tabbing and losing focus, Berri gives you instant access to: Smart Notes - Rich text editor with search so you can jot anything down instantly Clipboard History - Never lose anything you've copied again File Browser - Access files without opening Explorer/Finder Smart Screenshots - Rename, drag and drop screenshots as you click them. Extract text using OCR Web Browsing - View all your favourite websites in a secure way Berri is designed to be non-intrusive. Hide it instantly with a single shortcut, resize it to a tiny pill. It's there when you need it, invisible when you don't. Why we built this? We got tired of breaking our flow state every time we needed to reference something, take a note, or check a file. Berri keeps everything you need floating right there while you work. Privacy first Everything stays local on your device No cloud, no accounts, no tracking Incognito mode that hides Berri while screen sharing Available for macOS only for now What's next? This is v1.0, but I have big plans based on your feedback: AI powered workflows Plugin system Cloud sync (optional) Mobile companion I'd love to hear your thoughts, feature requests, or bug reports. Join us at r/berri_app for discussions! If you find it useful, sharing with others who might benefit would mean the world to me. Thanks for giving Berri a shot!
