## 正文
Hey HN, We’re the team behind Partnero — a platform that helps SaaS and e-commerce companies build and scale affiliate, referral, and influencer programs in one place. What is Partnero? Partnero is a powerful affiliate and referral management tool featuring advanced commission settings, automated payouts ( and Wise), fraud protection, and dynamic tracking options like branded links and promo codes. It’s built with partner experience in mind, offering intuitive, white-labeled partner and referral portals. We just launched v2 based on a ton of feedback from early users, and here’s what’s new and improved: Key Features: - Advanced & multi-tier commissions – Use an advanced, performance-based reward model to incentivize your partners. - Goal-based rewards - Keep your partners motivated by rewarding them for reaching set goals. - White-label partner and referral portals – Each partner gets a self-serve dashboard to track performance. - Automated payouts & invoice management – Pay partners at scale, or let us handle it billing. - Custom promo codes & branded links – Ideal for influencers and personal brand alignment. - Newsletter referral program – Let subscribers refer others and grow your list with incentives like coupons or free content. Integrates with Mailchimp, MailerLite, Kit, and more. - Lead submission & tracking – Perfect for B2B partnerships or sales-driven referrals. - Embeddable signup forms – No-code forms and popups for recruiting partners directly on your site. - Shopify & WooCommerce support – Seamless integration for ecom referral programs. - Partnero Marketplace – Connect with top-performing affiliates looking for new programs. We’re aiming to make partner-based growth just as plug-and-play as running paid ads — but with better ROI and long-term value. Happy to answer any questions, share what we’ve learned building this, or hear what you’d expect from a tool like this.
