## 正文
Yanjie — An English Speaking Learning Assistant Based on InternLM, aims to break the traditional interaction boundaries and eliminate the "mute English" phenomenon by combining text chat, speech recognition, and text-to-speech conversion. It is dedicated to improving users' spoken English skills.
