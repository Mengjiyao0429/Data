## 正文
Documind is an intelligent desktop assistant that allows users to upload documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and ask natural language questions about their contents. It leverages local LLMs , integrates advanced OCR for scanned files, and uses semantic indexing with LangChain + FAISS to deliver fast, context-aware answers.
