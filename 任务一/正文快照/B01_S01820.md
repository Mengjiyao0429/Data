## 正文
Kosmos-2.5 is a cutting-edge Multimodal-LLM (MLLM) specializing in image OCR. However, its stringent software requirements & Python-script based invocation make it difficult to use for application development. Here, it has been containerized and made available API, greatly enhancing its ease-of-use.
