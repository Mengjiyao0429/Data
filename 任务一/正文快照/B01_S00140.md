## 正文

RAG-powered document intelligence assistant built for the Midnight Hackathon AI Track. Users upload confidential documents (medical records, contracts, financial statements, legal files) and interrogate them through a natural-language chat interface. The system returns grounded, cited answers without exposing raw document contents unnecessarily.
