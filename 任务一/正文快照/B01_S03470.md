## 正文
Orchestrator is a real-time intelligent conversation system for building personalized multimodal AI interaction workflows, including speech recognition (ASR), text conversation (LLM), text-to-speech (TTS), emotion analysis (Classification & Reaction), memory management (Memory), and 3D animation generation (Audio2Face & Speech2Motion).
