## 正文
I trained a Keras CNN model (binary classification) on a custom image dataset. The accuracy of the model on test dataset came to be 90.53% (also fyi, precision=90.68%, f1_score=91.55). Since I intend to use this model on a mobile device, I post-training quantized it using TFLite (followed this tutorial: ). After quantization, the model returns a prediction probability of 1 always, no matter what image is fed. I have not performed any preprocessing on the images before loading to either of the models.
