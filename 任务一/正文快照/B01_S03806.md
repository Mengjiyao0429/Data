As reference I tried Yolov8 and Roboflow 2.0: Yolo and Roboflow report a recall per class between 0.5 - 0.7. (validation data) When I'm trying to train a model with the exact same data in PyTorch with Resnet 50. My validation recall isn't getting higher than 0.1 and the model is overfitting (since training recall goes to 100%).
