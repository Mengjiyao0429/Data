RAGDrive is an innovative open-source application that uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, combined with advanced large language models and LlamaIndex technology to deliver intelligent, context-aware AI solutions. It integrates seamless web search capabilities and supports mobile and web applications across macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android
