## 正文
A Python tool for transcribing historical documents from PDFs, EPUBs, and images using Tesseract OCR or multi-provider AI vision models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter ). Features intelligent auto-mode, batch processing, structured outputs, token tracking, and configurable preprocessing for large-scale digitization projects.
