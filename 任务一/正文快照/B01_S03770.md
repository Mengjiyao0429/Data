Calculating average_baseline_conversions & incremental_conversions. 3 scenarios: i) If the parent didn't have a channel classification: average_baseline_conversions = the average of the parent's conversions whose date was before the row in question's date. incremental_conversions = the difference between conversions and average_baseline_conversions. ii) If the parent did have a channel classification and we are in the "inherited set", then: average_baseline_conversions = the average of the parent's parent's conversions whose date was before the row in question's date (we can't copy the numbers - we should rather run the same calculation but based on the rows indicated by the parent's parent). incremental_conversions = the average of the parent's incremental_conversions. iii) If the parent did have a channel classification and we are in the other set, then: average_baseline_conversions = average of the parent's conversions whose date was before the row in question's date. incremental_conversions = the difference between conversions and average_baseline_conversions. Scenario 1 and 3 are in the fact the same, just relative to their own parents.
