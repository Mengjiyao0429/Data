## 正文
GenAI harnesses the power of advanced models like GPT-4 and Gemini Pro to serve as versatile coding and writing assistants for users in both R and, soon, Python. This toolbox empowers users with a range of capabilities, including text generation, code optimization, natural language processing, chat assistance, and image interpretation.
