# parrth20/Nyay-Saarthi

This project is an AI-powered web application designed to demystify legal language. It allows users to upload documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and instantly receive a simplified version in plain Hindi. Our goal is to empower individuals by making contracts, policies, and legal notices clear and understandable.
