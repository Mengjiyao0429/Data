## 正文
Hey everyone! I’ve always struggled with tracking transactions, so I built an app to automate it. It converts currencies, assigns categories, and writes descriptions automatically. While building it, I found and added features to track recurring subscriptions and notify me two days before they occur. The entire app was created using AI, as I had no frontend knowledge when I started. Now, it’s open-source for anyone to use or even build a startup with!
