# Ask HN: Need suggestions for our product/business

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37131639
- 发布时间：2023-08-15
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I think we have made the biggest mistake in creating an AI wrapper and calling it a start-up. Two years ago, when chatGPT wasn't in the news yet, we heard about OpenAI and learnt about Copy.ai and Jasper businesses from our friends who are in marketing, and we thought we could do the same and provide more features at a lower price. We ended up creating a business using OpenAI API to begin with and expanded to Dall-e, Midjourney, Hugging Face and Anthropic APIs, and we started providing services such as AI images, blogs, adverts, website summaries, etc. Since chatGPT came into the picture, there is very little left for people to pay for our services as most of the things can be done , and most of the people who have churned out have left feedback that they are using alternatives. What do you recommend? What course of action should we take? Is it worth continuing our exploration and attempting to offer services that chatGPT cannot provide such as connecting different things platform? Thanks.
