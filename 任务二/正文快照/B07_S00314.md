# Ask HN: Crowdsourcing problems in different professions

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=18239774
- 发布时间：2018-10-17
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Hello. My name is Sunil Kumar C and I love building new tools through technology that solve everyday problems we face. Until now I used to build tools to solve my own problems. But lately I've been thinking if we have a curated list of problems that different professionals face in their daily life, it would help all the programmers like me to come up with different products that can solve those problems. Through this post I'm trying to crowdsource those problems/pain points that you guys face. Please go ahead and list the problems that you would like to be solved. If you do not see the profession you're looking for, feel free to add a new column :) Questions which will help you think about problems: What stops you being more profitable? What tasks do you do everyday? What can be automated? What do you hate doing in your business? What product can help you save 2-4 hours per week of your time? etc... Link - I'm really looking forward to see some inputs from all of you!
