# Email queue/customer service tools?

- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=292758

- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
My startup, Kongregate, is currently just using gmail to handle customer service requests. We've automated things a bit with various filters plus structured pages for our top requests. We still get several hundred emails per day, and I'm looking around for tools to help manage it. Any recommendations? Obviously we need to keep improving our self-help system, so a tool that integrates the two usefully would be nice.
