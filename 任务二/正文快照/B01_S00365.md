## 正文
I've used products of this company for many years back then, now I'm retired and just wondering if the company is still OK given that new functionality which should become the driver of the future success is heavilyy downvoted by users: and old functionality, which was once a key driver and is still loved by users, is being consistently demoted by JetBrains (moving it to an optional plugin from the core build is definitely a demotion) Is everything fine, or something is kind'a wrong?
