## 正文
Hey, I need an honest opinion about my latest project idea - Trusted reviews hub. The idea came from a personal challenge I faced as a product designer. I wanted to analyze reviews and feedback about my company and our main competitors. What seemed like a simple task quickly became frustrating — managing reviews, switching between platforms, and finding meaningful insights was much harder than I expected. It was hard to manage reviews even for my own company, and competitors were in the line. So, I decided to solve this issue with my own solution. My main requirements for this platform are: - View combined reviews from different platforms in one place. - Filter, sort, and search reviews by specific parameters. - Get insights and summaries with AI assistance. - Compare companies based on key metrics. - Use custom AI tools to work with specific sets of reviews. As a product designer, I believe that company user feedback is valuable. There are data to dive deep and analyze, but it is to complicated right now, so I hope to solve this. My main concerns: - No one cares about users feedback on these platforms, companies just want to be listed and get badges. - Platforms can be against collecting and publishing open data from their websites to another website. - It could be too expensive for me to allow users limitlesly play around with AI assistance :) Please let me know what do you think about this idea? Any feedback now is really important, thanks. (landing page )
