# Ask HN: What is the purpose of humans in the wake of AI?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40087313
- 发布时间：2024-04-19
- 热度：点赞2、评论4

## 正文
There's no job that can't be automated. These systems will soon be more capable than humans in solving all kinds of problems. It makes me wonder if sentient systems would treat humans the same way humans have treated animals. A more likely scenario: Power gap between a select few and the rest widens massively. (There's probably a few people who are selfless and want the greater good of the society)
