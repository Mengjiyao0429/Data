## 正文
It's really convenient, and a huge time saver, to buy an eSIM just by talking to Claude or ChatGPT. But letting the AI use my saved card without security guarantees scares me a bit. I once had a bad experience with an AI I had given too many permissions: it hallucinated and changed months of work in one night. My question: what security guarantees have you built in? That's what will drive trust and adoption.
