## 正文
A modular macro keypad powered by ESP32-S3 with magnetic snap-on expansion modules. Features 8 switchable keymap profiles, voice commands & speech-to-text, USB+BLE dual-mode, LVGL touch display, RGB effects, and a WiFi companion desktop app for visual key mapping, OTA updates, PC monitoring, and script automation.
