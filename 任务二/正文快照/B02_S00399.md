## 正文
Hey HN, I've just finished an open-source platform for creating AI agents that can collaborate and access external tools, APIs, and real-time data. Here're some of the features: 1. Easily create and customize agents, and equip them with any function as a tool. You have full flexibility. 2. LLM output validation with full type-safety. so your tools never receive unexpected arguments. 3. +10 Streaming hooks that work both on server-side and client-side for modern fast experience. 4. Seamless web integration with streaming and client-side-actions... What are client-side actions? They're functions or tools defined and executed on the client-side, so your agent can perform actions in the user's browser in real-time, like simulating user interactions , Manipulating the DOM, or anything you want! 5. Smart errors auto recovery that communicate errors with the user or the developer based on the error. 6. Long-term memory with chat sessions that can be easily managed. 7. Let your agents collaborate by connecting them into multi-agent boxes, or passing agents as tools to other agents so they can call each other when needed. Scoopika works with your LLMs and on your servers. You can use it completely for free and only pay for extra features. and all your LLMs providers keys are kept on your server and never shared with us. Give it a try:
