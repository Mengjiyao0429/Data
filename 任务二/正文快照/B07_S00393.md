## 正文
Creator here. SkillForge watches what you do on screen and turns it into structured SKILL.md files that AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, etc.) can use. Instead of writing long instruction docs, you just demonstrate the workflow and SkillForge captures it as a reusable skill. Built for the growing ecosystem of AI agents that consume markdown-based skills. Would love feedback from anyone working with AI coding assistants.
