Hi everyone, I'm generally very impressed with ChatGPT* and want to convey the magnitude of it to some friends in various other fields (medicine, teaching, stay-at-home parent, etc...). However, I'm having trouble finding a good example of how to do this. Having it answer questions, write short stories/poems, or explain concepts generally gets a "Oh, neat!" but I feel like the weight of it doesn't really sink in. Has anyone had any luck with certain prompts or ways of presenting it? Thank you! *There is another answer I'm somewhat expecting from the HN crowd, which is that ChatGPT/Stable Diffusion/etc are actually not that impressive - as an AI research engineer I'm pretty biased/bullish on it, though, so you'll have a hard time convincing me of this :)
