# AI needs to address customer support issues

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36063482
- 发布时间：2023-05-24
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
After dealing w/ a horrible automated customer support menu from Microsoft, it made me wonder how come their big investments in AI (ChatGPT) don't fix their automated phone menus that barely function at all. All (or a lot of) the big companies who use AI don't have a proper customer support channel. I imagine this is on the roadmap, but it's a need right now vs. later.
