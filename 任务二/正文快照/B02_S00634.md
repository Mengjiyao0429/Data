## 正文
BuildStream provides a software & hardware solution to optimize heavy construction equipment operations. With our solutions, equipment such as excavators, bulldozers and trucks become connected and smart. Our hardware helps monitor equipment performance and condition in real time, while our software platform provides an all-in-one operations center that helps drive efficiency and productivity. ## About the role: We’re seeking an experienced Full Stack developer to lead the development of BuildStream’s software platform. We're building an exciting product for our users and we want you to help us make it even better! Your responsibilities will include working closely with the co-founding team to help build new features and improve existing ones. You will take control over our tech stack and contribute to our code base daily. You will also contribute to improving our hardware code infrastructure alongside our IoT developers. ## Skills and experience: - 5+ years of hands on software engineering experience in a professional team-based environment - Proficient writing in PHP, with particular knowledge of the Laravel/Lumen framework (We do a lot of API work, so knowledge of API frameworks is a big plus!) - Knowledge of Python. - Knowledge of React JS. - Experience with IoT devices (Raspberry Pi). - Experience with MySQL/MariaDB - Experience with Git, preferably using Bitbucket for source control - Solid understanding of web service architecture.
