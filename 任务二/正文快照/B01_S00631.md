# How to keep track of token news?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16794949
- 发布时间：2018-04-09
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
We know that the crypto market is volatile and there are so many changes happening every day. There is a lot of news to follow. Soros going into crypto. Founders being arrested (the founders of Centra token have been arrested recently in SF). FINMA publishing guidelines on ICOs. Those events have some influence on the market. And these news come to us from all sorts of places - Facebook feed, reddit, Cointelegraph, etc. But also news from the teams do - how they are doing, are they following they roadmap, are they communicating with their communities etc. And me & my team thought it would be useful to have that news in one place. So we added to our tool a newsfeed. Once you choose your favourite tokens (but also any other address that is not a token) you'll get news from them. At the moment we've implemented Medium (as that's where the teams usually share bigger updates) & reddit (automatically choosing the most voted posts not to be overflown by a sea of posts). We are thinking of adding more. What would YOU like to see here? We are building it for people to use it so your feedback will be super appreciated! If you wanna try it check out add some tokens to favorites, add friends or investors you admire to favorites & get your news in one place :) In the end, do you find it useful?
