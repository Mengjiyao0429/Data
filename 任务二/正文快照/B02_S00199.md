## 正文
A modular voice assistant application for experimenting with state-of-the-art transcription, response generation, and text-to-speech models. Supports OpenAI, Groq, Elevanlabs, CartesiaAI, and Deepgram APIs, plus local models . Ideal for research and development in voice technology.
