## 正文
I'm building an AI-driven voice note-taking application that allows you to speak up your mind and convert it into actionable notes. Dumai automatically detects the language you're speaking and transcribes your recording along with summarising the key points mentioned during speech. It also allows you to share notes if needed. Dumai is on TestFlight, and we are looking for beta testers to try it out and share feedback! Our first beta version includes: - Voice recording with instant transcription to capture every thought on the go - AI-generated summaries that transform long recordings into concise bullet points - Custom tags to organize your notes exactly the way you want - Powerful search across both tags and full transcripts to find anything in seconds You can download the app here -
