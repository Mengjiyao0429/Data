## 正文
Hi HN, I guess I'm too new to post on Show HN but I wanted to share about FortyOne, an AGPL-licensed, self-hostable, multi user AI assistant OS designed around SMS as the primary end-user interface. If you ever wanted to share/setup OpenClaw or similar with friends and family but hindered by the setup costs. Check this out! End users text their own named assistant.
