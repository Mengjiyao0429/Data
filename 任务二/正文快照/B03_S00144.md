## 正文
We used to have a scanner-tool* where you clicked one button, then a scan was made and sent directly to a pre-selected and properly pre-configured printer. So having a good scanner and a good printer in the office, you also had a photocopier. I would not refuse some extra features, where you could configure more details. But mainly I need a quick and simple tool (one click or key-shortcut) to make photocopies with our existing hardware without first clicking to do a scan into GIMP and then more clicking to create a print-job. *(Epson is still offering an Event Manager for download, but it cannot trigger their Copy Utility under Windows 11. Hardware is 15+ years old but still working very well. If anybody knows how to get this working in Windows 11, then I do not need a generic tool.)
