## 正文
OCR Invoice and Receipt using Gemini is a FastAPI-based application that processes receipts and invoices using YOLO for receipt detection and Google's Vertex AI for OCR and document analysis. It handles PDF and image files, providing detailed analysis and JSON responses for various document types
