# AI model, API or tool to extract meaningful and recurring segments of videos (.mp4 and .mov) in bulk, and transcribe or subtitle them

## 正文
Is there an AI model (or AI smth like tool) that can take mp4 and mov files in bulk, and return bookmarks representing SEGMENTS (ie start timecode and end timecode) in which a segment is a highlight in the video. Also, provide transcript or subtitles of the voice in the video, if any, BOTH within the highlights, and outside the highlights. Some pointers on the mov/mp4 files: is a mix of both vid files with speaker voices (possibly different) and natural or background sound with no voice many of them follow the same routine on the same special occasion over different years. The bookmark or segment data should roughly be in the following schema export interface Note { id: number; classEvent: string; instance: number; descriptionGroup: string; description: string; videoPath: string; startTime: number; endTime: number; metadata: VideoMetadata; pointers: Array ; transcript?: string; createdAt: string; updatedAt: string; } Especially important is the finding of the same kind of video segments or same kind of words spoken on the same special occasion over different years, and the segments that match in such a way should be given the same "descriptionGroup". The "description" should also be given by the AI model describing BOTH what is shown on the video, and what is heard.
