## 正文
The conventional wisdom is that AIs will replace some jobs in the near future. Every CPA I encounter is "currently not taking new customers" and some are trying to reduce the number of customers they have. From what I've seen, an AI-driven app that can replace a CPA (or even do 90% of the work of the CPA up to final signoff) will have a huge base of hair-on-fire users from day one. If anyone knows of strong contenders already out there, please share.
