# Ask HN: Is there an acceptable way of sharing partially AI generated content?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40200310
- 发布时间：2024-04-29
- 热度：点赞1、评论2

## 正文
Is there a way to share, specifically written, content, that includes AI generated content, publicly in an acceptable way? More context: I sometimes write blog posts that includes content that is, frankly, better for an AI to write (summaries). I don't want to contribute to the AI content apocalypse, and I definitely aim to fact check, edit, and check content for bias. However, is there more I can do? Perhaps ways of demarcating and clearly indicating it's AI generated?
