## 正文
I was implemented ChatGPT Real-time API, that allows to chat with bot in real time with a voice among other methods. But I've faced a problem that ChatGPT always starts talking with itself when using loud speakers ( .defaultToSpeaker ). It works well with headphones and telephone speakers. I've tried to use volume-based filter for speakers output, but it has approximately the same loudness that users' voices, so it didn't work even when I used .voiceChat mode. Adding .duckOthers helped me in case with telephone speakers, but it didn't do with .defaultToSpeaker . Along with that original OpenAI's application works well even with loud speakers. My current session setting is as following: try audioSession.setCategory(.playAndRecord, mode: .voiceChat, options: [.defaultToSpeaker, .duckOthers, .allowBluetooth]) try audioSession.setActive(true) Can you suggest me solution to cut-off speakers audio from microphone input?
