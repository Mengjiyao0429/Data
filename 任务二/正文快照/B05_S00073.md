# IMockupper: I built an AI tool to automate App Store asset generation

- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47155964
- 发布时间：2026-02-25

## 正文
Instead of browsing rigid templates, you just describe the environment you want (e.g., "dark mode, neon purple accents, floating 3D perspective") and the AI builds a professional scene around your screenshot globally You design the layout once, input your translations, and it instantly generates specialized, localized screenshots for every target market and language you need.
