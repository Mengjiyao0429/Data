# TldrLegal – Software Licenses Explained in Plain English

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7367905
- 发布时间：2014-03-09

## 正文
Do you really want people releasing code under your brand? In any case, a license with such restrictions is considered non-free, so you won't find them on the OSI or FSF. There's CC-BY-NC, but they don't recommend applying it to software. I don't know if there's any other freely usable license with such terms, you may need to pay a lawyer to write you one.
