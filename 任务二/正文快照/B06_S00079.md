## 正文
Most AI teams struggle with wasted tokens, repeated context, unpredictable bills, and expensive models. That’s why we built LightReach Compress, an OpenAI- and Cursor-compatible gateway that solves these problems without sacrificing output quality. It compresses repeated prompts and scaffolding, routes requests to the cheapest model that meets your quality target (HLE-based), and enforces Smart Budget determinism, automatically adjusting model quality to stay within hard monthly caps. Every request is tagged for cost attribution, and conversation history is preserved for debugging, replay, and analytics. BYOK security ensures your provider keys are never stored. Integration is simple: swap the base URL + API key, keep your existing OpenAI client code, and it works with /v1/chat/completions and /v1/cursor/chat/completions. Making this work reliably wasn’t tri . We had to handle exact SSE streaming, tool-call edge cases, missing tool schemas on follow-ups, protocol leakage prevention, and subtle UTF-8 issues—all while keeping costs predictable and outputs accurate.
