## 正文
Introducing RichAPI! A big thank you to Sebastián Ramírez Montaño for creating FastAPI, which made seamless documentation possible. Inspired by his work and Marcelo Trylesinski similar library(Fast-Responses), I developed RichAPI – a tool that uses AST to analyze your FastAPI code and enhance Swagger with all possible responses, not just 2xx. By parsing your router's code, RichAPI automatically detects raised HTTP exceptions, and use that to provide a complete overview of your API’s behavior.
