Update: I used cv2 to get the contours and used cv2.fillPoly to fill gray color. It is almost there but after filling the contour lines disappeared as below. Can the lines be kept after filling gray color? Thank you! import cv2 img = cv2.imread("image.jpg") gray = cv2.cvtColor(img,cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY) ret, binary = cv2.threshold(gray,127,255,cv2.THRESH_BINARY) contours, hierarchy = cv2.findContours(binary,cv2.RETR_TREE,cv2.CHAIN_APPROX_SIMPLE) cv2.fillPoly(img,[contours[1]],(128,128,128)) plt.imshow(img) plt.show()
