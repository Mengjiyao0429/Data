I spent weeks looking for an AI maturity framework that addressed both the org transformation AND the human capability side honestly. I couldn't find one. So I built it. Here's what the framework proposes: → There are 5 organizational AI maturity stages (Task Automation → Agentic Enterprise) → There are 5 individual maturity stages (AI as a Tool → AI-Native Thinker) → Each org stage has a *Human Maturity Gateway* — a minimum threshold of individual AI fluency in the workforce before the organization can credibly advance The insight that kept nagging me: *Organizational AI maturity cannot outrun the individual AI fluency of the people governing it.* Most AI programs invest heavily in technology and process — and far less in developing the individual AI fluency of the people who govern, design, and evolve those systems. That gap is not a training problem. It is a structural one. A quick honest diagnostic: When was the last time you used AI to *challenge your thinking* — not execute a task you'd already decided on? If you can't remember a specific instance, you're at Individual Stage I. Stage II is where the real transformation begins — for individuals and organizations alike. I built this framework in genuine conversation with AI. Which is itself a demonstration of Individual Stage II. The thinking is mine. The synthesis was sharper with AI in the room. Full framework attached — five org stages, five individual stages, workforce reality at each, and a self-assessment diagnostic you can use with your leadership team today.
