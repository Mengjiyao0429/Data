# Ask HN: Why don't we have "Guilds" for solo AI founders yet?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46497617
- 发布时间：2026-01-05
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I'm a solo founder leveraging AI to build faster. The efficiency is there, but I've noticed a paradox: while AI makes us independent, it also creates isolated silos. Currently, the support structures for founders are mostly vertical: Accelerators (Mentor -> Mentee) VC funding (Investor -> Startup) I feel we lack horizontal collaboration—a structured way for solo founders to act like a team without merging companies. Think of it like a "Guild" where we could: Swap automated workflows and internal tools Cross-promote products to overlapping audiences Share collective infrastructure Ask HN: Do you have a structured "peer group" or collective you actively build with? Or is everyone mostly building in isolation right now? I'm curious if new organizational models are emerging that I'm missing.
