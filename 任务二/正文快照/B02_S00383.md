## 正文
After working on OctoBot for 6 years, at first as a side project and now as a fulltime job, with my co-founder, we are very proud to announce the Product Hunt launch of OctoBot ! OctoBot is a free open source software (it's all on GitHub), in short, OctoBot: - Can automate most crypto investment strategies (including crypto baskets, DCA, AI & grid trading and TradingView automations) on more than 15 exchanges - Allows to test and optimize each strategy with virtual money before using real funds - Is designed to be easy to use, including a full user interface, a mobile app, an integrated Telegram bot If (like us) you are investing is crypto and would like to automate your strategy using a fully transparent and open source software, you definitely should give OctoBot a try!
