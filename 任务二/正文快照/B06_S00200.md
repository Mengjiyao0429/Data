Hello HN, Hope you're having a good start into your week!! I'm submitting this question because I'm curious about how to use the O1 Pro and want to learn from others. I subscribed to ChatGPT Pro when they announced it because as a person who earns their income purely from software, I believe that I cannot afford to be left behind in terms of developing an intuition for what's possible to do with AI, how, when, etc. Since then I've been using it for most chats since there aren't any limits and I've noticed that the responses seem more like a stream of consciousness and a little more composed compared to GPT-4 or 4o which sometimes is quite verbose. There's definitely a feeling that tiny nuances or side-notes that might affect the main task are considered more carefully. A job I've been using it for regularly is pasting my entire error log report on it and it gives me a summary of errors per user with a simple visualisation. I'm sure that I'm not being creative enough and would love to learn about how you've been using O1 Pro and what your experience or review of it is so far.
