## 正文
I am currently in the application process of an assistant professor for AI and would be interested in how it should be teached today. Up until recently, most courses always focused on the technical aspect like backpropagation, but with GenAI and LLMs the focus is shifting. How can tools like ChatGPT be implemented in the curriculum as a benefit without having the students just using it without correctly learning it?
