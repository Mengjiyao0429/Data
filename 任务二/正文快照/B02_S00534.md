# I’m an AI consultant, client wants me to sign a non-compete

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33978312
- 发布时间：2022-12-14
- 热度：点赞20、评论11

## 正文
I’m working as an AI consultant and I have a client who wants me to sign a 3-year non-compete that would not let me use any of the generative AI technologies they use in any other commercial or research projects even if it doesn’t compete with theirs. Obviously I can’t sign this contract. Has anybody come across something similar to this? If so how did you handle it? How common is this?
