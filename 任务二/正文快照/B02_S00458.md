# Which are top AI-Based Document processing Platforms?

## 正文
The top 5 AI-based document processing platforms involve: KlearStack, ABBY Flexicapture, Laserfiche, Parascript, Docsumo. KlearStack: KlearStack's AI-powered algorithms deliver remarkable accuracy, achieving an impressive 98% precision rate in data extraction from invoices, contracts, and forms and a 60% reduction in processing time compared to manual methods. This way, businesses obtain error-free and reliable information, reducing costly mistakes and improving decision-making. Healthcare, legal, government, education, retail, banking, finance, insurance are all the industries that it KlearStack works with. ABBY Flexicapture: ABBYY FlexiCapture redefines document processing by leveraging machine learning to automate data classification, extraction, validation, and routing. It achieves an impressive 95% accuracy rate in categorization and data extraction. This leads to a 50% reduction in processing time. It handles invoices, tax forms, onboarding documents, and more. Utilizing deep learning Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for image-based classification and advanced text analysis, FlexiCapture Laserfiche: Laserfiche revolutionizes operations through automation. It can achieve 30% faster application processing with streamlined automation, reduce resolution time by 40% through automated case tracking, and cut onboarding time by 50% with paperless, automated document handling. Parascript: Parascript leads in computer vision, excelling in image and text classification through state-of-the-art AI. Their unique topological character recognition, powered by neural networks, achieves remarkable precision. Parascript's AI-driven solutions achieve exceptional image and text classification, with accuracy rates surpassing 95%. Docsumo: Docsumo is a powerful AI-driven platform that automates data capture, extraction, and processing for a wide range of document types. By utilizing a blend of intelligent Optical Character Recognition (OCR), AI, and Machine Learning, Docsumo's intelligent OCR extracts text and data from documents with precision, minimizing errors during conversion. Its API models are trained to recognize diverse document layouts, leading to data extraction accuracy of up to 98%. Docsumo reduces manual intervention, saving up to 70% of processing time and cutting manual errors by up to 90%.
