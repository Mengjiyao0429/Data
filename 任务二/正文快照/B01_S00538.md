## 正文
Are you looking for a way to gain a deeper understanding of personality psychology underlying behavior of individuals and groups? Look no further than Sentino, a blend of a cutting-edge AI solution and personality psychology. This AI-powered semantic text analysis tool provides comprehensive psychological portraits of your customers, partners, and employees in a matter of minutes. Sentino can help you to better resonate with your target audience. Psychological basis of Sentino is represented by 20+ carefully selected psychological personality tests. These are scientifically validated and widely used inventories like BIG 5, NEO, RIASEC, ORVIS, ORAIS, DISC, etc. The technical basis is formed by advanced AI / ML / NLP technologies. Our development team has integrated vast psychological data and trained models using the state-of-art NLP stack that is able to transform any structured or unstructured natural text (self-descriptions, CVs, answers to questions, LinkedIn profiles, social media posts, messages, etc.) into psychologically meaningful representation and project this representation onto personality scales. We continuously keep researching and updating the models, striving for higher accuracy and better match with the customers’ needs. Sentino is served as an API. One of its key benefits is ease of use. Simply integrate Sentino into the existing system or platform and start analyzing text data right away. The tool is able to process large data volumes quickly and accurately and provide valuable personality insights. We substantiate the results with raw data and provide a statistical confidence level for all and any numerical values. Sentino is already successfully used in the following areas:  HR (hiring, onboarding, training and development, assessment)  Personalization of customer support services  Insurance and crediting (as a part of fraud detection)  Vocational guidance and academic content selection  Migrant integration  Intelligent customer segmentation Currently, our top-selling products are:  Interview Analysis API (processing of an audio or video interview records for generation of multifaceted personality portraits that can be subsequently updated and supplemented)  LinkedIn Personality API (scoring of LinkedIn profiles and delivery of comprehensive personality portraits without a dedicated testing procedure) Please visit our website to find more information and request your free demo:
