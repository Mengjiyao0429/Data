## 正文
We’re Athenic AI. We build tools that let teams explore and analyze data using natural language — AI-powered BI without writing SQL or manually building dashboards. We’re exploring whether to release a self-hosted Community Edition of our platform and want to get feedback before investing time. This version would be: - Bring-Your-Own-LLM (so you can connect it to the model you want) - Distributed as a self-contained Docker image - Designed for teams that want analytics/BI superpowers but prefer to keep everything in their own environment We’d love to hear from you: 1. Would this be valuable or useful to you? 2. What’s your use case? (personal project, internal BI, client work, etc.) 3. What’s your role at your company (data engineer, analyst, founder, etc.)? No marketing, no signup links. Just trying to gauge interest from people who might actually use it. Appreciate any feedback! — The Athenic Team
