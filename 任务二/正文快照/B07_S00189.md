# Ask HN: Founders, what was the major sourcing channel for your first 100 users?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41888197
- 发布时间：2024-10-19

## 正文
> Reddit is incredibly powerful if you are building something niche and are already a part of the community I generally agree with this but I will say (at least in my niche) that I’ve been pretty surprised recently with both how 1) anti ‘self-promotion’ some subreddits have become and 2) how brutal people will be if your thing is not fully free and open source. I understand where they’re coming from, but I’d recommend software founders know this when trying to use Reddit as a distribution channel.
