## 正文
Hi HN, I’m working on a new tool called bragsheet.io , designed to automate the creation of brag sheets for software engineers. For those unfamiliar, a brag sheet is a document where professionals track their accomplishments and contributions throughout the year, such as PRs, code reviews, documentation, presentations, and shout-outs on social media. These sheets are often used during performance reviews to highlight achievements and contributions. I'm seeking feedback from the community to help shape the development of this tool. Your insights will be invaluable in ensuring it meets the needs of software engineers like you. The survey should take about 5 minutes to complete. If you’d like to stay updated on the development of bragsheet.io or participate in beta testing, there’s an option to provide your email address at the beginning of the survey (completely optional). Thank you for your time and feedback! Best, Jeremy
