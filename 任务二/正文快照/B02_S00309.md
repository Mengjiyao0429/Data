## 正文
A few years ago, I was one of the top performers on my engineering team, shipping high quality code fast, reviewing PRs, and unblocking teammates. But instead of being rewarded, I started getting pushback from low performers who felt “pressured” by my output. The engineer manager, instead of looking at the actual data, sided with the complainers ( they all were more “Senior” ). That’s when I realized: engineering performance is judged on gut feelings, politics, and who uses the most sophisticated words. So I built PerfPerf, an AI-powered tool that automatically tracks engineering productivity with real, objective data. No more guessing, no more biased reviews. It’s just clear insights on who’s actually delivering.
