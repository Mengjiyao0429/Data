# Built an AI tool to automate staying updated market moves on your portfolio

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44624599
- 发布时间：2025-07-20
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
I love investing but I don't have the time anymore to keep track of all market moves on a weekly/day-to-day basis, and make up how it impacts my portfolio. I wanted to test out Cursor and OpenAI/Gemini and I built financebro.io. It uses Gemini and OpenAI to scrape the web for the latest news related to the portfolio tickers. It also gathers Price Targets and any updates to it for each stock. It sends out end-of-trading week recaps to the user on email, every Friday. I absolutely love it for my personal use and I have some friends that also actively use it. It's a bit pricy to maintain but I guess I'm looking to see if I can get a few paying customers for some validation to keep developing this. Have a look!
