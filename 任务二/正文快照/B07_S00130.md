# Ask HN: Is There Room for a Clear, Trustworthy, Human-Centric AI Search Tool?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44787862
- 发布时间：2025-08-04
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Traditional search engines are often overwhelmed with ads and cluttered blue links. Meanwhile, many AI-powered search tools struggle with inaccuracies and present cold, uninviting interfaces. Here’s my question to the community: Is there a real opportunity for an AI search tool that provides clear, reliable (validated) answers wrapped in an interface that feels genuinely human and approachable? These are the three qualities I believe matter most. What are your thoughts?
