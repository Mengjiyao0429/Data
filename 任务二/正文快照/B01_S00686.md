## 正文
Spectral graph-augmented retrieval for RAG. Augments dense vector search with a weighted chunk graph and its Laplacian spectrum, graph expansion, spectral diffusion, and cluster-aware selection, to retrieve context that is jointly relevant, non-redundant, and connected for multi-hop reasoning.
