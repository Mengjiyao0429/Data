## 正文
I'm using cloud models for development, and many local models for internal works. Local models a good for doing the part of work related to personal user information, internal functions, like facts extraction and chats consolidation, web search and market research, internal documents preparation and handling. I also use local decision models (nimble, clef) for classification and other related staff, local embedding and voice recognition models.
