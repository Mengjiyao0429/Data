## 正文
I know that conventional wisdom is that PR reviews are essential for engineers' learning and important to ensure that we are in the loop about what other engineers on your team are working on (plus checks and balances, of course). However, the amount of time it takes to review some of the PRs makes me question if this should be done differently so that we, as engineers, can focus on higher-level, more important, and creative work.
