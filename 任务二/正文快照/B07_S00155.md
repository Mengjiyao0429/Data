## 正文
I’m building an IP enrichment script to drop straight into your site. It captures visitor IPs (or bulk lists) and returns actionable traffic attribution data: Organic vs. Paid Traffic – Heuristics to estimate whether visits came from search engines, ad networks, or direct. LLM-Driven Geo Detection – Spot traffic originating from popular LLM proxies (e.g. ChatGPT browsing, Bard, Claude) and approximate their region. Custom Paid-Channel Tagging – Let you define IP ranges or ASNs for your own DSPs, affiliate networks, VPN-based ads, etc. Flexible Delivery – Enriched data can be POSTed back to your own endpoint on your site, or sent to a hosted web dashboard we provide. Key features: Zero extra tracking scripts — just a tiny client-side snippet + serverless lookup. JSON output to pipe into your existing analytics or BI tools. Configurable thresholds so you can tweak detection sensitivity. Use cases: Content teams: measure real SEO-driven users vs AI-powered scrapers. Growth marketers: detect if an LLM agent is skewing your geographic traffic data. Ad ops: attribute visits to specific IP-range campaigns without extra JS tags. I’d love your feedback on: Would you pay for something like this? What pricing model feels right? Pay-as-you-go (e.g. $0.005 per lookup) Monthly tiers (e.g. $49/mo for 100K lookups) Anything I’ve missed, other use cases you’d find valuable, or pricing expectations? Thanks for your thoughts!
