## 正文
Post: Hey HN community, I’ve been working with a platform called Core Form, an online form builder that aims to simplify data collection for businesses of all sizes. I wanted to share some insights on the process of building and scaling such a platform, and I’d love to hear your experiences and suggestions as well! Key Features of Core Form: Responsive Layout: Forms are fully responsive, ensuring a seamless experience on all devices. Drag & Drop Builder: Users can create complex forms without writing a single line of code, thanks to an intuitive drag-and-drop interface. Embed Options: Forms can easily be embedded on websites, blogs, or landing pages. Advanced Analytics: Users gain detailed insights into form submissions, including response rates, completion stats, and more. Data Privacy & Security: With growing concerns over data security, Core Form provides advanced protection features to ensure all data is safe. Zapier Integration: Automate workflows by connecting Core Form with over 2,000 apps . Webhook Integration: This feature enables users to send form data directly to their own servers or third-party applications. Challenges Encountered: One of the biggest hurdles has been finding the balance between offering enough features to satisfy power users while keeping the interface simple for non-technical users. We’ve also invested heavily in making sure that the platform scales smoothly, even as more users and data are added. My Questions to You: What are some growth strategies or features that can differentiate Core Form in the crowded market of form builders? Have any of you worked on similar projects, and if so, how did you tackle the challenge of scaling while maintaining performance and security? Any thoughts on the latest trends in online form creation and how they might impact the industry?
