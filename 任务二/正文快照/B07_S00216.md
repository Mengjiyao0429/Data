## 正文
Siri wouldn't be the same garbage it was 15 years ago if Apple was serious about AI. They didn't have to jump into frontier lab status, all they had to do was spend the last 10-15 years making Siri better and they'd have had something great to plug transformer-based models into or something even better for a chatbot. But they sat on their hands and lost a 10+ year advantage and now they are playing catch-up because Siri is still awful and getting crushed by this generation of chatbot. Apple screwed up sitting on Siri like that, and it did cause them to miss the AI revolution. That was Cook's screw up. Jobs set him up for success and he fell down on the job.
