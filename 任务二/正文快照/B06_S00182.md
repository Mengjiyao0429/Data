Trood CLI is an open-source, AI-powered debugging tool for software troubleshooting that leverages AI to identify current and potential problems in a project, such as dependency conflicts, bugs and other problematic issues, before they reach production. As we continue to shape this concept, we want to share our vision with you and gather your valuable feedback and insights to make this tool more efficient and useful for You. We struggle everyday with the frustration of debugging that's why We invite you to share your thoughts on the concept and contribute to our repository.
