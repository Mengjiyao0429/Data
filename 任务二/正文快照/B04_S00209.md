# Ask HN: Do you still use Search Engines?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43620442
- 发布时间：2025-04-08

## 正文
I used an AI tool for the first time this weekend to get a military CAC to authenticate to websites through Firefox on Arch. It took more than half a dozen uses of the AI tool to get what I was looking for though. Super edge case and even the AI struggled like a human. Yes, I still use search engines and almost always find what I need in long form if I can’t figure it out on my own.
