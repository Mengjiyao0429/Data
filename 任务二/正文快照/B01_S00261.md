## 正文
Hey HN, I’m excited to share EatDoSleep ( ), a micro-SaaS I’ve been working on to simplify travel planning with AI. It’s designed for solo travelers, groups, or anyone who wants a seamless way to plan trips without juggling endless tabs. I’d love your feedback to help make it better! What It Does: • AI-Powered Itineraries: Generate hour-by-hour travel plans with locations, photos, and curated details in seconds. • Save & Share: Save plans to your profile and share them with friends or the community. • Social Features: Follow other travelers, like/comment on plans, or co-plan with friends. • Book & Edit: Add hotel/tour bookings to your plan and edit anytime. • Inspiration: Discover trending plans from other users to spark ideas. Tech Stack: • Built with Next.js for a fast, server-rendered UI. • PostgreSQL for the database. What’s Coming: • Multi-City Trips: Based on user feedback, we’re working on multi-stop itineraries with transport options like trains and buses. • Expanded Destinations: Some users noted missing cities—we’re actively growing our database, adding more destinations daily. If you search for a spot and it’s not there, let me know which one, and I’ll prioritize it! Challenges I’d Love Help With: • Traction: Struggling with organic traffic. Any tips on growth for a travel SaaS (SEO, content, or low-budget marketing)? • Destination Coverage: How do you handle scaling location data for a global audience? I built EatDoSleep because travel planning often feels like a chore—scattered tools, outdated info, and no easy way to collaborate. My goal is to make it as intuitive as chatting with a travel-savvy friend. Try it out (it’s free!), create a plan, and let me know what you think. I’d love to hear about it. Looking forward to your thoughts!
