# I want to build AI shopping assistance for Shopify stores

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35490704
- 发布时间：2023-04-08
- 热度：点赞2、评论1

## 正文
i wanted to build an ai shopping assistance for existing shopify store, where just like intercom or other chat widget, people can simply copy and paste a js code snippet on their shopify website and ai shopping assistance will be installed on their site. This ai shopping assistance will be powered by openai api and will open a chat widget with voice and chat mode. You can ask it about any product or shopping help on that site. Ex - Show me silk dress for wedding occassion It will welcome the user first and the will reply and help them in finding the right product and discount, shpping info, return policy all in interactive voice or chat mode.
