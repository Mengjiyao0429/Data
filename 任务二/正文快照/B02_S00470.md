## 正文
Homegrown HN writing, only at Blog HN! Haha. What do you think? Will this become a thing? OK, onto the topic of today's little blog by me, your humble interlocutor. I just occurred to me that as a result of the tremendous velocity magnifier that is AI, there will be many upcoming societal changes, and many second order effects. One of these will be the rise in jobs that take care of, maintain, tend to, and operate these giant automated tools. Tools that, today, look much like ChatGPT, but in the future, will become highly specialized and do all sorts of funny things wonderfully well. Most likely. So, that's why I hit you with the paradoxical anachronism that jobs like Technician and Machinist are going to come back. AI Machinist (for a cyberspace machine), Technician (for an AI automation for some task). Typically Machinist and Technician are thought of as older-school jobs (although of course there are many fields were that is just manifestly untrue, Lab Technician for instance is a person, by definition, at the cutting edge; similarly CNC Machinist can be operating in this most esoteric high-falutin realms of the aerospace industry). But in general these trad terms are reserved for a more civilized age, to quote OBI-WAN. A time when steam punk was not a punk but a purpose. When machines, and engines, and factories, ruled verily the Earth upon which now you lazily stretch your feet curled up in your comfort ball lovingly caressing your featureless plank of rarified glass, sparkling with the bits of a 1000 worlds.
