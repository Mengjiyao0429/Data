## 正文
Yes you read that right. I made a software that eliminates the need for virtual assistants. My name is Marion, and I’m the Co-Founder of ReachWizard; A software aimed to serve as a virtual assistant for businesses. Why did I create this? I spent months trying to identify a pain point in the market. “What do businesses AND customers need? What is something that can help both?” I spent months thinking about this. Until, I stumbled upon the idea of virtual assistants. There are lot’s of issues that businesses run into when employing a virtual assistant: Time zone differences — depending on the location of the business and location of the VA, time zones can cause delays in communication and make it difficult to schedule/achieve things efficiently. Performance — VA’s may not always meet deadlines or keep up consistent quality work. It’s hard to ensure that your VA is staying productive and focusing on their tasks. The lists goes on but the point I’m trying to make across the board is, Businesses run into many problems when hiring a VA. This is not to say VA’s don’t get the job done or aren’t worth it. I 100% support them and everything they do. However, what if I were to tell you about a software that does all the tasks that a VA does: Handles conversation while maintaining authenticity, responds to customers within 1–2 minutes, gives 100% accurate and tailored responses to the client, and is 10x cheaper than hiring a standard VA. Introducing ReachWizard. Started by me and my partner, we offer a software that acts completely like a human VA. Testing this tool for the past month has been very fun. The responses it gives us is amazing. I would never guess I’m talking to a software. I have seen incredible results with this and I can’t wait to launch it to the market this week. Launch day is expected by the end of this week; we are currently adding people to our waitlist. reachwizard.co
