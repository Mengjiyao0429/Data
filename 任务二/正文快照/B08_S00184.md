## 正文
The Open Source Initiative released their definition of open source AI: Incidentally, I need to embed an LLM in my Android app for summarization/etc purposes. Question : What LLM fits the Open Source Artificial Intelligence definition? Must be usable by an Android app, even when offline. Bonus for those that perform well on various benchmarks, and for those that are tiny enough to fit in 1GB or even 100MB. Bloomz is said to be rather open, but its license does not fit the Open Source Artificial Intelligence definition. Llama and Deepseek, despite confused press coverage, do not either.
