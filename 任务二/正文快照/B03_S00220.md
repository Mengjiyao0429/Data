## 正文
On October 15, 2023, Moka launched Moka Recruiting, an international recruitment platform designed to help Chinese enterprises expand globally. As companies navigate new markets, they face hiring challenges—compliance issues, cultural differences, and logistical barriers. Moka Recruiting aims to bridge these gaps with a unified, AI-powered recruitment system. The Challenges of Global Hiring Expanding abroad means dealing with diverse legal and cultural hiring norms. A blockchain company recently ran into legal trouble for requiring "proficiency in English" in job listings, violating local anti-discrimination laws. Data protection regulations further complicate recruitment. Companies must now balance global talent acquisition with legal compliance and data security. How Moka Recruiting Solves These Problems Moka Recruiting builds on MokaHR’s domestic success by offering a streamlined, data-driven approach to international hiring. Key features include: Unified Recruitment Management – Centralized control over global hiring, ensuring consistent recruitment standards across regions. Automated Compliance Checks – Reducing risks associated with varying labor laws and data privacy regulations. Cross-Regional Collaboration Tools – Handling time-zone differences and language barriers in interviews. AI-Enhanced Talent Matching – Optimizing candidate sourcing to attract the right talent efficiently. Why This Matters Managing international recruitment without a centralized system can lead to inefficiencies, compliance risks, and hiring mismatches. Moka Recruiting addresses these issues by providing visibility across hiring processes, reducing errors in offer issuance and approvals, and ensuring alignment between headquarters and regional teams. The Future of Global Talent Acquisition With businesses expanding internationally, recruitment needs smarter, more adaptable solutions. Moka Recruiting equips companies to navigate the complexities of global hiring without sacrificing efficiency or compliance.
