# Ask HN: Recommendations for GitOps tools to manage Django apps on On-prem VMs

- 发布时间：2023-10-06

## 正文
I am embarking on a GitOps project to manage multiple environments of a Django app. The environments could range from various pre-production stages to separate production instances for distinct enterprise SaaS customers. The tech stack includes Redis, PostgreSQL, OAuth identity management, and the setup aims to be hardware and cloud agnostic. Currently, our deployment pipeline involves GitHub Actions with hosting on Digital Ocean managed Kubernetes. We're looking to extend support to on-prem VMs (VM Ware). I am keen on hearing recommendations on tools or approaches from the community, especially those with on-prem VMs experience. What has worked well for you in similar setups? Your insights would be invaluable. Thank you!
