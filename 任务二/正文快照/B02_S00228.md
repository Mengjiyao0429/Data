Hey HN, I've spent the last 2 years wrestling with the growing chaos of digital life—juggling endless apps, wasting time switching tabs, and suffering the “too many tools, too little actual work done” problem. So I built OneNoughtOne: An AI that doesn't just chat—it acts as your universal digital assistant, orchestrating actions across the tools you already use. No more logging in everywhere or context switching between apps. What Does It Do? Connects: Gmail, Google Docs, Canva, Stripe, Swiggy, Asana, Notion, Atlassian, Github, Project Management, Online Groceries, Payments, Trading, Error Monitoring… 20+ platforms at launch. Acts : Send emails, search groceries, automate finances, generate designs and presentations, pull financial reports, manage docs, search codebase docs and more—by simply declaring your intent in chat. True AI Delegation: Not just text generation. Ask for a structured Notion doc or a Canva presentation; you get the actual asset created automatically inside your workspace. Why Build This? I personally hit the breaking point: Sending emails, managing projects, tracking online deals, checking groceries on various quick commerce platforms, tracking finances, jumping between apps—all slowing me down daily. OneNoughtOne is my answer: an AI that spans your stack, interprets your intent, and executes actual work. Key Technical Details MCP Protocol: Uses the Model Context Protocol to plug seamlessly into a growing library of services—no glue scripts. Security: OAuth for sensitive services. Actions use the same permissions as you would UI. Extensible: Add new integrations as fast as new servers are available. Example Use Cases: - “Draft a Notion page about Indian culture.” An emoji-rich, organized doc appears in your Notion. - “Create Canva slides for Raksha Bandhan.” Receive a formatted presentation in your Canva account. - “Get Google’s Q2 balance sheet.” Latest data pulled dataset APIs. - “Search for chocolates and ghee across quick commerce stores such as Swiggy, Blinkit, and BigBasket.” Results in one reply. - “Send 50 custom emails, generate reports, schedule meetings…” All, in a single thread. Why Would HN Care? - Built for everyone- productivity-minded users from any walk of field, frustrated with fragmented workflows. - Not just another GPT wrapper—delegation, not generation. - Auto connects to the needed MCP Servers based on the task assigned, no need to toggle on and off MCP Servers. - Open to early adopters for free while perfecting the experience.
