I have been looking at saucelabs.com for automated testing and also looking into TDD(test driven development) but I am interesting in finding out what you do at your company to test for code/products before you ship. Another question I'd love to know the answer to, large companies such as GitHub who push code everyday how do they browser test? Is it someones job to just sit there and manual test all the browsers? Or do they use a system such as sauce labs?
