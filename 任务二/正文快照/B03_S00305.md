## 正文
Greetings, Can anyone recommend double-entry accounting software for a small business? Ideally features would include US payroll form filing, US state unemployment form filing, and the ability to share account info with a financial professional. I’ve been using Wave for the last few years, but recent issues with the IRS revealed they’ve been incorrectly filing my paperwork with the wrong address. They have had both my physical and mailing addresses in their system since I opened the account. However, although the account settings show these addresses correctly, they are unable to enter them properly on IRS or state forms, or even in the quarterly filing reports they email me, with zip codes swapped. This is a huge red flag for me with respect to their data integrity in general. Weeks of back and forth with their support have gone nowhere, and they are unwilling to reach out to me to discuss the issue (only allowing email contact). At this point, they feel it’s a non-issue that I continually receive address change notifications from the IRS and have decided they will do no further action to repair their systems. Anyway, appreciate any recommendations!!
