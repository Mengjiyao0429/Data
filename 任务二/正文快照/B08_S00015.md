My dream is to become a chairman who focuses on investing then the company figures out how to make money. When AI agents raising, I also tried vibe coding for building products, but I feel tired when still being in loop with them for building and testing. Then I came across with idea for a company operated by agents based on KPIs, not tasks. The only one thing I need to do is gave KPIs to CEO (smartest agent), then It break down as a list to Managers and Managers delivery it to team member. Or If I don't give any KPIs, Managers will daily and report to CEO what changes, what nexts to do, then CEO report to me.
