## 正文
In 2025, We still need Human in the Loop to make LLMs more powerful and effective. After 10 months of hard work, numerous ideas, and technology iterations, we are happy to launch Neuwark Engaging customers across platforms while maintaining quality can be overwhelming. Neuwark simplifies this with real-time AI chatbots, human-in-the-loop capabilities, and training with enterprise data. Why Neuwark? Real-Time Human-in-the-Loop Chat: Ensure quality at all times. Customizable AI Agents: Optimize with advanced settings and prompt adjustments. Team-Friendly: Collaborate easily on training and management. Responses with References: Transparent and credible answers. Built-in Observability: Monitor agent working processes. Multi-Channel Engagement: Connect across Web, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, and Instagram. We’ve built Neuwark to empower businesses to elevate customer engagement without compromising on quality. Whether automating support, scaling communication, or enhancing collaboration, Neuwark’s Human-in-the-Loop AI is here to help. This wouldn't have been possible without the incredible support from the LlamaIndex and Chroma teams, who truly acted as extended teammates.
