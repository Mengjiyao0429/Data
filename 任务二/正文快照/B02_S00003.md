I'm asking the question focusing specifically on what I could use/download/configure right now, not necessarily on any custom written harness you might be using which isn't shared and fits your specific workflow. Not that it makes it to this page at least. So to repeat the question - is there anything that you use that is available out there that helps your workflow, gets you better results in terms of lowering resource usage, costs and/or quality?
