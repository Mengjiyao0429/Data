## 正文
I know that there are already a few threads out there, where people are promoting different applications for Android file transfer. Some even call these « automated », even though you have to define each source folder and each destination folder separately, as well as create the folder pairs manually. I don't really understand what would make this automatic, though. What I am looking for is similar to something I had in-built in my old Nokia Android a few years back: just push a button in the storage manager, and the system will transfer all images, videos, PDFs etc. to SD card automatically . What makes the situation rather troublesome at the moment, is the outright diaspora of files in my Android system. For example, you can find images from Downloads (general folder), Downloads (browser's folder), Downloads (WhatsApp / Telegram / Signal ... each one with a separate folder), Screenshots folder, Camera folder, different image editor applications' folders ... I think you get the point. Now, repeat the excercise with audio files and video files. Even my FoxIt PDF-reader has a stash of its own! Therefore, I am looking for something that could do the trick by a single click (no pun intended). Any suggestions would be welcome! Over and out.
