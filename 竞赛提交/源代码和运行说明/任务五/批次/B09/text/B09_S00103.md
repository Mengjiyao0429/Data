# The AI Application Spending Report

- 发布时间：2025-10-03
- 热度：HN 讨论（所在帖 0 分）

## 正文
I haven't heard of half of the listings so I asked Gemini (the irony..) to explain what each company offers: OpenAI: An AI research and deployment company known for its large language models like GPT-4, the chatbot ChatGPT, and the image generation model DALL-E. Anthropic: An AI safety and research company that builds reliable and interpretable AI systems. Its flagship product is the Claude family of large language models. Replit: An online, collaborative coding platform and IDE (Integrated Development Environment) that supports a wide range of programming languages. Freepik: A platform offering a vast library of stock photos, vector images, icons, and illustrations, which now includes AI-powered image generation tools. ElevenLabs: An AI voice technology company that provides realistic and versatile text-to-speech and voice cloning software. Cursor: An AI-first code editor designed to help developers write, edit, and debug code faster with deep AI integration. Fyxer.ai: A service providing AI-powered executive assistants to handle administrative, operational, and business tasks for professionals. Lorikeet: An AI platform for finance and accounting teams that automates bookkeeping, reconciliations, and financial workflows. Micro1: A platform that helps companies hire vetted, full-time remote software and AI engineers. Notion: An all-in-one workspace application that combines notes, documents, wikis, and project management. It includes Notion AI for content generation and summarization. Delve: An AI-powered competitive intelligence platform that automatically tracks and analyzes competitors' digital footprints. Perplexity: An AI-powered conversational search engine that delivers direct answers to questions with citations and sources included. Instantly: A cold outreach and email marketing platform designed for B2B sales and marketing campaigns, featuring email warm-up and sending automation. Customer.io: A customer engagement platform that enables businesses to send automated emails, push notifications, and SMS messages based on user behavior. Kling AI: A text-to-video AI model developed by Kuaishou Technology, capable of generating high-resolution, realistic video clips from text prompts. Retell AI: A developer platform providing an API to build human-like, conversational voice AI agents for customer service and other applications. Canva: A user-friendly online graphic design platform for creating social media graphics, presentations, posters, and other visual content. Its "Magic Studio" offers a suite of AI-powered design tools. Lovable: An AI platform that helps marketing teams generate and test ad creative, copy, and audience segments to optimize campaign performance. Metaview: An AI-powered tool that automatically transcribes and summarizes job interviews, providing notes and insights for hiring teams. Adept: An AI research and product lab building general intelligence systems designed to perform tasks and automate workflows on a computer using natural language commands. Glean: An AI-powered enterprise search platform that unifies and searches across all of a company's apps and knowledge bases to provide relevant answers. Photoroom: An AI photo editing app that allows users to remove backgrounds, erase objects, and create professional-looking product photos and portraits. Solve Intelligence: An AI platform for intellectual property professionals that automates patent search and analysis. Gamma: An AI-powered tool for creating engaging presentations, documents, and web pages from simple text prompts. Clay: A relationship management platform and "personal CRM" that centralizes contacts from various sources like email, calendar, and social media. Cluely: An AI-powered platform for user analytics and product insights, helping businesses understand user behavior and improve their products. Crosby: A personal AI assistant designed to integrate with a user's applications and data to manage tasks and information privately. Midjourney: An independent research lab and the creator of a popular AI program that generates complex and artistic images from natural language prompts. Combinely: An AI-powered platform that provides tools for content creation, image generation, coding assistance, and more. Merlin: An AI browser extension that provides users with access to ChatGPT and other language models on any website to summarize content, write replies, or generate ideas. Descript: An all-in-one audio and video editor that allows users to edit media by simply editing the text of its auto-generated transcript. Grammarly: An AI-powered writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, and tone across various platforms and applications. Manus: An AI platform that automates the process of contract review and negotiation for legal and sales teams. Cognition: An applied AI lab focused on reasoning. They are the creators of Devin, an AI agent designed to act as an autonomous software engineer. OpusClip: An AI video editing tool that automatically repurposes long videos into short, engaging clips suitable for social media platforms. Happy Scribe: A service that provides fast and accurate audio and video transcription and subtitling, using both AI and human transcribers. 11x: An AI-powered sales automation platform that helps B2B sales teams find leads, research accounts, and write personalized outreach emails. PLAUD: The company behind the PLAUD NOTE, an AI-powered voice recorder that can transcribe, summarize, and create mind maps from audio recordings. Serval: An AI platform for customer support teams that analyzes conversation data to provide insights, quality assurance, and agent coaching. Ada: An AI-native customer service automation platform that provides chatbot solutions to resolve customer inquiries across various channels. Otter.ai: An AI-powered service that provides real-time transcription for meetings and conversations, and generates automated summaries. Alma: A platform that helps people find and access affordable mental health care by connecting them with therapists who accept their insurance. Applaud: A modern HR and employee experience platform that helps companies manage their workforce, from onboarding to performance management. CapCut: A free, all-in-one video editing application for mobile and desktop, popular for creating videos for social media platforms like TikTok. Motion: An AI-powered productivity tool that combines a calendar, project manager, and meeting scheduler to automatically plan a user's day. Crisp: An all-in-one business messaging platform that includes features like a shared inbox, CRM, live chat, and chatbot for customer support and sales. Arcads: An AI-powered platform that helps e-commerce businesses and marketers quickly create and test video advertisements for social media. Emergent: An AI product studio and consultancy that designs and builds custom AI-powered solutions for businesses. Read AI: An AI tool that joins online meetings (on platforms like Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet) to provide automated summaries, transcripts, and analytics. Tavus: An AI video generation platform that allows users to record one video and then automatically create thousands of personalized versions with unique variables for each viewer.
