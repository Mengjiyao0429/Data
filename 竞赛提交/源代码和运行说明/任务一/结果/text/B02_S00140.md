# Is it worth it? (2021)

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46810447
- 发布时间：2026-01-29

## 正文
One of my first visible actions when working at a new company is to recommend the search software Voidtools Everything, and to set a 30 minute meeting to teach people how to use it. For the next 6-12 months I get so much positive feedback about all the time that is saved while navigating the network drives. I joke that the efficiency gains from its introduction organization-wide alone pay my salary!
