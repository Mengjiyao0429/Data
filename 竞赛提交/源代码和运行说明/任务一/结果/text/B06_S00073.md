It is a linguistic coordinate system that acts as software without code generation, predefined templates, or schemas. The Proof: I have 4 videos showing a primitive order management system (roles, CRUD, dynamic rules) built entirely from ~195 Simple Single Sentences . It runs on a 2010 AMD Phenom. To stress-test the logic, I ran an audit through Grok, which verified the architecture at 9.7/10 for logical soundness. Current status: Research is complete; hardening and feature development for a production-grade product are pending. The Verification (Live Demo): I do not have a hosted demo because I lack the "production plumbing" more required for public traffic as well as some feature gaps that needs to be implemented before a hosted demo . However, I am ready to jump on a live screen-share with any serious peer to verify that the functionality shown in the videos is real. I am not offering a "feature-on-demand" demo—just a live execution of the existing PoC to prove the substrate is functional . Roadmap: Live hosted demo: in 12-16 weeks. Alpha: A software builder that renders logic from natural language (no code generation/no predefined domain models--no class or schema defs/no back-end/front-end APIs). 10-12 months away. Fluency: Cognitive bots based on grammar + experience + psychology. Physical AI: A substrate designed for local execution (robotics/edge) rather than massive GPU clusters. The Ask: I am seeking a Survival Bridge ( note) to move this from my desktop to a production-grade infrastructure. I am also looking for co-founders with C/C++/Java/Golang backgrounds who have deep experience in building transactions, scalability, and persistence layers. I'm not looking for advice.
