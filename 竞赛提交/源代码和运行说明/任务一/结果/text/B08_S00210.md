# Conversational Surveys – The future of user research?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40934306
- 发布时间：2024-07-11
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I'm exploring an idea for a survey tool that uses conversational AI to conduct more dynamic, in-depth surveys. Instead of static questions, it would: 1. Based on the goal the model auto-generate questions 2. Allow users to respond in text or audio (like WhatsApp) 3. Use AI to analyze responses in real-time 4. Generate follow-up questions to dig deeper when initial answers lack depth The goal is to get the qualitative richness of a conversation, but at scale. Has anyone tried something similar? What challenges do you foresee? Would this be valuable for user research, customer feedback, or other use cases? Looking for validation, critiques, or any thoughts on this approach. Thanks!
