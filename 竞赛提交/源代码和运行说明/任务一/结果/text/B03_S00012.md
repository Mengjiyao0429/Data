## 正文
Hey everyone, we are currently a group of Berkeley students building a tool that watches how a team works, finds the repetitive tasks eating their time, shows what each one costs in hours, and then builds an agent to automate it. It runs locally with sensitive data redacted. We have a working MVP and are looking for a few early testers, starting with accounting firms but really applicable to all businesses. We've previously gotten top 10% of batches and are looking to push through to get real companies to implement out service. If anyone has any feedback or is willing to chat about this, feel free to let me know!
