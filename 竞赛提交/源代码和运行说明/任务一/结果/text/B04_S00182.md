# Ask HN: Will "indirect phones" replace today's phones?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44387449
- 发布时间：2025-06-26
- 热度：点赞1、评论9

## 正文
With an indirect phone, you’d never speak directly to anyone. Instead, you’d talk to an AI that serves as your communicator. When someone calls, the AI answers and later relays a summary of the message to you. You can ask the AI for more details about what the caller said. If you want to reply, you do so AI. For example: “Tell Joe that we can have a business meeting on Tuesday.” Do you think such indirect phones would become popular?
