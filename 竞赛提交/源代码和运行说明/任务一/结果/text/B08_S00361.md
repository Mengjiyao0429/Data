# Ask HN: What survey/poll service do you use?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12778110
- 发布时间：2016-10-24
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
I need to automate a newsletter with a poll. So I'm looking for a service with strong API and overall high quality. My needs are: - to receive parameters from poll's link (e.g. client id hash); - conditional branches in the poll; - pass all the data from the poll back to my service (preferably even unfinished polls) - mobile optimized version; - preferably some CSS or other styling customization. It seems that the market is oversaturated and it's hard to choose the right tool. I'd love to hear HN's opinion.
