# Ask HN: How do you imagine a website which shows live progress of anything?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17456210
- 发布时间：2018-07-04
- 热度：点赞2、评论1

## 正文
I'm a full-stack software engineer and recently I was thinking to work on a site where you could see live progress of community goals, a company's projects, national milestones, etc. Currently I just have a vague vision of how it feels. It is what people will look at every morning. It looks like a string of news/progress towards a special topic you want to follow. Do you want to see how Google is progressing towards their new product? Do you want see how AI, as an industry, is making progress and where we are now? Even as a manager or CEO you can see the progress of your projects, initiatives, milestones. I know there are many tools and technology that can achieve this, but I'm thinking of something exciting and really useful in every one everyday life. Imagine you have a magic wand, how do you want it to work? What do you like it to look like? What is the most exciting thing you want to see in this website?
