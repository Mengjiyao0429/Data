## 正文
I've seen a lot of people asking to roast their landing page on Reddit and IH and over time, I've realized how important, but also time-consuming it is to get landing page messaging correct. As a founder, you just want focus on building your product, and let the landing page do the selling. As such, my cofounder and I are working on a AI-powered A/B testing tool for SaaS landing page copy. To test out the AI model, and validate the value we can offer, for a limited time (and as much work we can take on) we offer our time and solution for free to analyze your landing page and provide you with a one-time report. You can sign up on our website and we'll get back to you with a free copy optimization report as soon as possible.
