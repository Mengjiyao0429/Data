## 正文
I have been following up on 3 things lately: 1- AI being able to work as a literal coworker. Hence AI software not being a tool but the work/service itself 2- User/customer acquisition costs rocketing 3- (partially related to #2) Rise of content marketing, especially in B2B Linkedin and B2C TikTok. Hence I have been thinking about building an app that can centralise your media and content generation and publishing for Twitter, Linkedin, TikTok and more. The unique thing about the UX design would be that it would not require you to log in, enter data and publish. Rather it would generate the posts and content by its own, based on your Slack messages or Linear/Jira tasks. Then it would send you an email or notification asking for permission to publish these. Just like a junior marketing manager you would hire. Full transparency: I am looking to invalidate this before building. Hence any harsh feedback is welcome as long as the intention is good.
