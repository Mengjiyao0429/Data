## 正文
This is an AI-powered wardrobe management application that helps users digitally organize their clothing and receive personalized outfit suggestions. Users can register, log in, and upload photos of their clothing items, categorizing each piece in their digital wardrobe. The application integrates with Claude 3.5 Sonnet AI to analyze clothing items and provide smart outfit recommendations. Take a Look and please give feedback! :\ Thankyou for your time. Features 1. Wardrobe Management: Upload, categorize, and manage your clothing items 2. AI-Powered Outfit Suggestions: Get personalized outfit recommendations using Claude 3.5 Sonnet AI 3. Token System: Manage outfit generation with a token-based system 4. Image Processing: Automatic compression and optimization of uploaded images 5. Saved Outfits: Save and manage your favorite outfit combinations Tech Stack 1. Backend: Python/Flask 2. Frontend: HTML, JavaScript, Bootstrap 3. Database: SQLAlchemy with PostgreSQL 4. Storage: AWS S3 for image storage 5. AI Integration: Claude 3.5 Sonnet API
