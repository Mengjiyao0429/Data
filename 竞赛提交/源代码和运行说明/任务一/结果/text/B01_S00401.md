## 正文
I'm the Co-founder of an AI/FinTech startup. All is well, going 4 months strong. But... Where can I go to get basic questions answered? From someone impartial, and trustworthy? Like... For questions on financing... do i need financing? Should i get it? Can I get it? How much would I expect to get? How much would I expect to offer? I'd like to ask someone and get a genuine opinion. And what about like... health insurance? lol Like... I've got this tingly in my arm lol. I'm thinking... "It'd be nice to have health/dental insurance rn" In the pre-seed phase, is financing just like... The founders trade equity for these benefits basically? So that they can keep working, but with basic benefits? Like... rn, we dont NEED more ppl. We just need time. And... it'd be nice to like... have basics covered without using some of the more time-consuming guerilla warfare tactics. And like... how far is one Engineer expected to be able to go? Like... at what point is there a genuine demand for more engineers? And... how do we know how well I'm performing? (I'm the "CTO" :) ) Like... at what point is there a genuine demand for more labor, vs im just inadequate? Like... is it expected that I build a full Consumer facing SaaS app, accepting payments, marketable, nice enough that you'd show random non-founder ppl? In what timeframe? Like... how "M" should the "MVP" be? But yeah, i keep having a lot of questions like that. idk who to turn to. I fear that talking to non-startup ppl will yield bad advice. And honestly, it usually gives off answers of pessimism and doubt, and a "dreams dont come true" mentality. Idk (m)any VC's sadly Yeah... like... idk wtf im doing basically lol. Where can i go to for help? (basically... is there a place LIKE YC's office hours?? but that doesnt cost equity or have that 1% admission barrier?) (also for context, we dont want to get financing unless we need it. we prefer to bootstrap. we genuinely believe we can get pretty far without financing and potentially bootstrap. but... again, idk how realistic this is either)
