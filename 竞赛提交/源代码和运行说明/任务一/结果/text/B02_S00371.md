# Ask HN: Advice on early-stage Hard-Tech funding requirements

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41430288
- 发布时间：2024-09-03
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Hi there – I'm looking for advice and opinions from those who have raised early-stage funding previously for a hard tech startup. For context, I’ve been working on humanoid robotics/ai for about 12 years now (I'm 30+ years as a software engineer) and finally after all these years, having made some break throughs in artificial muscles and subsequent innervation control, it’s time to start moving out of the R&D phase towards building a complete prototype. I am trying to engage with early-stage investors – and while I’ve done that previously with software startups, hard tech is vastly different in terms of investment thesis and I’m still unsure as to what I should be presenting? My questions are– 1 - What were the most important areas to cover in a hard tech deck (as opposed to a deck for a SaaS business for example) 2 - Did you have some key tech that you needed to demonstrate before closing the deal? If not, what was key to present and what was your strategy in doing it 3 - Did you address usage of funds by primarily defining milestones? I’m really interested in the twists and turns of previous hard tech founders – more on the non-technical side of how you went from the idea stages through to team building and product validation etc. I appreciate any insights people can share.
