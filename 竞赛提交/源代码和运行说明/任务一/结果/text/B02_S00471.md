Hi everyone, Hope you all doing well. I would like to introduce our new product, Allclues ( ), with you all. We've been working on it for 6+ months, and we hope you like it as much as we do! To give you some background, our goal is to empower individuals and teams to effortlessly organize, process, and collaborate with information in a seamless and efficient manner. In the past, we lacked this ability, but now with AI, we can liberate users from organizing folders and tags, losing clues between messages, and reading long and deep articles... Allclues wants to change the way people manage knowledge. In the web beta version, you will experience the game-changing features: Save with Ease - Allclues supports a wide range of content types. From web articles, PDFs, and newsletters to images and PDFs, you can capture anytime, anywhere with the Allclues browser extension. Clues AI will automatically process and categorize what you've saved. - Quick shortcut to save entire web pages or any texts, links, images… - Subscribe to newsletters custom Allclues email address - Quickly capture fleeting inspirations and find relevant connections - Automatically parsing saved web articles with Clues AI Ask Clues AI - Ask Clues AI to summarize articles, generate content, answer your questions, or guide you through complex concepts. Turn your reading into an interactive learning experience. - User-friendly chat-like dialogue system with contextual understanding - Interactive context understanding allows for querying selected text, full-page articles, and all collections within Allclues - Real-time saving of all chat information ensures seamless continuation of previous research - Seamless integration with all your Allclues knowledge improves accuracy as your usage accumulates over time - Ask questions to Clues AI through the extension on all webpages AI Report - Allclues can automatically generate insightful reports based on your collection. Cut through the noise and get a comprehensive view of your chosen topic. - Summarize a collection of content related to a similar topic, rather than just a single article - List the key points from all the articles, not missing the most important content - Conduct pros and cons analysis to help you rationally identify various perspectives - Link similar pages to create a knowledge map - Ask questions to help you confirm your understanding of the topic OmniMap - Visualize connections with OmniMap. See how concepts connect, overlap, and interact. With Allclues, you get a dynamically evolving knowledge landscape that grows with you. Smart Reader & Editor - Allclues provides a clean and intuitive interface for both reading and writing. Use the Markdown editor to articulate your thoughts, expand your ideas, or create your masterpiece. Allclues is not just a document tool, it is your creative partner. AI Search Engine - With deep search using natural language processing, you can find the information you need by searching for any question or keyword in your own own way. Clues AI is the solid backing of your memory. We will continue delivering an exceptional user experience, leveraging the power of AI to simplify knowledge organization, streamline collaboration, and boost personal and team productivity.
