# AskHN: What advice do you have for an ADHD,information hoarding, Researcher

- 发布时间：2023-01-13

## 正文
I’m a Ph.D student who’s also suffering from ADHD. I come across loads of research papers, books, articles, lecture notes, video lectures, audiobooks, podcasts, media , links and everything that interests me. I love to learn and most of the stuff I find are extremely interesting/useful which I either have some use, an interest, or want to consume someday at least. I also write copious notes both digitally and on notebooks. I write blog posts but leave them half completed, only to start over again and use the earlier one to use as writing material for a fresh draft. What this has resulted in is unsurprisingly an overwhelming number of stuff to consume and work upon both in terms of work - interesting research papers in my field and excellent sources for everything that I am unable to delete, for i definitely know that I want to consume them someday, even if they aren’t a priority at the moment. So if I have a day off, there are just too many options and I waste all energy on analysis paralysis just to choose what to enjoy working on. Ideally I would want a system where I know everything is, like a file Wikipedia like system. I have tried so many tools and left so many that it creates even more duplication. For example, having first tried notion, then shifting to obsidian then logseq etc that sometimes I forget what are in my vaults and rewriting notes from the source in a different app again. Another example being calibre for e-books but the same books on the Apple Books app, my kindle and in my pdf cloud storage. Similarly research papers within Zotero and in my PDF cloud storage. Trying apps like hazel, notion, obsidian, DEVONthink, Zotero, raindrop, everything tool, sync thing. It doesn’t help that I have multiple devices- a windows laptop, an iPhone. This caused usage of multiple cloud systems iCloud,OneDrive, yandex(for Zotero WebDAV). The first thing I did was just dump all of my files into a single folder and created just a few sub folders for the kind of file. Subfolders beyond a certain level was the worst pain for what I thought was an organising exercise. What all this results in is that I have difficulty prioritising what to do, when to do it, find stuff and keep it sustainable. I just need to structure a system like a reference for everything where I just know where everything is, and non redundant. Would love to hear some strategies and tips. The reason I can’t continue with a single folder dump is that it limits discovery of what can I do and has high search effort.
