## 正文
One of the biggest blockers for AI agents is the inability to pay for services programmatically. We build an end-to-end demo for AI agents showing how to solve that. It has two components: - A simple API that provides stock price information, paywalled with the L402 protocol - An AI Agent that autonomously pay for and consume the API, performing complex tasks like comparing stock prices In just a few lines of code, this demo highlights the power of L402 for enabling AI agents to handle payments and unlock new possibilities Want to learn more or join the discussion?
