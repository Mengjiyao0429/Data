## 正文
Hi everyone! I’m working on a backend platform to help developers build AI-driven agents and apps by simplifying access to structured business data. Key features: • Semantic Search API: Query business data with natural language (e.g., “Find properties under $500k in New York”). • OAuth Integration: Authenticate users to access personalized data like saved searches or preferences. • Custom Interactions: Send messages, subscribe to updates, or trigger workflows like bookings or notifications. • Tool Integrations: Connect with platforms like Zapier or Make for automation. We’re starting with real estate but plan to expand to e-commerce, travel, and more. Questions for you: 1. Does this solve problems you face as a developer? 2. What data or integrations would be essential for you? Your feedback will help shape the MVP—thanks in advance!
