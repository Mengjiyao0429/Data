# Seeking Feedback on a New Startup Idea

## 正文
I’m in the early stages of developing a startup in the B2B sector. I have several ideas that could potentially solve real-world problems, but before I write a single line of code, I want to ensure these ideas resonate with you.Here’s where it gets interesting: I haven’t written a single line of code yet. Yes, you read that right! I’m putting validation before development because your feedback is that important. It’s not just about creating a product; it’s about creating a solution that truly makes a difference.So here are my ideas: 1.AI-powered Social Media Analyzer:Develop a Chrome extension that utilizes AI to analyze social media data and provide marketers with insightful information about their target audience, engagement rates,post scheduling, engagement tracking, and sentiment analysis and trends. This extension can help marketers optimize their social media strategies and improve their online presence. 2.The idea is to create a comprehensive Twitter growth like Twitter Hunter and content generation tool that uses AI to provide daily personalized tweet suggestions, predict tweet performance, schedule tweets, offer automations, and provide analytics & insights. I invite you to share your thoughts on this idea. Your feedback could be the deciding factor in whether this idea comes to life or goes back to the drawing board.Specifically, I’m interested in: Relevance: Do you see a need for these products/services in your business? Why or why not? Pain Points: What challenges or frustrations do you currently face in your business that these ideas might address? Improvements: How could these ideas be improved to better meet your needs or solve your problems? : Based on your experience in the B2B sector, do you think these are good ideas to pursue? Why or why not? Your honest feedback is crucial at this stage of my startup journey. Thank you in advance for taking the time to help!
