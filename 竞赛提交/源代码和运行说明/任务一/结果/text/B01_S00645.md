# Ask HN: How do you find and read academic papers?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=14570149
- 发布时间：2017-06-16
- 热度：点赞1、评论2

## 正文
I'm curious to find out how those of you who read academic papers (either professionally or recreationally) consume the plethora of available data sources. Do you use Google Scholar and view the PDF/PS document using a normal document viewer? Do you browse arXiv regularly and see what new research has been published? Finally, would you use a simple tool for finding, reading and collating papers from the various sources? Would an automatic "reference flow" help, so that you can easily click through long chains of references and see how they interconnect? Would you pay for such a tool?
