There are a lot of tools in the toolbox, but a tool is not a solution. Again, the problem is not technology. You won't solve for this with software. I also don't think you are looking at the problem. You are looking at the symptoms. Ibuprofen will decrease inflammation, but it won't fix the internal bleeding or broken bone causing the inflammation. Missing data between teams is a symptom of deeper problems. You have to fix the people/organization and technology can help a little bit. This is what external accounting teams do when they go on site to a client location. They provide an organizational business solution that can be coached and not a balance sheet.
