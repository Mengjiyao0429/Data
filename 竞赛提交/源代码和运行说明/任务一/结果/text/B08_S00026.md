If your team is hiring and conducting interviews, I urge you to consider ScreenStack: an all-in-one interview platform that replaces your fragmented stack of tools. It was built by engineers who have spent years at FAANG, conducted over 400 hours of interviews, and felt the pain firsthand: juggling Zoom, CoderPad, Excalidraw, and an AI notetaker, then burning hours stitching them together to reach a decision.
