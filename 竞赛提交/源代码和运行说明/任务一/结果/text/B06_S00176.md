## 正文
My name is Carl and for about 18 months I have been working on an web app. It's an app that aims to be a sort of agentic / automated editor. For now, it handles 40 images tools, but soon there will be more types such as text and video tools. The agentic capabilities are limited for now, but at this point the app can chain actions, choose and update tools. The aim is to simplify the users tasks while being able to have a manual control over the app. The system of tools has been made so that it can be used by other apps and controlled by an AI. The project has taken much more time than I had planned and now that I'm entering the marketing step. I'm about to run out of funds. There are about 400 free users. But not knowing how much time it will take to get enough paid users to make the project sustainable, I'm reaching out to you guys. I'm open to solutions to sale better or else. Or to potential collaboration or investment. Or even to bring the tech to a project or to another startup that could benefit from this.
