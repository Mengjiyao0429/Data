Procurement leaders agree that software procurement can be a tedious process. Contract reviews can take weeks, multiple sales pitches, various renewal deadlines and complicated pricing structures. Things can get messy, but what if you had a single software vendor for your entire tech stack? Procufly is doing exactly that - we have partnered with 80+ leading software tools to provide a unified procurement service under one contract, one monthly invoice and a single point of contact. The team will help you purchase and manage software on a discounted price, as well as provide support and installation - all free of charge! Well, what’s the catch? Software tools provide commissions, but we decided to split the revenue with our clients - so you can purchase software AND receive cash back.
