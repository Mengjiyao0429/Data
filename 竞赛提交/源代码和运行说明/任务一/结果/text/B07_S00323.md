# Ask HN: Evaluation/promotion depends not on my past performance?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=14815799
- 发布时间：2017-07-20
- 热度：点赞1、评论4

## 正文
I am wondering what to think of my current company in this situation: I am a data scientist, working for about 19 months at bigcorp since graduating with a masters in AI. Past 6 months, I have been working to get a promotion. Manager and I have set a few benchmarks, and I feel I meet all the criteria. Manager and colleagues say good things about my performance. Fast forward to now. I will move from a business department to an IT department, still be a data scientist there and do basically the same things. (creating ml solutions and bringing them to production) Manager doesn't want to give me the promotion until I have spoken with the general IT manager and my future, lower IT manager, which will be selected next month. They have to approve my promotion, taking input from my current manager and IT colleagues I work with. The thing is, both my manager and his manager say that my skills meet all the criteria for a promotion - that my performance has been good. But I need to wait for a conversation with a yet to be chosen manager at IT to get the promotion. The reason for this is to "ensure harmony within IT" and to make sure that my promotion "fits the context in which I operate within the company". I have protested that I should be reviewed based on my past performance and market value, not my future context, but they have simply refused this. What should I think of this? What would you do?
