# Ask HN: I will automate your processes for free

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=18265529
- 发布时间：2018-10-20
- 热度：点赞3、评论6

## 正文
Share with me 1-3 processes related to the web you do regularly, which you find tedious and would like to automate. I will only pick a handful of projects right now to allow myself to deliver the best value to them. My preference goes to use cases that tick the most boxes of these: - Very repetitive and/or high value to you - Well defined processes (you can write down the rules / flowchart of what you do precisely, no human judgement should be involved) - Low in complexity - Realistic automations - no AI or ML involved - No internal systems (for now) Please express your wishes (things with the highest impact) , and besides the realistic aspect, do not feel restricted to my preferences. Example use cases: - Synchronize Asana to Google Calendar - Backup data from Airtable or perform some actions based on it - Consolidate data from several sources such as your CRM, your analytics, your Medium statistics and whatnot to a single location such as a spreadsheet or even into a Rest API - Setting up custom alerts based on tweets (if tweet from user X or contains keyword Y and has a positive sentiment send me an email) - Setup follow up rules for emails such as: If user didn't login for 10 days send them a reminder / survey - Create your own push notification system, and receive custom events (I can hook you up to webhooks or other systems) - Setup a basic slackbot that triggers some API call for example, or receive messages in slack from an arbitrary source - Watch news sources or job boards for keywords or more complex rules, and do whatever you want (receive a notification, store it in the database ..) - Scrape a website (you'll need to bring your own captcha service if it's required) - Automatically post for you in several platforms using one submission Thank you for your time, hope this will lead to great collaborations :-)
