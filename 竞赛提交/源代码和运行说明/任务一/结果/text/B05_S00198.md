I'm looking to explore the latest technology for performing audio analysis on the web, both on the frontend and backend. Specifically, I'm interested in open-source solutions that can: Extract stems from audio files Identify genres or moods Separate different tracks or elements (e.g., vocals, instruments) Analyze and generate metadata or insights from audio files The goal is to automate and enhance the experience for users who upload their music or audio files by extracting useful information that can help them optimize their content. Are there any cutting-edge tools, libraries, or frameworks in the open-source space that you would recommend? Features like automated mix feedback, real-time audio enhancements, or even AI-driven suggestions could be really impactful. Thanks in advance!
