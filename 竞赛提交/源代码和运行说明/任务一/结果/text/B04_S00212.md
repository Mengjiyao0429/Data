## 正文
Hey HN! I’m currently building SubTrackr, a lightweight subscription tracking SaaS designed for small businesses. The idea is to help companies monitor, manage, and optimize their recurring expenses by connecting bank accounts, categorizing subscriptions, and providing actionable insights. A bit about me: I’ve been diving into entrepreneurship and SaaS development, and this is my first SaaS project. I run an e-commerce business (Onyx Vault), have experience with dropshipping and finance, and I’m now looking to build a simple but useful tool that solves a real problem. Unlike Rocket Money, which focuses on individuals, SubTrackr is tailored for businesses—helping them track software subscriptions, SaaS expenses, and recurring costs with automated reminders and integrations. Would love to get your feedback! What pain points do you have when managing subscriptions for your business? What features would make a tool like this essential for you? Excited to hear your thoughts!
