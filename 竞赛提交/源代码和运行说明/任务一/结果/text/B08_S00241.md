We are working on a managed service ( ) that aims to make it much easier to utilise graph databases in software projects (apps, services, automations). Think of it like Airtable or Google Firestore but for knowledge graphs. Our understanding is that graph databases can 'liberate' a technical team from the restrictions of relational databases (particularly when representing and querying entities with complex/nuanced connections) and the arbitrariness that can result with no-code databases.
