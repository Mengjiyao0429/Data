# AI use leads UC Berkeley math professor to see 'no point' in grading homework

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50236973
- 发布时间：2026-10-25

## 正文
The machine can't do your work. It can automate some of it, but the work isn't done until a human has verified it, and may need to fix it, modify it, etc. That's why we still teach. And we must verify people learned, through tests, practice, etc. Don't believe the crazy lies that AI can replace your job. AI can't replace anyone's job, because it lies, cheats, hallucinates, can't learn from its mistakes, and can't be held liable.
