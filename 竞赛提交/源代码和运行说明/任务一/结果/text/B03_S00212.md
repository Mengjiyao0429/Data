Hey Guys, My friend and I built AI tool that detects urgent emails and sends you notification with suggestions on how to reply. The idea came from a real problem: we get so many emails every day that important ones can easily get buried or overlooked. This tool helps by filtering out the clutter and highlighting what really matters. What makes it different from other tools is that it understands industry-specific terms and filters out spam, newsletters, and low-priority stuff—so you only see what really matters. A bit of background: I used to work in international logistics and would get 250–300 emails a day. Missing even one critical message could cause serious issues. So I asked a friend to help me build something to make life easier—and this is what we came up with. We’re now looking for early users. It’s completely free and super quick to set up.
