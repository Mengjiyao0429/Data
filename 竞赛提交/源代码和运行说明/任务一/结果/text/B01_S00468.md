- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36871820
- 发布时间：2023-07-26

## 正文
Relevant to this is an indie movie called Artifice Girl, the premise of the movie is a genius 3D artist and software developer creates an AI to catch online predators, part of the tool involves generating realistic images to use as lure and using an LLM to chat with the predators and get enough information to get them to jail.
