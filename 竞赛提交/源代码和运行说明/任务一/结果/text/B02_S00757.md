# Recommend a Tool to Host Contracts and Send Notification

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7588107
- 发布时间：2014-04-14
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
I work for a medium size firm that operates within the Healthcare construction industry. We provide both manpower and software to our clients. However, while we subscribe to a well-known web tool to host time-sheets and process invoices, we are in need of a tool that could host our different project contract(s) and amendments, with the capability to send notification(s) to stakeholder(s) when the contract(s) is coming due or expiring. Any idea(s) please?
