## 正文
I'm looking for a tool that periodically reads the content of my clipboard, scans it for any links/URLs found and saves those as some kind of bookmarks. I often need to extract and store multiple URLs from longer texts. The best workflow for me would be just to copy those texts into the clipboard and have a tool that watches the clipboard for changes and than extracts all the URLs it can find. Currently, I'm using jddownloader for that task, but this is clunky, slow and way overkill to just grab and store some links. So I'm looking for a more lightweight alternative. I'm currently running debian with xfce 4.18
