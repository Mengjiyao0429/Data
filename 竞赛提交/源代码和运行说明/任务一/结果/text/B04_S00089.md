## 正文
SMBs and mid-market companies ($10M-$200M revenue) spend 40-60% of finance time on manual data entry, invoice management and basic reconciliation. We built FinCrew AI to automate this entirely. FinCrew is an AI CFO platform with multiple specialized agents (Data Ingestion, Transaction Recording, Invoice Management, Fraud Detection, CFO Orchestrator) using multi-agent orchestration with anti-hallucination safeguards. Existing solutions don't serve the mid-market segment. They're either SMB-focused or enterprise-only. We're targeting the $150B+ TAM in between.
