# Ask HN: Thoughts on a Patreon-Style Crowdfunded Dating App?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41020265
- 发布时间：2024-07-20
- 热度：点赞2、评论2

## 正文
Hi HN community, I recently launched a unique dating app that's gaining traction, especially in Africa. My app features video-based profiles and an AI chatbot assistant for user onboarding, enhancing the user experience right from the start. One idea I was considering is incorporating a Patreon-style crowdfunding model. This could allow users to support the app's development and access premium features, helping sustain and grow the platform while keeping it accessible and free to use. Given our current success (most of our users are women, and the app is going viral in parts of Africa), I'd love to get your thoughts on this crowdfunding approach. * Do you think a Patreon-style model makes sense for a dating app? * What potential benefits or challenges do you foresee? * How would you feel about supporting an app like this through crowdfunding? Looking forward to your insights and feedback and if you’d like to try the app it's available on both the App Store and Google Play: Thanks!
