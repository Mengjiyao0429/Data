# Software Patents – what was your experience?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31037941
- 发布时间：2022-04-15
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
I have a couple of ideas around software that (I think) are worthy of patenting. I want to know more about real-world examples of people, especially regular salaried software developers like me, who have successfully filed patents for themselves (not on behalf of and paid for by the organizations they work for). I have only heard anecdotally that software patents are particularly tough to file and even more tough (and expensive) to defend. Some of that anecdotal knowledge about software patents I have is: - They're difficult to file and get approved. - Expensive to defend (as would be any patent, I'd imagine?) - I have heard that personal notes and diagrams regarding the idea can serve as a basis for the patent filing application. Questions: - What has your experience been like? What would you advise a novice with plenty of software experience? - Did you file in multiple countries to make it robust? - What other pertinent questions should I be asking? I'm not particularly rich but have fairly good savings. I am in Australia working as a software developer. Thanks!
