## 正文
Hi HN, Woody Wang here. For the past 3 months, I've been building workflowhub.ai, a platform I wanted to share with you all today. I've noticed many talented people creating incredibly useful automation workflows with tools like n8n, Dify, and comfyUI, but they often sit on hard drives or are only shared informally. Existing marketplaces feel too general. So, I built workflowhub.ai – a dedicated space for buying and selling these specific workflow files. Our core promise to creators is a 90% profit share. We want to help these underserved creators turn their expertise into a revenue stream We're looking for people who build these automations – consultants, internal experts, hobbyists – who aren't currently selling them or find other platforms unsuitable. I'd love to get your feedback on the platform once launched, our model, and especially hear from anyone who creates these kinds of workflows. What would make this valuable to you as a seller?
