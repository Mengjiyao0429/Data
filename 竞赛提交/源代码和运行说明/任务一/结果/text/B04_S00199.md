Hey! I'm building an AI voice journal app to clear your head to make your life faster! I often think faster than I type, and traditional note-taking slows me down. So I'm working on Voice Notes — a lightweight journaling app powered by AI: - Open the app → hit record → speak your mind - It transcribes & summarizes your notes (digest + action points) - All entries are saved in a clean, timeline-style feed It’s still in early beta, but already part of my daily workflow.
