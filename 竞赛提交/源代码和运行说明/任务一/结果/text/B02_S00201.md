## 正文
I am looking for an AI model or app which fixes the casing in a sentence, e.g. if it's written in all lower-case or SHOUTING CASE, it would just give me the correct case. Sure, LLMs can probably do that. I am looking for a more specialized model. My hope is that it would be much smaller in size, so I can easily run it on my own hardware, maybe even on a phone. A minimum answer would do it for English. Next I would need German, then French, Spanish and Italian. The final size expectation would be <100 MB (I hope that's realistic for the task). Right now I'd be willing to accept a 2 GB model as well. Also, non-AI solutions would do. However, I don't want typical grammar and spelling correction like Word has. I want to input a sentence and out goes a corrected sentence. No human interaction.
