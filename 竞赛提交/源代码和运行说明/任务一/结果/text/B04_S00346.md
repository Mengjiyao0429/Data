- 来源：Hacker News

- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
I recently learnt about NYC Local Law 144 ( ), which will be enforced beginning July 5, 2023. The law governs the use of an automated employment decisions tool (AEDT). Here is what an AEDT is: AEDTs are broadly defined as “any computational process, derived from machine learning, statistical modelling, data analytics, or artificial intelligence [2], that issues [a] simplified output [3], including a score, classification, or recommendation, that is used to substantially assist or replace discretionary decision making [4] for making employment decisions that impact natural persons.” Would anyone consider that their company is using an AEDT at the moment? Is anyone tracking this law and is doing anything proactive to prepare for it?
