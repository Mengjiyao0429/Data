## 正文
CTO / Co-founder We are a pre-funded stage start-up in the law tech space. Firms in this space have had liquidity events approximately 2 years after launching. We have done the work to understand the need for our product, its market opportunity and what customers need and want. We are looking to roll out an initial version of the product in the next four months and are in need of a technical co-founder willing to work on an equity-only basis. We have a design consultant and parts of the application prototyped. We foresee being able to raise an initial round of funding towards the end of this year. This is a C-level position and is a sole contributor role to start with. The individual will work closely with the co-founders to understand the workflows, data flows and customer needs and then develop and deliver the software, driving the overall engineering and product development strategy. Responsibilities: • As co-founder, identify technological opportunities and risks for delivering the company’s services as a web-based business • Evaluate and identify appropriate technology platforms (including web application frameworks and the deployment stack) for delivering the company’s services • Bring product to market Requirements: • Minimum 5 years of experience developing web/mobile applications and experience in leading developers (both employees and consulting resources) • Experience with and love of start-up companies • Ruby on rails • CSS, HTML5, one or more Javascript frameworks • Knowledge of web standards • Experience with UNIX system administration and web server configuration • Database design skills and experience with NoSQL • Good knowledge OOP, design principles and patterns • Agile project management skills We will discuss the details of our service in greater detail during the in-person interview. Please submit a cover letter and resume, plus any links to previous projects / portfolio, to iwantthisjob22 .com.
