## 正文
Creators of Slack Look at all the stupid stuff people are posting on social media, there is no need to broadcast such private and personal things to the entire world. Oh wait! Let’s capitalize on this and create a software for the workplace where people will post on internal public forums, what they are working on so that they may be able to get feedback, kudos, constructive criticism, and at the same time serve as knowledge base for the entire company Users of Slack Hey you three, gather around here. I want to discuss this really important thing I’m doing that I’m pretty sure no one else within the company has done or would care about.
