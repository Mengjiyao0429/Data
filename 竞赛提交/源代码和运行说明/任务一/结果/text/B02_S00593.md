# Ask HN: Simple task/project management tool for freelancers?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26101904
- 发布时间：2021-02-11
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
I'm a freelance consultant & developer working for approx. 30-40 clients since many years. Typically, 2-3 "bigger active projects" run in parallel to spontaneously occurring tasks (small or very urgent). I found excellent solutions for communication, time tracking, invoicing, etc. but still search for a simple but effective solution for task / project management, especially with a focus on resource (my time) planning. For me, project management tools target teams and feel – at least for my requirements – too extensive and complex. Pure task managers lack in resource planning features. Does anyone know or even use a system fulfilling similar requirements, especially suitable for freelancers without a team?
