## 正文
I am currently a QA intern at a small software development firm. For the past 2 months all my coworkers and managers have been talking about how we need to be an "AI-centric" company. I've been encouraged to explore AI based solutions for our QA work. I've tried a few libraries to automate test writing and some E2E tests, but I've found them to be pretty useless and more of a hype tool. Unfortunately, I am still hearing about finding ways to innovate my work with AI and am unsure what else I can do or try. So I am curious, how have you guys went about integrating AI in your QA work if it all?
