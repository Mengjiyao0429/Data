# Wero – Digital payment wallet, made in Europe

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47040343
- 发布时间：2026-02-16

## 正文
There's no need for a worldwide solution, federation and interoperability between major schemes are enough. The same way AliPay can be used in most Asian countries already, we can imagine a world where EPI, UPI, Pix and so on interoperate smoothly. I assume Visa and MC will try to remain walled off as the US are a big and slow mover in this space, until they'll need to open up as well. Note: payments (unlike transfers) are never really zero fees, for good reasons.
