# Ask HN: Has anyone built a simple habit app that works?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44863646
- 发布时间：2025-08-11
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
I’m an indie developer planning to build an app to help people build good habits—consistently and with as little friction as possible. What got me started was hitting a wall myself. About six months ago, I began my solo founder journey. The first couple of months were awesome—I was energized and productive. But by month three, things started slipping. I got distracted, procrastinated, and stuck in my own head. I tried everything to get back on track: * Working at the same time and place to cut down on decision fatigue * Breaking tasks into tiny steps and checking each one off to build momentum * Using Pomodoro sessions with little rewards like stretching or a sip of water to stay focused * Regular workouts to keep my energy and mood steady * Most importantly: shifting from relying on willpower to building systems I realized that unless actions become automatic, it’s tough to keep going long term. It took me about two months to rebuild a solid daily rhythm. These small habit tweaks made a huge difference—more focus, more clarity, and way better output. Now, I’m turning that low-friction, sustainable, anti-procrastination system into a simple, practical app. No fluff, no hype—just one clear goal: make consistency easier. Do you have a similar need? Would you use a tool like this? How much would you pay monthly for a stable version? Any features you’d want to see?
