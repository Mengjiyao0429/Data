## 正文
ChatGPT is interesting. It’s a decent utility for my work, which is networking and distributed systems stuff. However, I am not particularly interesting in a career in AI or ML. Does that mentality seem detrimental? I wonder if people who used to be expert Fortran developers or COBOL developers felt the same when new technologies started coming out like Java, the web and C++.
