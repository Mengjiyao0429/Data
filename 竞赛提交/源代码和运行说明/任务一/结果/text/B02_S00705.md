# Ask HN: What do you wish someone would build?

## 正文
Looking to build something really productive and useful as I invest time into learning additional technologies. My skills are available to HN members. I'm able and willing to build pretty much anything along the lines of B2C or B2B tools (therefore excluding compilers, database engines, or operating system, and the like). What do wish someone would build for you? The emphasis is on something that solves a practical problem for you, not just interesting. I will select a wish that appeals to me, and give free invites to HN members when it is done.
