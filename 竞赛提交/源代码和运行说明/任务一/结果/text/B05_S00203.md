# Solving a good issue to make life efficient

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41264769
- 发布时间：2024-08-16
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
We all have limited time and energy every day, so we all desire to make these most productive. The ways we have for these are either to drop some non-important work and put all energy towards important things that matter, or the second way is to reduce inefficiencies. As I don't know what's important for someone, and what's not I decided to make life efficient through other ways. So, for this, I picked a problem, which is very known to anyone who has done business. As Customers are the oxygen of any business, contacting, and following up on them is like a tedious cycle that can't be stopped. As we all know, when a business scale, so does its user base, for which there is a very manual way of calling them. This is something which hasn't changed for a long. Still, a business needs to have a team of people, who constantly spend hours on customers, conveying that same information again and again, contacting them for little things. As I thought, I could try to solve this, I am now working to create an AI call Agent, that can contact thousands of customers, based on some basic info you provide. I want it to be the most smooth talker, which does not speak, but listens, questions, understands, and then replies. Also after the call it will provide a good concise summary of the call, provide good analytics of each call, and also give recommendations about which calls a follow up and get a lead, so if a human wants, he then also contact that specific users, saving a huge chunk of time. As this is gonna be a big project, upon which I am working, I need some help towards it. So, if you know full-stack or ML, please contact me. Also, it would be great to hear from everyone about suggestions and advice. Thanks for reading!!!
