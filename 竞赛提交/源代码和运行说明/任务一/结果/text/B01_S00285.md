The Problem: Why Traditional Hiring is Broken Recruitment is still a slow, manual, and inefficient process for many companies. HR teams struggle with: High-volume applications with low-quality filtering. Fragmented hiring workflows across spreadsheets and emails. Poor collaboration between recruiters, hiring managers, and interviewers. Lack of data insights, making recruitment decisions reactive rather than strategic. The Solution: AI-Driven Applicant Tracking An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) solves these inefficiencies by automating candidate sourcing, resume screening, interview scheduling, and recruitment analytics. MokaHR is a modern, AI-powered ATS that helps companies scale hiring through: Automated resume parsing & intelligent ranking to filter the best candidates. Customizable hiring workflows that adapt to different recruitment needs. Data-driven insights on hiring efficiency, time-to-fill, and candidate drop-off. WeChat & mobile integration for a seamless hiring experience in China. Why MokaHR? Unlike legacy ATS platforms like Workday or Taleo, MokaHR is: Designed for speed → Minimal learning curve, quick implementation. AI-enhanced → Smart recommendations reduce manual screening. Optimized for China → Local job board integration, compliance support, WeChat hiring. Built for collaboration → Real-time feedback loops between HR, hiring managers, and recruiters. Who is Using It? MokaHR is trusted by fast-scaling startups, mid-sized companies, and enterprises that need a modern hiring system. Whether you’re hiring 10 or 10,000 employees, optimizing the recruitment funnel is critical. Hiring is a competitive advantage. If you’re still relying on outdated tools, you’re already behind.
