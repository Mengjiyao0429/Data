# Ask HN: What high quality paid (non-subscription) software would you recommend?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37426910
- 发布时间：2023-09-07
- 热度：点赞2、评论1

## 正文
More and more software is moving into a subscription model. I'm looking for high quality suggestions for software that you can buy and fully own without having to keep paying for a license/subscription. I'm fine with software where you eventually have to pay for a new version - if the new version has genuinely compelling features and the old version continues to work. Good examples that I personally paid for and keep using: - Sublime Text - GoodNotes (iOS app) - Pycharm (sort of qualifies) - Strongbox (offers a lifetime license) Anything else that people really enjoy and they feel like they got their money's worth?
