## 正文
Hey HN, I’ve been working on a project for marketing teams and writers who feel the pain of producing SEO-ready content consistently. The problem I kept hearing: coming up with topics takes time doing keyword + entity research takes even more time writing a full piece in a consistent voice takes hours and scaling this reliably is basically impossible without a full team So I built a workflow that turns a topic (or a cluster of topics) into a fully SEO-optimized article draft in minutes — including: topical research SERP entity mapping semantic structure brand-voice alignment internal-link suggestions batch generation for entire content calendars I made this originally for agencies, but it ended up helping solo bloggers and founders just as much. If anyone here regularly publishes blog posts, I’d genuinely love to hear what parts of your writing workflow feel the most painful or repetitive. I’m looking to learn from real users, and I’m giving out early access to anyone who wants to test this. Just lmk I’m especially curious about: how you validate topics what your research process looks like any tools you’ve automated with what “SEO-friendly” means to you whether batch creation would actually help you or not If you want to check out the tool/demo or get access, let me know — happy to share more details. Thanks for reading, and open to any feedback from the builders/writers here. — Trav @ Firekind.io
