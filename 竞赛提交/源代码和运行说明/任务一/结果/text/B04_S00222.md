## 正文
"2048 Cupcakes" isn't just about classic gameplay—it also introduces exciting new features. Feeling stuck? No worries! The game comes with a smart AI assistant. In “AI Help” mode, it suggests the best next move in real time. Want an even easier experience? Switch to “AI Auto”, and the AI will play for you with optimal strategies—perfect for learning pro techniques while you watch! Plus, each game includes 10 undo chances, giving you extra flexibility to go for that high score.
