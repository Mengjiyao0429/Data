# Ask HN: How do you promote apps which are vibe coded but has real life usecase?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47677971
- 发布时间：2026-04-07
- 热度：点赞3、评论1

## 正文
I am quite keen on developer tools, built some tools for developer productivity. But when I post about the app on the reddit (or other place), I don't get interaction instead getting more harsh comments about being vibe coded. In some cases, people are not even opening my app's website, rather just commenting like a bot about being vibe coded or having text using LLM. It was not like that, 2 years ago, any packages I created (more simple but useful), I posted on reddit and people shared their feedbacks, but now I am not getting reach or useful feedbacks. Even though I am posting on the same sub-reddits/channels, some channel is even deleting my post by saying AI slop. Most of my work is open source, and I am not trying to sell them. I am curious, how people are trying to build a community around a product or project, how you are promoting apps/project which are clearly vibe coded and but really useful or solving some real problems (maybe even a startup can be built around that). Note that, I am an experienced developer, who is utilising LLM to ship and solve system faster and having problems on making people understand the value of the project rather the code itself.
