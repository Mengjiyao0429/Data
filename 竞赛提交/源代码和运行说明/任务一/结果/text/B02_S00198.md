## 正文
We’ve been building Dromos Console, a post-canvas way to create personalized automation agents. Instead of drag-and-drop flows or if/else trees, you say the outcome once in plain English and publish a working agent in ~10 minutes. Preview: How it works (concrete flow) Say it once — e.g., “Reconcile invoices from Gmail + Drive, flag mismatches, update the ledger Sheet, and notify Slack.” Once this is done you publish — the platform chooses tools, applies guardrails/policies, enables audit, and you have a ready agent you can work with, and ask for different outputs.
