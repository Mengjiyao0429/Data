# Ask HN: Why don't parents impart their knowledge to an AI for their children?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35440524
- 发布时间：2023-04-04
- 热度：点赞2、评论15

## 正文
Children probably don't want to hear endless and boring details about what they should do when their parents die. For example, how should they make funeral arrangements, continue to pay bills, maintain the house, etc.? But this knowledge could be imparted to an AI that their children would access after their parents die.
