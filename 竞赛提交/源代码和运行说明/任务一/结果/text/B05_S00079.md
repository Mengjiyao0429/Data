# What I want from AI – two specific use cases for a Home Assistant

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46958274
- 发布时间：2026-02-10
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
The household chores tracker : - I want some sort of house cleaning/family chores assistant device that I can talk to. - It should live in the kitchen - I do not want another forgotten phone app. - It must remember conversations long term and be able to return to exactly that conversation when I come back to it. - I want to be able to talk about the housework. - I want to tell it randomly what chores needs to be done as I think of them "we need to sweep the floors twice a week". - Every member of the household does chores after dinner for 10 minutes. - I want it to tell each person what needs to be done, ensuring there is an orderly rotation of chores done and nothing is forgotten. - I want it to record what has been done thus it can keep track of what needs to be done next. The study assistant - this would be incredibly valuable and useful if it worked well: - This might be a dedicated device or possibly a phone app though I feel like a dedicated device is likely to be used more, rather than yet another forgotten phone app. - I want a homework/study assistant which retains a long memory of what I am studying. - It tracks what study i need to do. - It tracks the homework I have due. - It tracks the exams I have coming up. - It organises my study routines and tells me what I need to do when. - It manages spaced repetition learning.
