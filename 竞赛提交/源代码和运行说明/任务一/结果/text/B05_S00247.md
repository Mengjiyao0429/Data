# SageBrush: AI Painter – the simplest way to create with AI

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=38788420
- 发布时间：2023-12-28

## 正文
Hi, everyone! Been a long time since I've launched something on Hacker News. Gregory here, creator of Polychord for iPad. Today my new app SageBrush: AI Painter was approved and released on the App Store. I wanted to make an AI tool that makes people a part of the process. With SageBrush you draw – either with an Apple Pencil or your fingers – and SageBrush turns your scribbles into AI imagery. I also wanted SageBrush to be simple. Something that would appeal equally to my kids, or to an architect who's looking for a new brush in their toolkit. SageBrush is built on Replicate, and I want to shout-out to them again and thank them for building a really nice API, and for their support during the development of this project. Can't wait to hear what you think.
