# Ask HN: What if your team chat organized tasks for you?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45176869
- 发布时间：2025-09-09
- 热度：点赞2、评论3

## 正文
Hi HN, We’re working on a new tool and I’d love to hear your thoughts. The idea is simple: - Imagine WhatsApp-style team chat. - Every conversation automatically turns into action items (tasks, docs, calendar events). - An AI “coworker” lives inside the chat: it organizes tasks, reminds you of deadlines, and can even take care of simple work on its own. - You can also record voice conversations, and the AI will generate a summary doc + extract action items into tasks. Looking ahead: - Those generated tasks themselves can become prompts, so the AI can proactively resolve what it can. - We plan to connect this with MCP to let AI agents handle tasks directly across tools and services. So instead of endless messages being buried, the chat itself becomes your task manager, doc hub, and meeting note system with AI doing the heavy lifting. We’re still early, but I’m curious: - Would you try a tool like this? - In your workflow, what would make an AI teammate inside chat actually useful (vs distracting)? Any feedback would be super helpful as we shape the product. Thanks!
