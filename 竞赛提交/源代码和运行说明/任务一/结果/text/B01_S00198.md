- 发布时间：2025-09-01

## 正文
Hi HN, I recently launched visadocs.ai, a small project that helps travelers generate professional visa application documents like itineraries and accommodation plans. It came out of my own frustrations preparing visa applications — piecing together flights, hotels, and day-to-day plans was time-consuming and often felt like busywork. The tool automates a lot of that: you enter your trip details, and it generates a ready-to-use PDF in minutes. I’m a solo founder and built this end-to-end (design, front-end, back-end, deployment). The technical stack is React + Vite for the front end, Cloudflare Pages/Workers for hosting, D1 for the database, and Stripe for payments. I've got more of a Product Designer background (been working in the industry for the past 10 years) so there was a bit of a learning curve and I might have not build this in the best way possible (especially on the back-end). I’d love your thoughts on a couple of things: For those who travel often, do you see value in this type of tool? Are there obvious use cases or pain points I might be overlooking? Any red flags you see with how this might scale (or not)? Thanks in advance — I’d really appreciate any feedback from this community
