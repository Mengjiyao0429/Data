## 正文
We built an AI email assistant that integrates natively with Gmail, providing contextual email writing and automation capabilities without requiring users to leave their inbox. What it does: - Runs as a lightweight Chrome extension - Uses LLMs fine-tuned on email context/threads - Privacy-first approach: no email content stored - Handles both personal emails and marketing campaigns - Works entirely within Gmail's interface The project started 6 years ago as a mail merge tool and evolved into a complete email suite. We're now adding AI capabilities while keeping it focused on email productivity. Core features: - Contextual email writing using thread analysis - Automated follow-ups with smart detection - Campaign management within Gmail - Detailed analytics and tracking Try it on Chrome Store : Product Hunt launch :
