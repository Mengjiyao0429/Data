# Ask HN: What still sucks about FP&A (financial business planning) software?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22778735
- 发布时间：2020-04-04
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Hey finance professionals at HN, We're building a financial planning tool for businesses in the 25-200 FTE range with a focus on real-time data (automated/swift input), an excellent overview of financial insights and powerful ways to model scenarios. Working at startups, we've seen how the finance dept. often lags behind, with data scattered across multiple spreadsheets, software, emails, and files. We want to collect this in one structured flow, built with native collaboration in mind. If you're working in finance, regardless of company size, it would be invaluable to hear about what works and what doesn't in your daily job. If anyone is excited about this stuff and want to have a closer discussion, feel free to email me at chris at trycashflow dot com. (Incidentally, we're also looking for a finance professional to join the team shortly. I hope me mentioning that does not violate any HN rules.) Thanks!
