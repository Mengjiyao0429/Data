# Do you know a good alternative to JIRA in terms of configurability?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=10909264
- 发布时间：2016-01-15
- 热度：点赞4、评论7

## 正文
I'm looking for a project management tool that would allow us to have custom workflows, custom fields, workflow triggers + web hooks and some REST api. We want to automate couple of processes we run at the company. JIRA is so complex and bloated and I wonder if there's anything competitive on the market.
