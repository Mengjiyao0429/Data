# Ask HN: How do you apply for jobs in the age of AI?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46613323
- 发布时间：2026-01-14
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
With AI being used by applicants to spam-apply to any open positions, and recruiters using AI as a filter, it feels like there's a lot of noise and few humans in the system right now. I thought about simply doing the same (building some n8n automation, or using one of the countless find-a-job-and-apply AI tools on GitHub), but I think there's a better way to do all of this - maybe even by getting around all of this AI shitshow?! Things I was thinking about: - don't optimize the CV/motivational letter with AI so that it sounds authentic - do unsolicited applications at companies i find interesting (since no job posting is open, no AI will suggest this company - and probably the recruiter will read the application) - do more networking (i hate that shit but IK its the most effective)
