# Ask HN: Is natural language a good interface for AI?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12692180
- 发布时间：2016-10-12
- 热度：点赞3、评论5

## 正文
All the mainstream AI platforms seem to bet on natural language as the interface with AI. - Amazon's Alexa - Microsoft's Cortana - Google's Assistant - Apple's Siri - Samsung's Viv I can't help but feel like these interfaces will continue to feel wrong short of perfect NLP and AI. Even if they were on par with human assistants (which is a big if), that still wouldn't be optimal. Clearly, natural language is not the ultimate form of communication. Natural language interfaces are slow, they do not challenge you, they do not allow auto-completion, they are linear, they are not very visual, they vary across cultures, etc. Is there any reason to want natural language as an interface to AI, other than because that's all people know?
