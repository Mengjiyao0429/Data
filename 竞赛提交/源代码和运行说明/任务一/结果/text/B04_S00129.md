## 正文
Hi everyone. I've been building Spidra.io, a no-code web scraping tool that can bypass popular CAPTCHA and login pages. It also lets you describe the pages you want to crawl with AI and set how the data from each page should be extracted. No other platform offers these features in one place, and I'm excited to see people test it and share their feedback.
