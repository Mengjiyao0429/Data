## 正文
Completely new to working with LLMs. Over the weekend I built a POC API that uses Langchain and calls GPT4 to perform something like a classification task, accepting a list of strings and returning a list of structured JSON objects. The problem is that is takes ~10s for each request even with small-sized input/output, which prevents the whole thing from being a usable MVP. (at least for the usecases I want). Sharing my high-level approach with the above at the end. I tried 3.5-turbo but could not get it to have any match of accuracy nor consistency. The closest to okayish I found was text-davinci-003 with a relatively large starting prompt including some examples (weirdly way better than GPT-35). 1) Is there a way to achieve reasonable latency ~3s with GPT4? (somehow setting the initial prompt once? removing langchain from the picture?) 2) Does langchain given my code-sample below send the whole conversation every time? Since I do not care about history for now, will removing it help? 3) Can and should I use GPT4 to generate training data and use it to fine-tune davinci-text-003? 4) Should I stick to GPT4 even with the deadly latency and somehow convince test users that speed will drastically change in X months? 5) Any other advice in terms of how to move forward?
