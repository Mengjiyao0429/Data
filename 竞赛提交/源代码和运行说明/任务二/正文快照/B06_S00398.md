# The Myth of AI

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=8609894
- 发布时间：2014-11-14

## 正文
Forgive my lack of knowledge on the topic but a question keeps popping into my head when I read comments about the danger to humanity where AI runs amok. > There are many paths towards dangerous AI futures, Are there not just as many paths towards protective, helpful AI futures? Is there a reason to believe that there will only be one AI in the future and that given a directive to do something, the elimination of humanity will be a logical endgame scenario for it? Why not many AI entities with different and competing goals? Granted, this opens up a different can of dangerous worms. But still, if there is a probability of an AI evolving to 'think' that elimination of the human race is a logical path then isn't it equally likely that there will be another AI evolving logical paths to preserve the human race?
