## 正文
I’m exploring a AI tool for product and UX teams that manages the full user-research workflow, not just AI note-taking/transcription. The idea is to help teams: ◉ Plan interview sessions ◉ Create agendas / questions ◉ Keep sessions on-track during the call ◉ Record + transcribe ◉ Extract themes, insights, and action items ◉ Aggregate research outputs into a unified knowledge base Think of it as a combination of tools like TL;DV/Fathom (capture) and Dovetail/Condens (synthesis/insights), but integrated across the whole workflow rather than siloed. I’m trying to validate whether this solves real pain, who needs it, and where the value is highest.
