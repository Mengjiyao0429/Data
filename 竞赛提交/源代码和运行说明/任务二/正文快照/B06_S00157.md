## 正文
I’m currently validating a problem I’ve seen often (and experienced myself): early-stage founders wasting time building in the wrong direction. Inspired by the SaaS Playbook, LeanStack, YC School, and other frameworks, I’m exploring an AI-powered tool that guides founders through idea validation and early-stage decision-making—using the best principles, made actionable. The idea is a self-guided product that combines structured startup advice with AI models like GPT-4 or Claude Opus to act as a real-time co-pilot for early builders. I’d love to hear your thoughts. Have you felt this pain? Would something like this be useful? And if you’re open to a quick feedback chat, let me know—especially if you’re a solo founder or early-stage builder (but anyone’s welcome). Thanks! ~Mihai
