## 正文
Zivor.io is designed to make customer engagement simple and effective for businesses of any size. With an intuitive interface and a powerful set of tools, Zivor.io helps you manage everything from customer support to team collaboration—all from one platform. Here’s how Zivor.io can help you: Meeting Scheduling: Easily book meetings with clients or team members. Sync schedules, set reminders, and eliminate the hassle of back-and-forth emails. Knowledge Base Platform: Provide quick answers to common questions with a self-service knowledge base. This reduces support requests and gives customers the information they need, anytime. Task Creation & Assignment: Manage your team’s tasks efficiently. Create tasks, assign them to team members, and track progress to ensure nothing slips through the cracks. Ticketing System for Customer Support: Handle customer queries seamlessly with a smart ticketing system. Prioritize, assign, and track tickets to ensure quick resolutions and happy customers. CRM for User Contacts: Keep all your customer information in one place. Use Zivor.io’s contact system to manage and nurture your relationships, like a built-in CRM. Visitor Analytics: Get valuable insights into who’s visiting your website. See where visitors come from, how they interact with your site, and use this data to improve customer experiences. Chat Automation with AI: Set up automated chat flows powered by ChatGPT to handle customer inquiries with ease. Zivor.io’s AI can answer common questions instantly, giving you more time to focus on important tasks. Live Chat: Provide real-time support directly from your website. Engage with customers when they need help, turning interactions into opportunities. Unified Messaging: Manage all your communication from one place. Reply to messages from WhatsApp, Telegram, and Viber using a single panel, saving you time and effort.
