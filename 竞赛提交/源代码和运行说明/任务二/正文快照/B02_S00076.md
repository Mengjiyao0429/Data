# Ask HN: How do you use AI at your regulated, restrictive company?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47912605
- 发布时间：2026-04-26
- 热度：点赞1、评论1

## 正文
I work in finance, and the company policy is that mainstream LLMs are allowed when using public information but anything private / sensitive has to be done $ copilot, which I find crap for anything other than searching company documents. The result of that is that my usage of AI is massively limited. I expect my only option is to effectively go to Mordor to get a Claude or a ChatGPT approved by risk and compliance - a daunting task - unless I’m missing something with copilot. For those in similarly restrictive environments, how do you use AI day-to-day?
