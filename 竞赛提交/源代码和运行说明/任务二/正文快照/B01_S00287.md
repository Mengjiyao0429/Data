# I Spent 3 Years Building a Startup No One Wanted

## 正文
TL;DR: I quit my job, built a startup, and watched it fail for 3 years. Just when I was about to give up, a small pivot changed everything. Now, it's a profitable SaaS making $500K ARR. The Painful Lessons I Learned: The Original Idea Flopped – I built a "smart" AI meeting scheduler, but nobody cared. The market was saturated, and our solution wasn’t 10x better. Burning Through Savings – I thought "if I build it, they will come." They didn't. After $50K+ down the drain, I realized I needed to sell before building. The Pivot That Saved Us – While talking to a few loyal users, I discovered they loved one tiny feature: an automatic follow-up email generator. That became our entire business. Now? 500+ Paying Customers – Instead of fighting competitors in a crowded space, we niched down and built the best AI-powered follow-up tool. That small shift made all the difference. My Takeaway: The startup world glorifies big ideas, but tiny pivots win battles. Listen to your users. Don’t be afraid to throw out your original plan. Would love to hear from other founders—what was your biggest pivot?
