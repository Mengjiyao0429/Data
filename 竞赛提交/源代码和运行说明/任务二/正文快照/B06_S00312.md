## 正文
The problem I need to detect hatch-filled rectangles on a huge image. There are other lines and forms connected to the hatch-filled rectangle. My desired output would be like the black form on the right side of the image. All other forms (not hatch-filled rectangles) are ignored and not in the output. What I tried: Build a mask by using a Hough transformation and calculating the angles of the detected lines. Ignore everything that is not about 45°. Use dilation to fill the detected lines into a rectangle. Form a mask and apply it to the gray image. Here I was able to detect the lines for the upper left rectangle, but not for the one in the bottom (lines too short?). Furthermore, this did not work if other lines are attached to the rectangle (as can be seen in the upper left). Any ideas on how to approach this problem the best way?
