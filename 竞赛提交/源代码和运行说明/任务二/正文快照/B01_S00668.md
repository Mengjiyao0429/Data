## 正文
Hello Everyone, I am the cofounder of a company called Resume Reviewers. Resume Reviewers is a software and resume reviewing company that focuses on maximizing the effectiveness of resumes for every type of application. We are currently in the process of creating our new software which we call Reed. Reed is a semi-artificially intelligent resume reviewing algorithm that finds key elements of the resume that are missing or should be removed. The goal is to provide a quick solution for people who need a resume reviewed quickly with accurate and insightful feedback. We are finally happy to announce that Reed is ready for beta testing and that we are looking for beta testers to test out the product.
