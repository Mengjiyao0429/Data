Hello HN, I built an MVP of a chatbot widget for websites over the last six months (worked by weekends). The core idea: it automatically collects insights from real visitors from conversation and behavior - the things GA4 and similar tools don’t show. Moreover, it can execute tasks based on a specific behavior (visited some page, asked something specific...). Tested on two live websites in Aug '25 (online education and consulting): ~300 conversations + ~1,000 insights. It surfaced issues like browser-specific UX bugs, incorrect information, frequent unanswered questions, missing content on first touch, etc. I don’t have time to maintain or promote it, so I'm selling. *Price: $15k.* What it does: - the widget initiates conversations on specific pages (checkout, onboarding, product pages) - each session is analyzed afterwards - insights are grouped by similarity - you see why drop-offs and problems happen, not just how often - parses products and shows to visitors if they ask to do so or its tasked with - no babysitting with business information - everything is collected / updated automatically Example use cases: - understand reasons for cart abandonment - reduce churn in SaaS help on key pages - upsell related products when visitors browse Average conversation cost for the business is ~ $0.01 (LLM usage optimized). Tech: Python, FastAPI, Qdrant, Redis, Postgres, Vue3, Stripe, OpenAI. Included in sale: - domain whilio.com - full codebase - deployment help - 20 hours of support (Q&A) Revenue is $0 - I didn’t launch paid plans due to lack of time on continuous support and development.
