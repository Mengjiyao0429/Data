# Ask HN: simple affiliate tracking software?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1163520
- 发布时间：2010-03-03
- 热度：点赞1、评论2

## 正文
Working on a site that is similar to groupon - what would you recommend as an affiliate tracking software if you were to work with a handful of publishers who are not your usual "affiliate people" but rather large publishers? Mainly we're looking for a system that is simple to administer both from the merchant's side and especially the affiliate's side. It should also be easy to integrate and support delayed tracking (someone might signup for a service but only buy a couple days later - it should still be accounted for)
