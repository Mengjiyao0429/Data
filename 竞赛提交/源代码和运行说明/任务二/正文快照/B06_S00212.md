# How to remain "relevant" in the job market

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41595981
- 发布时间：2024-09-19
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
I am "older", oh well, it happens to everyone. I work in operations for a life-science company in NYC, and unfortunately the company is on its way out due to mismanagement and lack of funds. Through the years I have carved myself a niche where I am a ops guy with strong systems/data science (more like data analytics) skills. And now I have become an experts in AI tools in my industry (not that many, yet). I have been looking for a job on and off for 9 months now, and I sent lots of resumes out, but very few interviews, and no offers. But both during my interviews and in the job posts, I don't see any mentions of AI skills, AI tools. This is 2024 after all, and I am in NYC!!! Am I missing something? Where/How do I find innovative companies that are looking for AI (and data) savvy workers? T.I.A.
