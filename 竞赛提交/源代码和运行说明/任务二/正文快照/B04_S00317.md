## 正文
A few months back, my friend Alessandro and I, Andrea, embarked on a side project named SplenderAI, delving into AI and automation consultancy for local businesses. After continuous outreach to many of them in our area, our initial excitement was met with rejection – a typical part of the entrepreneurial journey. Upon reflecting on our outreach approach, we started investigating online tools for outreach automation (since we found it an extremely laborious process) but found them lacking. Drag-and-drop solutions requiring intricate setups or even the having to define “outreach sequences” didn't align with our vision, so we decided to build an AI tool for internal use to help us get clients. Through conversations with fellow entrepreneurs and contacts from hackathons, we discovered a shared dissatisfaction with existing solutions. This realization prompted us to pivot SplenderAI into developing scalable solutions and to commercialize our internal tool as Sarah AI, a fully autonomous sales agent streamlining the lead-to-meeting process. Following up with the previous contacts, we identified beta testers who, after reaching out to thousands of prospects with our tool, provided valuable feedback, enabling us to enhance our product further. Fast forward, Sarah AI has evolved into a robust solution cherished by our initial users. So, what sets Sarah AI apart? - Fully Autonomous: Handles conversations without your intervention. - Adaptive: Tailors responses based on conversation stages and prospect intents. - Customizable: Functions as an extension of your business, adaptable to your niche. - Highly Integrable: Seamlessly integrates with major communication channels and CRMs. - Human-like: Unlike conventional chatbots, Sarah behaves like a human, handling multiple sequential messages. - HTL (Human-in-the-Loop): Notifies you promptly if third-party action is needed or if the prospect wants immediate interaction. … and so much more! Our journey led us to this refined product, Sarah AI, offering a novel approach to appointment setting, but there’s so much more we still want to do! If intrigued, you can explore further at splenderai.co. We hope you enjoy our startup story and find our product beneficial just like we do!
