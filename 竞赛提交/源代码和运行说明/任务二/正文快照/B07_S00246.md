# Space Travel Generator

- 发布时间：2023-04-05

## 正文
I'm looking for software with a 3D galaxy / universe that will automatically choose interesting features in space and fly to them for you. It will keep flying to different objects until you stop the program. Something where you have to write your own scripts to accomplish this would be fine as well. Think something like SpaceEngine but with a fully automatic exploration mode. SpaceEngine does have this as a planned feature, but I don't know when the developer will get around to it.
