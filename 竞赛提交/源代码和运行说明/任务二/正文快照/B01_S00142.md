My name is Michael White and I have been reading HN daily since 2010. I am an entrepreneurial software engineer who has founded two companies: - GetchaBooks.com (2010-2012), a course-based textbook price comparison site that scraped BNCollege and Follett bookstores on-demand with optional course metadata integration. Founded with [Michael Lazer-Walker]( ) and [Richard Mondello]( ). I originated the idea and wrote the backend. Shut down after five semesters. - MyGEDCOMs.com (2015-2022), a [GeneWeb]( )-based GEDCOM (genealogy database) hosting site with BYOD, Dropbox photo upload, and unlimited generation maps on any tree or report. I have also worked at [Dimagi]( ), a company I made up on Wikipedia and then registered an Inc for prior to attending college, and [Merck]( ). I minored in linguistics, attended an AGI conference, and have been following the development of AI relatively closely but haven't used it yet except for a few Google queries. I am looking for a non-technical co-founder for a declarative point-and-click application editor company. I have a whitepaper almost complete titled "A Self-Hosting Point-and-Click Editor for Any Functionally-Specified GUI-DB Application" in Python and JavaScript with a JavaScript-only frontend that builds on top of React, React Router v3, Backbone-Relational, Flask, SQLAlchemy, and Flask-Restless. Two of the most important additional components are almost complete. I am looking for a non-technical co-founder to handle administration, fundraising, and marketing for anywhere from 15-30% equity. If there is a person who is interested in being a technical co-founder, I may be able to accommodate them. Email me at appalchemist .com if you are interested.
