## 正文
Body: I am architecting a Forensic Data Audit system (Multi-Agent RAG) to analyze fragmented, large-scale archives. A critical bottleneck is maintaining Entity Resolution (ER) across millions of unstructured tokens. The Challenge: In investigative archives, entities often appear under aliases, abbre , or misspellings (e.g., "J. Doe", "John Doe", "Doe Corp"). Standard Semantic Search clusters them, but for a deterministic audit , I need a verifiable link to a unique Knowledge Base identifier. The Question: What is the recommended modern stack for integrating Named Entity Linking (NEL) into a RAG pipeline to ensure "Referential Integrity"? Architecture: Should NER (e.g., GLiNER or SpaCy ) be implemented as a pre-processing step during ingestion, or as a post-retrieval agentic refinement? State Management: How to maintain a global "Entity Graph" that agents can query in real-time without bloating the LLM’s context window? Tooling: Are there specialized libraries for Real-time Entity Linking that integrate natively with LangGraph or LlamaIndex ? Constraints: High precision is mandatory; false positives ruin the investigation. Scalability for 1M+ documents.
