## 正文
Do you have experience using an AI Assistant for code creation? What has your experience been? I've been rolling Copilot out to our developers at a large organization, we currently have 6,000 of our 13,000 developers using it. I'm looking for others to research. Our findings with Copilot: * Folks who try it, overwhelmingly like it. Some curmudgeons refuse to use it * It sucks for very old codebases that have diverged from public standards/topics * People who show potential as managers, have a much easier time onboarding.
