# Ask HN: Which CRM can help manually curated leads and automate lead discovery?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43120642
- 发布时间：2025-02-20
- 热度：点赞1、评论3

## 正文
I'm currently juggling a lot of CRM-related work using Notion and Gmail—taking meeting notes, sending follow-up emails, and tracking lead statuses manually. While this setup works, it’s becoming increasingly cumbersome. I’m looking for a tool that can both: - Import manually curated leads: I already have a database of leads that I've built up and want to easily dump into a new system. - Automate parts of my lead discovery process: It’d be great if the tool could help streamline or even automate the process of finding new leads. Has anyone successfully integrated such a tool into their workflow? What solutions have you found that meet these requirements? Any recommendations, insights, or experiences would be appreciated!
