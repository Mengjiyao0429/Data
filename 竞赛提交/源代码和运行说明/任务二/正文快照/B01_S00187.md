# AI Agents Are Quietly Taking over Project Management

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45618229
- 发布时间：2025-10-17
- 热度：点赞3

## 正文
Project management used to mean spreadsheets, timelines, and endless updates. Now, AI agents built on LLMs and predictive analytics are starting to handle the repetitive work — scheduling, reporting, data entry, even resource allocation. They don’t just automate; they predict. Feed them historical data, and they’ll forecast risks, budgets, and timelines faster (and sometimes more accurately) than humans. We might be close to the first “AI project manager” — one that can plan, assign, and track tasks across teams with minimal oversight. Would you trust an AI to manage your next project if it could outperform a human PM?
