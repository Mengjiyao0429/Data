## 正文
Hey HN, I built Glimpsy – an AI-powered automation tool that summarizes the most interesting articles from top tech and business publications. The goal is to make it easier for founders, investors, and tech enthusiasts to stay informed without drowning in long articles. Check it out on X (Twitter): Why I Built This I kept running into the same problem: too much content, not enough time. Most tech news is bloated with filler, and by the time you extract what matters, you’ve lost minutes (or hours). Glimpsy cuts through the noise. It delivers quick, actionable summaries of: • Startup & VC news (funding rounds, acquisitions) • AI & disruptive tech (OpenAI, Google, Tesla) • Stock market & macro trends • Cybersecurity, fintech, and more It’s 100% Free – Just Looking for Feedback I’m testing whether this is actually valuable for busy founders, engineers, and investors. Would love your thoughts: • Would you use this? • What’s missing? • How do you currently keep up with tech news? Appreciate any feedback. Let’s make news consumption smarter.
