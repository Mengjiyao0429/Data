On the website, it says: "Meta builds technologies that help people connect, find communities, and grow businesses." This sounds very generic to me. In an interview, Mark says he wants Meta to be the most important AI company. So, it is now an AI company? That is confusing. AI is the goal, not an instrument to reach another goal. What is Meta?
