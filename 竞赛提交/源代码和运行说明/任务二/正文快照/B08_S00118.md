## 正文
Hi everyone, After running a software agency for about ten years, I decided to build my own product — Arahi . It’s a no-code platform where anyone can create and deploy agents that handle real work across tools — things like research, content generation, SEO, lead management, and reporting. These agents can take action by connecting with over 2800 different apps through built-in integrations. I built this to make automation more accessible to small teams and solo founders who don’t have the time or technical setup to wire everything together manually. I’d love your feedback on: What kind of agents or workflows would be genuinely useful How the interface and builder experience could improve Whether this could fit into your existing workflow You can try it here: Happy to answer any technical or product questions in the comments.
