# Ask HN: Email provider to send bulk of similar emails at a human scale?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35297612
- 发布时间：2023-03-25
- 热度：点赞2、评论5

## 正文
I recently started a new business and wanted to move away from gmail. Following the community recommendations, I used Fastmail, a great alternative so far. After sending +50 emails yesterday to contact potential customers and editing them manually to adapt the content to their business, I learnt that Fastmail banned my account without warnings because I was sending "spam" and suggested using automation tools instead. I understand that email reputation is important for Fastmail but I am deceived that there was no warning and that I have to change email provider to send emails rapidly. Anyway, I am looking for an email solution that would allow me to write emails manually that are 95% similar, without being banned. I am not looking for fancy workflows, simply an email service that won't complain about doing things that don't scale. Any tool to recommend?
