## 正文
I'm looking for a tool or method to quickly bring-up a GUI based on incoming stream of structured data (JSON, MessagePack, etc). Specifically, I'm looking for the `visualize` part of this pipeline: `my | custom | data | feed | visualize` Is there a method or a tool that does so? Such tool shall limit freedom and granularity of widget placement and instead, substitute it with ease-of-use and script-ability. For plotting graphs, there is feedgnuplot [1].
