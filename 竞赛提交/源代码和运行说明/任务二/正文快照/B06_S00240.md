# Ask HN: Possible to build an AI version of Wordfeud?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39513252
- 发布时间：2024-02-26
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Hello HN, I used to play Scrabble with my mother (Dutch version) and I would lay words that in my imaginitive mind exist, but that would never be in a dictionary. Imagine a scenario where I want to extend the word "katten" (cats): --- kattenpoep (cat poo) would be approved kattenpis (cat pee) would be approved as well kattenspeeltje (cat toy) would probably be approved too. --- These words are common enough for Wordfeud and my mother to approve them. However, if I extend the word "katten" like this: --- kattengrappen (jokes about cats) kattentransport (cat transport) kattenpolitie (cat police) --- These words will not be accepted by Wordfeud or my mom. These words are rare and quirky but valid in my opinion. Is there an AI/API available that would test an input like "kattengrappen" to check if it "could be a valid word"? Not by using a word dictionary, but by using API. Are the AIs/APIs available for this purpose and could they be used to create a free-to-play game? I'd love to hear your ideas! RobbieJS
