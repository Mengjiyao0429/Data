Hi HN I’m Srihan. I’m 10 years old. And next week, I’m launching my first startup — live on stage — at SaaStock Austin. My product is called Agent Srihan. It’s an AI tool that monitors business phone numbers and sends alerts when they’re marked as Spam Likely or Scam Likely by carriers like AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile. This helps salespeople fix the issue before it hurts their connect rates. I started this journey by cold calling with my dad last year (he’s a founder too). We posted some of that on LinkedIn — and it went kind of viral. Since then, I’ve been learning in public: talking to people posting videos building a waitlist (146+ signups so far!) …and now I’m getting ready to pitch my startup on stage in front of hundreds of people. I’m nervous. I’m excited. And I’d love your help. If you’ve ever launched a product… Or pitched in front of a crowd… Or just want to support a kid trying to figure this out… What advice would you give me before I step on that stage?
