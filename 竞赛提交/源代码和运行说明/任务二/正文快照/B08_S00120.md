## 正文
Hey HN, I built a tool to help find intent signals in online discussions where people are already asking for recommendations or describing pain points. The idea came from the frustration of classic lead gen: scraping lists, sending cold outreach, trying to contact people who never asked to hear from you. But some of the best leads come from real conversations — forums, discussion threads, niche communities, etc. The problem is that finding those conversations manually takes hours every day. What Leado does: Monitors discussions in real time Identifies posts that signal buying intent (e.g., “looking for X tool,” “we need help with Y”) Summarizes context and reasoning Suggests a natural, useful reply that adds value first, not a sales pitch What it does not do: No scraping emails No mass automated outreach No spam No growth hacks Just surfaces conversations where showing up is actually helpful.
