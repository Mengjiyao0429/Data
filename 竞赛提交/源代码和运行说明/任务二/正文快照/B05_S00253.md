## 正文
Are there alternatives to Triplebyte? Looking for ability to adminster free online tests to evaluate from 100s or 1000s of candidates for skills such as AI/ML, DSP, Python, JavaScript.... I am just surprised that the YC community has not built a wrapper around chatgpt to 1. Get a list of 15 questions on a topic 2. Evaluate the responses to questions and put it in a database 3. Small dashboard to sort/filter candidates
