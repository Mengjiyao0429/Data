## 正文
We kept hitting a wall trying to scale — one tool for CRM, another for invoicing, another for tracking inventory, and a bunch of Google Sheets duct-taping it all together. So we built Barawave, an AI-powered ERP + CRM that pulls everything into one place: → Contacts, chats, invoices, orders, inventory, team workflows — all connected → Built-in AI to auto-write follow-ups, summarize convos, flag deals, and more → Real-time dashboards and automations out of the box We made it because nothing on the market felt like it was built for speed and focus. It’s still early, but we’re testing with agencies, SaaS founders, and eCom operators now. I'm curious: What’s been the biggest bottleneck for you: CRM, ops, or something else? Are you happy with your current stack, or Frankensteining it together like we were? Would an AI-native ERP/CRM actually make a difference in your workflow?
