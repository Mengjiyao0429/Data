# Ask HN: What simple finance tool for a small business?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12537613
- 发布时间：2016-09-20
- 热度：点赞3、评论2

## 正文
Can you recommend an online finance tool that would help me track the ins and outs of my (business) finances. I'm looking for something simple, I'm not incorporated in US so I do not need accounting or payroll or to charge people through that tool, but multi currency would be highly desirable (as I'm invoicing in both USD and EUR). Just to be aware of where I am financially. Apart from ins and outs, a nice feature would be to be able to write down my invoices and be able to track paid ones by a simple checkbox or similar and, therefore, have an overview of the unpaid ones. Everything I have found so far is either full of features I don't need or way too simple (and made for personal finance). Any idea, is something there that I have missed? Thanks!
