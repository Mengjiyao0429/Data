## 正文
UXReality ( ) is an outstanding solution that represents the newest tendency in the mobile UX world. It is an inclusive app for creating your own universe of user insights while interacting with mobile sites and apps. It helps to create an awesome digital experience by seeing through users’ eyes, understanding their feelings, knowing what they say AI-powered app for getting mobile behavior insights. UXReality is the first solution in the world that literally turns any smartphone into a fully-featured tool for studying user behavior as close to reality as possible. One app replaces bulky and expensive usability lab and helps to understand how people really interact with a mobile website or app by knowing what they see, think, and feel. It has all needed tracking features such as heatmap, gaze plot, emotions, voice, scroll and click recordings with AI-powered webcam Eye Tracking, Facial Coding, and feedback surveys.
