# Fable Took My Job

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50054412
- 发布时间：2026-10-12

## 正文
>The building of the software happens tri now. You do not need a software engineer, sorry guys. This part is quite "load-bearing". I heard from someone who worked at Lovable that what most customers produce with that magical textbox is software that shouldn't go anywhere near production. Apparently the problem of thinking like an engineer is not solved by these AI tools. Most people who are not software engineers by trade are not capable of verifying what they produced, nor would they even consider needing to. No offence, I think you might be a classic case of "senior who forgot how it feels to be junior", where after a lot work experience deva start taking their software engineering instincts for granted and forget that they are a skill that takes years to master. You can prompt an AI for a solution, you can even prompt it for ideas, but you cannot prompt it to think for you.
