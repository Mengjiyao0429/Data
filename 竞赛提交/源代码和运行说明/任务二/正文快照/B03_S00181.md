## 正文
I’ve launched MoneyIQ, a platform for retail investors. One of the tools is a Company Report Generator that produces a plain-English, investment-grade report on any public company, including 16 quarters of data, key ratios, trend charts, and AI-generated highlights. You just enter a company name or ticker and let the report run. You get 3 reports free without sign-up. I’ll happily offer more credits to anyone testing and sharing feedback. Would love thoughts from HN on usability, usefulness, or areas to improve. Thanks! Steve
