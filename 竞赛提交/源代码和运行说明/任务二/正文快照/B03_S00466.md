## 正文
I recently had to be issued a new credit card because of some fraudulent charges. Now, I am going through the process of trying to remember all the places that I have my old card number saved and update with my new info. I am particularly scattered with my financials as with "organization" in general. I'm the guy with 3 monitors that have 20 windows open and a browser with 10+ tabs at all time. I've always thought of that as my "in memory" bookmark cache. That being said, I am interested in consolidating my bill paying activities and wanted to tap the collective wisdom of HN. I have a Mint account (I created out of curiosity) which I believe is a reasonable choice to use for reoccurring payments. I also could use my bank's software which I have to say has gotten pretty good with respect to other functionality. I would love to hear your experience and recommendations for these or other services which allow you to pay all (or as many as possible) bills from a single account. Thanks!
