# I Got the Idea for an AI Email Generator While Bench Pressing 220 Pounds

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43898705
- 发布时间：2025-05-05
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Hello everyone, I just wanted to share a brief story about how my current project, Mailteorite, came to life. One day, I was at the gym, doing heavy bench presses (100 kg / about 220 lb). During my rest periods between sets, I was scrolling through my phone. I had just received promotional emails about protein supplements, and while checking one of them, I realized I was much more focused on the email design than on the offers themselves. I started thinking: "How can people create such clear and professional-looking emails?" As a developer, I knew there was a lot of coding behind this, but then the idea hit me: What if you could simply describe the email you want and a tool generates a beautiful template ready to be sent for you? At first, it was just a passing thought during a gym session. But once I got home, I started building a prototype... and that's how Mailteorite was born. Today, Mailteorite is a simple, AI-powered tool where you enter a prompt and it generates clean, optimized email templates that you can use immediately. I'd love to hear your feedback or see a quick review! It would mean a lot and help me improve it even further.
