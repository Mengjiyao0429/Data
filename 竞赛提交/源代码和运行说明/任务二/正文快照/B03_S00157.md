Wow so much hate in this thread For me I’m looking for an AI tool that can give me morning news curated to my exact interests, but with all garbage filtered out. It seems like this is the right direction for such a tool. Everyone saying “they’re out of ideas” clearly doesn’t understand that they have many pans on the fire simultaneously with different teams shipping different things. This feature is a consumer UX layer thing. It in no way slows down the underlying innovation layer. These teams probably don’t even interface much. ChatGPT app is merely one of the clients of the underlying intelligence effort. You also have API customers and enterprise customers who also have their own downstream needs which are unique and unrelated to R&D.
