# Ask HN: Could you create a competitor to your company at 10% of the cost?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47160050
- 发布时间：2026-02-26
- 热度：点赞9、评论14

## 正文
I'm trying to wrap my mind about the AI tools, and while I believe there is way too much hype, I'm quite impressed with the progress. The current mood seem to be that big companies will automate away many white collar jobs and just get bigger profits. My question is - what if it's the other way around ? Could said white collar workers just spin off competitors much more easily than before ? Obviously this mostly apply to software, but I'm curious what people think about it in all industries.
