# Ask HN: How would you use an AI-powered browser agent?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45460226
- 发布时间：2025-10-03
- 热度：点赞3、评论4

## 正文
I’m exploring the idea of AI powered browser agents tools that can navigate the web like a human: clicking, typing, scraping, filling forms, or even completing multi step workflows. All you do is prompt the task. If you had such an agent, what would you use it for? - Automating repetitive tasks? - Gathering and cleaning data? - Handling customer or internal workflows? - Something else entirely? I’d love to hear how the HN community imagines using this.
