# The most useful LLM-based product I've used this year isn't for coding

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44446138
- 发布时间：2025-07-02
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Most of my LLM use has been for text generation, debugging, or idea drafting. But weirdly enough, the most impactful use case in my life has been language practice. I’ve been using an app called Praktika that combines GPT with voice avatars to simulate real conversations: everything from small talk to interviews. What stood out is the focus on active use. It doesn’t just quiz you; it forces you to speak, get feedback, and keep going. It’s helped me get past the mental block of speaking in my target language. Anyone else exploring voice-based use cases of LLMs? Curious what’s out there that doesn’t just feel like a ChatGPT wrapper.
