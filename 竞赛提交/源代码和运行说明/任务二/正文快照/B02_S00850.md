# Ask YC: Entrepreneurial/Startup Reading List?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=253223
- 发布时间：2008-07-22
- 热度：点赞6、评论9

## 正文
My friend and I are looking to start our own small web business in the coming months, and I am looking to expand my entrepreneurial and business knowledge base. I am looking for books that deal with starting a business as well as managing software teams. So far on my list, I have Peopleware by Tom DeMarco, Groundswell by Charlene Li and Josh Bernoff, and Raving Fans: A Revolutionary Approach to Customer Service by Ken Blanchard and Sheldon Bowles. Does the YC community have any other suggestions to add to my list? Thank you!
