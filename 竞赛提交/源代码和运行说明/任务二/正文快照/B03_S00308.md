# we are building a startup exploration platform

## 正文
we are solving the problem of limited visibility of startups. let me paint a picture for you — while scrolling through your social media you accidentally came across an ad or a post of a startup to which you got very excited. then you followed it, and maybe eventually you liked the product and purchased it. but have you ever thought that was accidental and several other startups are building such innovative products and you might not be able to know about them. now for someone like you who is a startup enthusiast (or is starting a startup), has it ever happened to you that you are interested in a startup and you strongly believe that as soon as it is launched it will blow up the market. but when you launch it on social media no such thing happens! if you could relate to the above, you would have realized that this is because social media is noisy and startups compete not only with startups, but also fully grown and mature brands, and most importantly, content creators. well, to solve this we are building a dedicated platform for startup exploration, it would be a place where only startups can create content and people like you and me, i.e. startup enthusiasts can follow along their journey. and since from user-content interaction we will generate a lot of data we process this data and provide analytics to startups for targeted growth (it acts as a tool for startups). we believe if used strategically startups can easily convert our users to their customers! the platform tackles the challenge of limited visibility for emerging startups. in the current landscape, users discover startups through tv shows or stumble upon them on social media, leaving countless innovative ventures struggling for attention. our dedicated platform revolutionizes startup exploration. we empower emerging startups to showcase their journey through engaging content and offer exclusive analytics tools for their targeted growth. users actively participate in the startup journey and discover new innovative startups. it is a one-of-a-kind fusion of social media, product hunt, and analytics tools. we aim to reshape the startup discovery landscape, ensuring every innovative idea is spotlighted. how it works: - startups register and share their journey through posts, polls, and promos, fostering storytelling and trust-building. - users engage with content, providing feedback, and, if they love the product, potentially making a purchase—resulting in the discovery of new startups. - user interactions generate valuable data, processed and provided to startups for targeted growth strategies, such as demographics of engaging users. please let me know your thoughts and feedback!
