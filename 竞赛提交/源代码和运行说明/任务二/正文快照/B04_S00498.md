## 正文
From a background in Java, Pascal, PERL, SQL, and R I want to find a least resistance path to (semi-) automated extraction of concepts from text and turning the result into something statistically analyzable. I imagine I will need to perform some NLP tasks on text, particularly POS processing, identification of noun phrases, word sense disambiguation. The latter I believe, may require semi-supervised machine learning for accuracy. Where do I start learning practical NLP? A course or NLP books get into far more detail than I need. I just need to know what it does, how accurate it is, and what alternatives there are. I've used GATE for POS processing, but the output was either in XML which I have no idea how to process, or PostgreSQL which was a bear to manipulate. Also, at the time GATE had no good method for extracting word sense.
