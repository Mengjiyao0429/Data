Hello, Everyone; I'm Anwaar Malik. I have a background in CS+Finance from the University of Waterloo with additional education in Economics from Harvard. Today, I officially launched AllMind AI to the public. We have been in beta for the past two months and have had analysts, independent investors, and professionals from the finance world review our product to see how we can improve it. I developed AllMind AI to address common challenges faced by investors. In the finance sector, investors typically encounter three significant hurdles: 1) High cost of data; 2) Data is scattered, requiring visits to multiple locations to obtain information, each with its learning curve and fees; and finally and 3) The complexity of transforming raw financial data into actionable insights for investment or trading decisions. This led me to create AllMind AI, designed to significantly reduce the time and money investors spend on research and analysis. During our beta testing, professional analysts from major firms and independent users reported an average savings of 90% in research time and 98% in subscription costs. Our goal at AllMind is to save time and increase profits for investors. We want you to make confident data-driven decisions for your investments.
