## 正文
I'm seeking a co-founder in the Greater Boston area to help me startup a company at Social.com (not related to the current web site). My plan is to offer consumer microblogging services over utilities such as Twitter, with a possible social networking component. I'm looking for a proven professional with Web 2.0 startup experience who can help me explore the market, develop a business plan, finish a prototype, and find funding. You would be working with someone who is an experienced programmer and techie. I've been working on some general infrastructure code toward an example Twitter App. I take a solid approach to programming projects which includes exception handling, testing, code coverage, documentation, etc. I'm open to the possibility that two co-founders might fill this need - one to assist with UI and backend tasks (currently Perl/Catalyst) and the other to focus on business needs. If you are interested and live in the Greater Boston area (this is a must), please send e-mail with some information about yourself to: cofounder (at) social.com Thanks!
