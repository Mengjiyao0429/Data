## 正文
A bit of a duplicate of my question on AI.SE (I've been told this is the place to ask): YouTube was recently suggesting to me videos of people training NEAT neural networks for video games. I've noticed that often the training process was quite slow (for example in this Trackmania example ). Is there a way/a tool to easily simulate video games, without actually rendering the pixels on screen and making the training much quicker? (it sounds reasonable for such tool to exist since OpenAI was able to train their "Dota 2" model this way)
