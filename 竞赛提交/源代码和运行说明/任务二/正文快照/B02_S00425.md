## 正文
Hey everyone, Just wanted to share that in response to consultants' requests for a tool to automate their report generation, my co-founder and I quickly built and launched in a 15-day sprint. Our aim was to create a platform that could easily generate detailed reports for client engagements, inspired by feedback for something akin to opinly.ai. How We Did It: Tech Stack: We used existing solutions and code to speed up development. This includes Clerk for user auth, PlanetScale for our database, Next.js and React for the frontend, styled with Tailwind CSS, and SST for backend development. Quick Turnaround: Leveraging these tools, we managed to design, develop, and deploy our MVP on Product Hunt within just 15 days. Features: AI-powered reports generated from files, URLs, or direct input. Customizable to fit various reporting needs. User-friendly interface designed for efficiency and ease of use. We're thrilled to see v0.report live and can't wait to hear what you think. If you find it useful or are interested in what we're doing, please support us with an upvote on Product Hunt here: v0.report on Product Hunt. Thanks for checking it out!
