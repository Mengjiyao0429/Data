# Ask HN: Why does your company PII-wall software and documentation?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16042202
- 发布时间：2017-12-31
- 热度：点赞2、评论1

## 正文
I frequently encounter websites that demand that I fill in some personal information (many don't authenticate it at all) before permitting me to download software or documentation. Frequently it's a hardware vendor (e.g., ST Micro). I find it an annoying waste of time and always fill out those forms with garbage. Why does you company do this and what value do you derive from this anti-user behavior? I'm hoping to hear from folks working at companies that actually do this, rather than speculation from folks who don't.
