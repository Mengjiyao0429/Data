# Ask HN: Would this tool work? Payform

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46473592
- 发布时间：2026-01-03
- 热度：点赞1

## 正文
Problem: Business processes are overcomplicated and painful to manage: intake forms, proposals, invoices, payments, follow-ups, etc. Free form submissions attracts spam. Solution: The goal isn’t to replace these tools. It is to streamline the whole flow into one clear path. Payform is a conversational commerce tool that helps businesses collect customer information and take payment at the end of the flow. A conversation leads to a paid conversion. A free form attracts spam at scale. A payment at the end reduces spam almost entirely. Flow: • Intake questions • Options / add-ons • Price • Checkout • Receipt • Notify the business owner It’s only a concept for now. I am looking for feedback: 1. What would make you switch from “form + invoice” tools? 2. What are the must-have integrations or features? 3. Is the messaging clear and easy to understand? I find it makes sense for services, events, deposits, bookings, etc.
