# Ask HN: What timesheet / staff schedule tools do you use?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12684743
- 发布时间：2016-10-11
- 热度：点赞2、评论3

## 正文
We are looking for an easy-to use, extensive, staff schedule and timesheet solution. We are a software consulting company with about 80 devs, and every single tool I find seems either over-complicated and restrictive, or too simple to handle projects. Speaking with different size company, I couldn't find anybody happy with their actual solution. It can be a SaaS solution or an open-source tool. API for import / export are a need (so we can feed invoicing for example). So, what do you use and what are the pro / cons ?
