## 正文
Hey guys, I've decided to use erlang for my next project. I'm in need of a solution that has massive parallelism (thousands of threads) and works over a geographically distributed system. From what I've read, erlang is the way to go. The syntax is kind of wonky, but it only took me about a day to get used to. Erlang is my first functional language though, so I'm still trying to get into the swing of functional thinking.
