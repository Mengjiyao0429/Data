# Ask HN: What Needs to Be Automated?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34573551
- 发布时间：2023-01-29
- 热度：点赞9、评论18

## 正文
Chat GPT and Stable Diffusion are really cool, but outside of Alpha Fold, AI hasn't, to me, yet brought a ton of value to humanity. I have been thinking about what would really be a game changer to people's lives to have automated processes. Would it be food production, medical care, or education? Insulin production, maybe? Putting aside if the tools are ready or not, what would be a high-leverage area that, if there were automation at some level, would have a huge positive impact on people's lives?
