# Ask HN: Are you seeing AI used in places where it shouldn't be?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39208887
- 发布时间：2024-01-31
- 热度：点赞21、评论44

## 正文
It's no surprise, really, but I'm starting to see companies sticking AI into places where it just doesn't make any sense. I'm not talking about the use of AI all over marketing materials. That's a given. I'm talking about things like my CEO wanting me to build an AI sales assistant when a simple web form would actually be faster to develop and use. What are you seeing?
