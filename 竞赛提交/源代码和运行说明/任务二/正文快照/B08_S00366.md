## 正文
My son is 7 and I think it's time to teach him how to write some software. What language do I pick though? Go? Precise, fast, C-like. Ruby? Easy to read, fun, will hamper his future learning of other languages? Javascript? Ubiquitous, yet terrible? Elixir? Fun for the both of us, but maybe too hard for him?
