## 正文
OpenClaw is an open-source autonomous artificial intelligence (AI) personal assistant software that runs locally on the user's laptop or desktop computer. I am looking for a personal assistant software that runs locally on the user's Android smartphone, using a local LLM model, without the need for another computer. Requirements: Open source (but may use non-open small libraries whose role is simply to call locally-stored models) Gratis. Runs 100% on the Android device Even when it has no Internet, it can do reasonably helpful things such as finding a local downloaded document, summarizing things, setting an alarm.
