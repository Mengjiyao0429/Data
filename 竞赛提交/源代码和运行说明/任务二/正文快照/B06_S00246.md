# Ask HN: Good AI-assisted behavioral interview prep tools?

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=38922105
- 发布时间：2024-01-09
- 热度：点赞2

## 正文
Know any good tools for behavioral interview prep that help sort experiences and create custom answers? I'm prepping for interviews, and going through past experiences, while organizing them neatly one by one is such a pain. An AI tool for this would be awesome. Also, using my experiences to answer BQs the right way is quite challenging. There are so many questions out there, and how I presented my experiences seem to make a big difference. If I'm unprepared, I tend to ramble and get rejected. This market is tough. There are guides online for BQ preps, but I need something to: 1. Dump and neatly organize my experiences; 2. Provide good answers to BQs using my most relevant experiences; 3. Maybe even help create perf packages or docs; Anything like this out there? I wouldn't think this is too hard to build, and might even build one if there's demand.
