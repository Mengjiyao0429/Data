## 正文
Hi, everyone! I'm George; a 15-year-old High-School student from California, and I've recently been working on a tool to help company's track/boost their culture. I am in the processing of developing a Slack app, which tracks your team workspace, and using ML/AI builds culture reports based off 6 key aspects of company culture (e.g. each week you get a report pertaining to that week, and what you low/strong points are, and how you can improve them). No more wasting your team's time with tedious assessments! I'm looking for companies that use Slack on a daily basis, and have ~10-20+ team members. If you decide to become an early adopter, you'll receive 3 months access free-of-charge, plus some company goodies from us :) Interested or want to chat more?
