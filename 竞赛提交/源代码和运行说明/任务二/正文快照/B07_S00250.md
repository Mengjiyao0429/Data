# My love-hate relationship with AI

- 来源：Hacker News
- 链接：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34726233
- 发布时间：2023-02-09
- 热度：点赞3、评论2

## 正文
Since the day I started using ChatGPT, I have developed a love-hate relationship for AI. Disclaimer: English is my second language and I am not an expert in AI, just an opinion. Why I love AI - I have no doubt that AI is the future and the future is here. AI has the potential to generate massive wealth for millions of people regardless of their location. Why I hate AI - I am afraid of what will come out of AI if applied wrongly. For example, I love ChatGPT and I use it everyday from drafting letters, learning and more but at the same time I feel the more I use ChatGPT the dull I become (I hope we don't become robots one day). I can't think about solving something without opening a link to ask ChatGPT. In some way I feel like the same way Social media has had both good and bad, AI may bring both sides too... though the bad side may be more dangerous mentally compared to Social media (just an opinion, every good thing has the bad sad). Finally, if you have time to build AI solutions or learn please don't miss this big opportunity. Just be a good person to yourself and others. I am equally trying to see how my small business can use AI to improve our processes.
