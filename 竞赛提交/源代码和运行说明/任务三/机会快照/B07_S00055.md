Considering how much we talk to AI agents now, I think a separate notes app is no longer relevant. Ideally, the agent should remember everything. Though there are still a lot of problems here. I ran into this when I was renewing my Japanese business visa, and that thing is very bureaucratic. So I spun up a RAG in the cloud and connected it to my Claude over MCP. It made me happy, I got really hooked on it, then got my colleagues hooked, and in the end turned it into a full service.
