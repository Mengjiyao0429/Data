## 正文
I have an infinite (windowed) list that currently works with a cursor based pagination system. The problem with my current approach is that users have to scroll to the bottom of the list, wait for results to load, scroll to the bottom again, and so on. I would like to allow users to scroll to the middle of the list without having to download all the data in between. I don't understand how can this be done following the Relay pagination recommendations.
