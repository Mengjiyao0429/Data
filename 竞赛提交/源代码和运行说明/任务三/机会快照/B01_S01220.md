## 正文
A robust Legal Document & Case Law Analyzer using OCR, Elasticsearch, and Gemma 3 (Ollama). Upload legal documents, extract text, and perform intelligent queries on contracts and case laws. Supports case law ingestion and retrieval with an intuitive UI. Built with FastAPI, LangChain, and a two-panel frontend for seamless analysis.
