# Why Whisper and medical speech APIs are making potentially fatal errors

## 正文
Appen (a global AI training data and annotation provider) just open-sourced MedTerm-90, a new benchmark designed to evaluate Speech-to-Text (STT) systems in medical environments. Instead of relying on traditional Word Error Rate (WER)—which treats a missing comma the same as a mutated drug dosage—this benchmark introduces Clinical Entity Recall to prioritize patient safety.They tested 7 major speech APIs (including OpenAI Whisper and ElevenLabs Scribe v2) across 90.5 hours of real-world physician audio spanning 14 medical specialties. The results show a massive gap between standard accuracy scores and actual clinical safety.
