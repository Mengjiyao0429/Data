## 正文
This repository contains a FastAPI-based app for KYC (Know Your Customer) and KYB (Know Your Business) processes, utilizing OCR for ID card evaluation and face comparison for identity verification. It also analyzes business documents to determine types and validate contents using Google's Vertex AI. Includes detailed results and analysis.
